## 8. quiet-luxury — warm restrained minimalism · register: neutral

The 2020s “quiet luxury” current: warmth instead of neutrality, quality signalled by typography and spacing rather than by structure. Distinct from `swiss-grid` (which is mathematical) and from `humanist-quiet` (which removes devices from a structured system) — this one has exactly one small device and spends its colour on the employers.

- **Heading device**: the title (12.5pt **bold** `dark` — never lighter than the bold role line under it; the quiet comes from space, not ink), then a **short 34pt × 2pt accent tick** on its optical centre, in the accent — inline punctuation, not furniture. Measured and rejected on the way: a hairline running from the title out to the right margin, which reads as word-processor furniture beside every heading.
- **Skills block**: the label is set in the **metadata gray** (`ink`), the items in `dark` — *colour*, not weight or a glyph, does the separating. No colon, no dash, no bullet.
- **Accent role**: the organization names and the heading ticks. Nobody else puts the accent on the employers.
- **Icons**: **line** style in the **metadata gray**, never the accent — the accent has its two jobs already; the platform marks follow, in the same gray.
- **Header**: **contacts first** (a small gray letterhead line), then the name, then the role — the reverse of every other family's order. Photo slot = right cell of the header row.
- **Type**: one warm humanist sans, generous but unshowy, display and body. Pool: `pick_design.py` FONTS `quiet-luxury`.
- **Accents** (mono harmony): `#5c4632` bark · `#33475b` slate · `#0e6862` teal.
- **Verified**: leading `0.508em`, spacing `0.608em`, list spacing `0.834em`, section `above` 26pt (31pt airy) × rhythm 0.938, 9pt after the title → 1 page, fill 96% (the 6pt of extra space above each heading is what makes the boundary visible: measured gap above ≈1.34 line pitch, heading 1.19× body, boundary-salience score .31 → .46 of 14 families; spacing and list spacing are the tuner's rescale of `0.608em`/`0.834em`).
- Markets: any; safe for conservative; reads slightly senior/executive. Photo: yes.
