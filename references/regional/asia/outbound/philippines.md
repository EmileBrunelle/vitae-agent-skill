# Outbound: Philippine credentials, other markets (K-12 and pre-reform degrees)

Loaded on demand when a candidate with Philippine credentials targets a market outside the Philippines. Read together with [`common.md`](common.md). Local CV habits: [`southeast-asia/philippines.md`](../southeast-asia/philippines.md).

- « As of 2012, primary and secondary education have been reformed through the 'K to 12 Basic Education Program' ». Before it, « secondary education lasted 4 years, so 2 years shorter than now »; Senior High School (grades 11-12) was introduced in 2016 and its diploma first awarded in 2018 (Nuffic, « Primary and secondary education », Philippines, undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S188). Nuffic's bachelor's comparison splits at programme start before/after 2018 (Nuffic, « Higher education », Philippines, undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S189): give enrolment and graduation years on the degree line; claim no equivalence.

## Netherlands target only
Nuffic mappings, read with the Netherlands section of [`india/outbound/europe-common.md`](../india/outbound/europe-common.md): started before 2018 « an associate degree or 2 years of HBO »; 2018 or later « an HBO bachelor's degree or 2 years of WO » (Nuffic, « Higher education », Philippines, undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S189).
