# Outbound: Asian credentials, other markets (excluding India), common rules

Loaded on demand when the candidate holds credentials from Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, the Philippines or China and targets a market outside their home country. Read together with the target market's file, which still applies. This add-on covers only what is specific to these credentials.

## Scope
India: [`india/outbound/anglosphere.md`](../india/outbound/anglosphere.md).

## Rules that hold for every country below
- Write the degree as printed; state a length only when the transcript shows it; never write "equivalent to a US/4-year degree". With an evaluation report, add its conclusion in its own words naming the evaluator; without one, claim nothing ([`india/outbound/anglosphere.md`](../india/outbound/anglosphere.md) rule). Grades as printed, never converted: [`references/education-cases.md`](../../../education-cases.md).
- Nuffic distinguishes Pass from Honours and 2-, 3- and 4-year lengths, and adds that the result depends on the programme's content: keep both on the degree line. Nuffic's mappings are for Dutch levels only (mappings at the end of each origin file); never restate them as a US/Canadian/UK outcome.
