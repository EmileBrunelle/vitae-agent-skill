# Outbound: Chinese credentials, other markets (CHSI and CSSD verification)

Loaded on demand when a candidate with Chinese credentials targets a market outside China. Read together with [`common.md`](common.md).

- China Daily republishing CHSI, 2017: CHSI online verification reports are for « employers, individuals and other related institutions » and cover « student records, qualification certificates, and admission information »; a third party checks one by entering the verification code on the CHSI page, and holders can extend validity (China Daily gov. channel, « Brief introduction to online verification report », 2017-07-19, consulted 2026-11-07, S190). Fees and procedure may have changed: verify before telling the candidate.
- In general, WES refers applicants to CSSD (Center for Student Services and Development, formerly CHESICC): a report in Chinese needs its translation sent « in CHSI Archive » via the International Cooperative Application portal, and for Sino-foreign programmes WES says « contact CSSD (formerly CHESICC) to verify the documents » (WES US Help Center, « The CDGDC degree verification report ... English translation », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S191; WES US Help Center, « Sino-foreign program (中外合作项目) documents », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S192). The names CDGDC, CSSD, CHESICC and CHSI appear in these sources as the bodies involved; no loaded source explains how they relate beyond "CSSD (formerly CHESICC)".
- Mention a verification on the CV only if the candidate holds a valid report (default validity one year, extendable; CSSD (Ministry of Education, PRC), CHSI, « Qualification Verification and Student Information Verification », undated, consulted 2026-11-08, S567); CHSI says the report's barcode « can be pasted into resumes », so a barcode is acceptable, and individuals and social departments can authenticate a report for free with its online verification code (CSSD (Ministry of Education, PRC), CHSI, « Brief Introduction to Online Verification Report », undated, consulted 2026-11-08, S568; CSSD (Ministry of Education, PRC), CHSI, « How to Verify the Online Verification Report », undated, consulted 2026-11-08, S569). No official page addresses printing the text verification code: omit it (LOW, unsourced: convention, prudence).
