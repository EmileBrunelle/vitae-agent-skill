# Pakistani, Bangladeshi and Nepali credentials, other markets

## Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal
- The length on the transcript decides, not the title. Nuffic lists Pakistani Pass degrees (BA/BSc, 2 years; BBA/BCom/Technology), 3-year Honours and 4-year Honours separately; Bangladesh pass (3 years) and honours; Nepal 3- and 4-year bachelor's (Nuffic, « Higher education », Pakistan, Bangladesh and Nepal pages (undated), consulted 2026-11; S185, S186, S187). No two-year Bangladeshi or Nepali bachelor's appeared in the pages loaded.
- Pakistan, 2-year BA/BSc (HEC's own statements): HEC phased out the 2-year BA/BSc (deadline: academic year 2019) and MA/MSc (30 June 2022); « degrees obtained from such programmes will not be recognized and attested by HEC » (HEC news, « HEC Cautions Students against Admission in 2-year BA, BSc and MA, MSc Degree Programmes » (2024-06-21); S215). Its replacement, the 2-year Associate Degree (60-66 credit hours) from HEC-chartered institutions, is « equivalent to generally old BA/BSc (14 years schooling) », decided case by case; an Associate Degree in Education is « not equivalent to BEd » (HEC FAQ « Frequent Asked Questions for obtaining Equivalence of Pakistani Degrees » (undated), accessed 2026-11-07; S216). HEC statements, not a foreign evaluator's: write the degree as printed with enrolment and graduation years; for a 2-year BA/BSc begun after 2019, warn that HEC will not attest it (LOW: action inferred from S215).
- NOT confirmed: WES/ECE treatment of the Associate Degree; UGC Bangladesh and Nepal (not loaded). Ask for degree, year and evaluation report; state no recognition status.
