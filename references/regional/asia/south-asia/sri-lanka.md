# Market: Sri Lanka

Loaded on demand when the CV goes to an employer in Sri Lanka. Thinly sourced: no official CV convention found; the data protection regime is phased and its dates changed in 2025-2026, so state the date of the source.

## At a glance
- Paper: not covered here (not checked).
- Photo: not covered here (not checked).
- CV language: English is the working assumption (LOW, unsourced). Sinhala `si` (Noto Sans/Serif Sinhala installed) or Tamil `ta` (Noto Sans/Serif Tamil, Droid Sans Tamil installed) only if the posting asks.
- Personal data: the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022, amended by Act No. 22 of 2025, is enacted but mostly not yet in operation. The Authority (Part V) operates since 2023-07-17; the original 2025-03-18 start for the controller obligations was replaced by phased dates (Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka, « Personal Data Protection Circular No. 01/2026, Application of PDPA in the Public Sector », 2026-08-07, S873). Press reports Extraordinary Gazette No. 2498/16 of 2026-07-22 setting 2027-01-01 for sections 2-3, Part I (processing) and Part III (controllers and processors); data subject rights (Part II) and penalties (Part VII) were not dated (Financial Times (Sri Lanka), « Data protection compliance regime takes effect on 1 Jan. 2027 », 2026-08-10, S874; the gazette itself did not load). Protects citizens and non-citizens alike (S873). It sets no CV content rule.
- Length: not covered here (not checked).
- Typst flags: `--lang en --region lk`; Sinhala `--lang si --region lk`; Tamil `--lang ta --region lk`.
- Fonts (`typst fonts` checked 2026-11-26): Sinhala and Tamil both present; nothing missing.

## Regulated professions
- Doctors: the Sri Lanka Medical Council keeps the registers « to ensure that only authorised professionals are permitted to practise », under the Medical Ordinance (Sri Lanka Medical Council, « About SLMC », consulted 2026-11-26, S878). Graduates of foreign universities need degree approval, the ERPM/ERPDS examination, provisional registration, then an internship with the Ministry of Health (Sri Lanka Medical Council, « Overseas Graduates », consulted 2026-11-26, S879). A foreign-trained candidate without those steps is not registered: do not write a practising title.
- Not covered here (not checked): UGC Sri Lanka recognition of foreign degrees (a 2014 notice was found but not readable), Bar Council, CA Sri Lanka, engineers (IESL).

## Public sector
- Appointments to the public service go through the Public Service Commission and the posting's own notice and form; not covered here (not checked). Do not substitute a CV for the form.
