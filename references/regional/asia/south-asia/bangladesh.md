## At a glance
- Paper: not covered here (not checked).
- Photo: AIUB's career office sample CV says « Include a clear photo of yourself in professional attire » (AIUB, OPA career portal, « Brush up your CV », undated, consulted 2026-11-26, S882; one university, a local custom rather than a rule). Offer it; the candidate decides. Omit for an ATS or multinational target (skill default, LOW, unsourced).
- CV language: English is the working assumption (LOW, unsourced). Bengali: `bn`, with Noto Sans/Serif Bengali (installed).
- Personal data: the Personal Data Protection Ordinance 2025 was approved by cabinet on 2025-10-09 and gazetted on 2025-11-06 (The Daily Star, « Bangladesh's Personal Data Protection Ordinance 2025: key takeaways », 2025-10-21, S871; TBS News, « Govt issues gazettes of 2 landmark ordinances on data protection, governance », 2025-11-09, S872). It covers private and public entities and requires informed, explicit consent; the Daily Star says some sections take effect 18 months after the gazette; the ordinance's own text, commencement and a reported 2026 amendment were not read (not checked). It does not tell the candidate what a CV may contain. Local CV habit puts father's name, NID and religion on the form; do not add them unless the posting's form asks (LOW, unsourced).
- Length: AIUB: 1 page for fresh graduates, 2 pages acceptable for experienced professionals (S882).
- Typst flags: `--lang en --region bd`; Bengali `--lang bn --region bd`.
- Fonts (`typst fonts` checked 2026-11-26): Noto Sans Bengali and Noto Serif Bengali present; nothing missing.
