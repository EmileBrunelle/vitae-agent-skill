# Market: Pakistan

Loaded on demand when the CV goes to an employer in Pakistan (private sector, public sector, university). Thinly sourced: no official body found publishes CV conventions; most custom rules below are not covered or labelled « LOW, unsourced ». For a candidate with Pakistani credentials going abroad, read [`../outbound/south-asia.md`](../outbound/south-asia.md) instead.

## At a glance
- Paper: not covered here (not checked).
- Photo: not required by any CV guidance found. LUMS Career Services advises against it on an academic résumé (« not standard globally ») (LUMS Career Services Office, « Academic Resumes », undated, consulted 2026-11-26, S880); official application forms are a different document and may ask for one (FPSC form: not checked). Default omit for private-sector/ATS targets; the candidate decides.
- CV language: English is the working assumption (LOW, unsourced). Urdu: `ur`, right-to-left; only if the posting asks.
- Personal data: no comprehensive data protection law in force. The Personal Data Protection Bill had finished consultation but had not passed both houses of Parliament, as of a guide covering to January 2026 (Chambers, « Data Protection & Privacy 2026, Pakistan: Law and Practice », consulted 2026-11-26, S870; secondary source, the bill's current stage is not checked). So no statute tells the candidate what to omit; follow the LUMS advice: no CNIC, marital status or photograph on a private-sector or academic CV (S880). Never a CNIC number on a CV sent widely (skill default, LOW, unsourced).
- Length: LUMS: résumé 1-2 pages, CV « can be multiple pages » (LUMS Career Services Office, « Resume & CV Writing », undated, consulted 2026-11-26, S881).
- Typst flags: `--lang en --region pk`; Urdu `--lang ur --region pk`.
- Fonts (`typst fonts` checked 2026-11-26): Noto Naskh Arabic and Noto Sans Arabic are present, so Urdu renders in Naskh. No Nastaliq font is installed (the usual Urdu print style): an Urdu CV in Naskh is legible but not the local typographic norm; tell the user.

## Degree verification and equivalence (HEC)
- HEC attests degrees, transcripts and certificates of Pakistani institutions through an online application: HEC scrutiny (including NADRA/CNIC consistency), verification by the university, then an e-Attestation Certificate; fee Rs. 3,000 per document on the page read. HEC does not attest foreign degrees: it issues equivalence letters, and only those are attested (HEC Pakistan, « Attestation of Degrees/Diplomas/Certificates », undated, consulted 2026-11-26, S875). An employer may therefore ask for an attested copy: do not state a degree as « attested » unless the candidate holds the certificate.
- For the 2-year BA/BSc and Associate Degree, see the outbound file (HEC statements S215, S216).

## Regulated professions
- Engineers: the Pakistan Engineering Council Act (Act V of 1976) defines the registered engineer as a holder of an accredited engineering qualification registered by the Council; it punishes whoever « not being registered under this Act, uses with his name of title any words or letters representing that he is so registered » (up to 3 months or a fine) and whoever undertakes professional engineering work without being on the Register (Pakistan Engineering Council Act 1976, Gazette of Pakistan Extraordinary 1976-01-14, ss. 2(xxvii), 27(1) and 27(4), copy hosted on a Gilgit-Baltistan government site, consulted 2026-11-26, S876; the 1976 text, later amendments not checked). Write « Engr. » or « PEC registered » only with a PEC registration number the candidate holds.
- Doctors and dentists: PM&DC grants a provisional licence for house jobs and a full licence after one year of house job (Pakistan Medical and Dental Council, « Frequently Asked Questions », consulted 2026-11-26, S877). Do not write a full-licence claim for a candidate still on the provisional one.
- Not covered here (not checked): Pakistan Bar Council, ICAP (chartered accountants), pharmacists, nurses.

## Public sector
- Federal and provincial public service commissions (FPSC, PPSC) recruit through their own application form and documents; not covered here (not checked: the FPSC pages did not load). Do not substitute a CV for the posting's form.
