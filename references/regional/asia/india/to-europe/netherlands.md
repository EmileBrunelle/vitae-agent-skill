# Indian credentials, Netherlands

Add-on to [`common.md`](common.md), which still applies.

- Highly skilled migrant: the IND page loaded states no diploma condition, only a recognised sponsor and a salary in line with the market rate (IND, « Highly skilled migrant », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S182). Do not write a diploma-evaluation line for this route unless the candidate or employer requests it.
- Nuffic credential evaluation (applied for through IDW): a comparison of a foreign diploma with a Dutch one, showing its level in the Netherlands. It is « not legally binding » and « Dutch employers determine who they hire » (Nuffic, « Applying for a credential evaluation », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S181).
- Where it is required: the EU Blue Card sponsor application asks for the diploma plus the original Nuffic evaluation, or, for someone without a diploma, a statement of skills, a CV and the vacancy text (IND, « Application for the purpose of residence 'holder of the EU Blue Card' (sponsor) », form 7517-2026/3, consulted 2026-11-07, S180). The orientation-year permit: « All foreign diplomas have to be evaluated by Nuffic before they can be accepted » (IND, « Residence permit for orientation year », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S179).
- CV: degree as awarded; `Nuffic (IDW): <Dutch level as stated in the evaluation>` only with the report in hand: « The credential evaluation is in Dutch and is electronically signed » and « indicates which Dutch level of education is comparable to your foreign diploma » (IDW, « International credential evaluation », S515); copy the level in its Dutch wording, or an English label only if the candidate holds an English version. Nuffic evaluates foreign programmes comparable to HAVO/VWO and higher education (HBO, WO); SBB evaluates those comparable to MBO/VMBO (IDW, « Who are we? », S516). The label format is the skill's convention. The evaluation itself goes to the employer or the IND, not the CV.
