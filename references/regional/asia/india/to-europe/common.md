# Indian credentials, European markets

Loaded on demand, in addition to the target market's file ([`europe/germany.md`](../../../europe/germany.md) + [`dach-common.md`](../../../europe/dach-common.md) for Germany, [`europe/ireland.md`](../../../europe/ireland.md) + [`uk-ireland-common.md`](../../../europe/uk-ireland-common.md) for Ireland, [`europe/france.md`](../../../europe/france.md) for France), when the candidate holds Indian credentials and targets Germany, Ireland, the Netherlands or France. This add-on is the only home of Ireland for Indian credentials. The market file gives the CV format; this add-on covers only degree evaluation and what the CV says about it. Indian-format blocks to drop and degree wording: [`india/to-anglosphere.md`](../to-anglosphere.md) and [`india/market.md`](../market.md). The Netherlands has no market file: do not infer its CV format; verify or ask, and label the assumption.

## Rules for every market below
- Degree wording, grades as printed with scale (never convert), evaluator conclusion in the report's own words with the evaluator named, exact month + year dates: rules in [`india/to-anglosphere.md`](../to-anglosphere.md) (Degree wording, Three-year bachelor's). Never write « equivalent to a German/Irish/Dutch/French degree ». No source gives CV wording for an evaluator line (the `Evaluator: ...` label formats below are the skill's convention); what each report states is sourced in each country file.
- ZAB itself says its statement « does not convert international grades into German grades » (ZAB, « Statement of Comparability », consulted 2026-11-07, S174). Under a German heading, the grade pattern from [`references/education-cases.md`](../../../../education-cases.md) applies (« Abschlussnote: 8,4 von 10 »).
- The report, the permit route, salary thresholds and the evaluation procedure belong in the attachments, the application form or the cover letter, not the CV. Never invent a permit or work-authorisation status ([`europe/switzerland.md`](../../../europe/switzerland.md)).
- Thresholds, fees and processing times change yearly and are not stated here: check the official page.
