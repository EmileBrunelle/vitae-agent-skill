# Indian credentials, Germany

Add-on to [`common.md`](common.md), which still applies.

- EU Blue Card: the visa « requires you to hold a German, a recognised or a comparable foreign academic degree », relevant to the job. The degree and the institution are checked in anabin; if the degree is not listed « entspricht » or « gleichwertig », or the institution is not « H+ », a ZAB Statement of Comparability is the alternative (German Embassy London / Auswärtiges Amt, « D-Visas: EU Blue Card », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S176). ZAB: the statement « proves that your university degree is comparable to a German university degree » (ZAB, « Statement of Comparability for an EU Blue Card », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S175).
- The ZAB statement names the degree, program and university, the program's length, mode, and graduation date and place, and the matching German level (bachelor's or master's); it « does not entitle its holder to university admission, continuing education, or to work in a regulated profession » (S174). Regulated professions need their own recognition; « Ingenieur »: [`europe/germany.md`](../../../europe/germany.md#protected-titles).
- CV: `Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.), Informatik, <awarding university>, Indien, 2015-2019, Abschlussnote: 8,4 von 10 (CGPA)`, then, only with the report in hand, `Zeugnisbewertung ZAB: <Ebene laut Zeugnisbewertung, z. B. « Bachelor-Ebene »>` (the ZAB report « nennt die entsprechende Ebene im deutschen Bildungssystem, zum Beispiel Bachelor-Ebene oder Master-Ebene » and « eine Notenumrechnung ist nicht Teil der Zeugnisbewertung »; ZAB (KMK), « Zeugnisbewertung für ausländische Hochschulabschlüsse », S514; the label format is the skill's convention). An anabin status (« H+ ») is a lookup result, not a credential: leave it to the form unless the candidate asks. Scans of diplomas go in the attachments ([`europe/germany.md`](../../../europe/germany.md#application-package)).
