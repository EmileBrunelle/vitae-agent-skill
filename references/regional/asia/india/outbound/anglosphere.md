# Outbound: Indian credentials, other markets

Loaded on demand when the candidate holds Indian credentials or Indian CV habits and targets the USA, Canada, the UK, Australia or New Zealand, in addition to the target market's file. Read together with [`india/market.md`](../market.md) (conventions in India itself). The target market's file still applies; this add-on covers only what is specific to Indian credentials and habits.

## Scope
Ireland, Germany, the Netherlands and France: [`india/outbound/europe-common.md`](europe-common.md).

## Drop Indian-format blocks
- Always drop the Declaration and Personal Details blocks; drop Class X/XII marks once the candidate has work experience; CTC and notice period stay off the CV. Details: [`india/market.md`](../market.md) and the target market file.

## Degree wording
- Write the degree exactly as awarded in English form (B.Sc., B.Com., BCA, B.E./B.Tech.). State only a verifiable length ("3-year program"); never write "equivalent to a US/4-year degree".
- Affiliated college: awarding university first, college in parentheses: "B.E. Mechanical Engineering, `<awarding university>` (`<affiliated college>`)", so the CV names the body that awards the degree (UGC Act 1956, s. 22: degrees are conferred only by a university established under a Central, Provincial or State Act, a deemed university, or an institution specially empowered by an Act of Parliament; Government of India, « The University Grants Commission Act, 1956 », s. 22, copy hosted by UGC, modified up to 20 Dec 1985, consulted 2026-11-07, S521); the order (university first) is a presentation convention (LOW, unsourced).
- Grades: as printed, with scale and class/division (« CGPA 8.4/10, First Class with Distinction », « 72%, First Division »); never convert. Keep a "First Division"/"Division I" label when the marksheet prints it: it matters for WES's 3-year rule below.

## Three-year bachelor's
- When a posting or form requires a 4-year degree and the candidate holds an evaluation report, add its conclusion to the degree line in the report's own words, naming the evaluator (e.g. "WES: `<equivalence as stated>`"); without a report, claim nothing. WES may treat a 3-year Indian bachelor's as a US bachelor's only if « The degrees have been earned in Division I » and the institution is « accredited by India's National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) » with a grade of "A" or better (WES, « Understanding the Three-Year Indian Bachelor's Degree in the U.S. », 2024-01-19, consulted 2026-11-07, S159).
- UK ENIC, since Sept 2025: "3 years/120+ credits" = UK Bachelor degree; "4+ years/160+ credits" = UK Bachelor (Honours); both FHEQ level 6 (UK ENIC, « Revised UK ENIC comparisons for Indian Bachelor degrees », 2025-09-30, consulted 2026-11-07, S160).
- USA, H-1B with a 3-year degree: routes in [`europe/outbound/north-america.md`](../../../europe/outbound/north-america.md#credential-evaluation). Give exact experience dates (month+year); equivalence is for an evaluator or immigration counsel, never written by the CV.
- Canada: ECA rules in [`europe/outbound/north-america.md`](../../../europe/outbound/north-america.md#credential-evaluation); for Indian degrees, the evaluation report states the Canadian equivalence, quoted as reported.
- Australia: a skills assessment only if held, in the body's own wording ("ACS Migration Skills Assessment: suitable, ANZSCO `<code>`, `<date>`"). « Suitable outcomes are valid for 2 years » (ACS, « InfoHub - Assessment Process », © 2025, consulted 2026-11-07, S162): drop the line once expired.

## Professional titles
- Chartered Accountant: ICAI membership rule in [`india/market.md`](../market.md). CPA Australia only once membership is confirmed: the ICAI-CPA Australia agreement is « a mutual pathway to dual professional recognition », not a membership by itself (CPA Australia, « CPA Australia and ICAI renew Mutual Recognition Agreement », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S164).
- Engineers: "B.E./B.Tech. (NBA-accredited)" only if the candidate confirms the program is accredited. India (NBA) became a Washington Accord signatory on 13 June 2014, for programmes NBA has accredited (Press Information Bureau, Ministry of HRD, « In a major achievement, India becomes permanent Signatory to Washington Accord », 2014-06-13, consulted 2026-11-07, S522); make no Washington Accord claim unless the candidate shows that their program was NBA-accredited at graduation (scope per programme and year: not checked). Protected-title rules of the target market apply ([`north-america/canada/market.md`](../../../north-america/canada/market.md), [`north-america/us/market.md`](../../../north-america/us/market.md), [`oceania/australia.md`](../../../oceania/australia.md), [`oceania/new-zealand.md`](../../../oceania/new-zealand.md)).
