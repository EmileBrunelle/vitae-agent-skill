## Indonesia

- Photo: « pas foto » still customary, decisively so for printed packets, government and BUMN (state-owned) recruitment; optional/de-emphasised in private tech and startups. Print sizes 3×4 cm or 4×6 cm; red or blue background survives in government/BUMN practice, white/light elsewhere; formal attire. Sector, not seniority, decides. HIGH (norm) / MEDIUM (private-sector optionality).
- Personal data: the traditional block (tempat/tanggal lahir, jenis kelamin, agama, status pernikahan) is now actively discouraged by current local career sources, anchored to UU PDP 27/2022; NIK never. Government/BUMN portals may still demand the full biodata on their own forms — that is the form, not the CV. HIGH on the advice, MEDIUM on practice.
- Language: Bahasa Indonesia for local/government/BUMN, English for multinationals and startups. Dates DD Month YYYY (« 12 Agustus 2026 ») or DD/MM/YYYY — never MM/DD.
- Sections: Data Pribadi · Riwayat Pendidikan · Pengalaman Kerja · Keahlian · Pelatihan · Bahasa · Hobi/Minat. The document is « Daftar Riwayat Hidup »; **« Surat Lamaran Kerja » remains a separate expected cover letter**, not merged into the CV as in the US. Hobbies are still normal here — closer to France than to the US/UK.
- Protected titles: « Ir. » (Insinyur) is a *professional* title, not a degree outcome — UU 11/2014 Keinsinyuran requires the Program Profesi Insinyur plus **STRI** registration, administered by **PII** (Persatuan Insinyur Indonesia). Accountants: « Ak. »/CA via **IAI**, public accountants (CPA Indonesia) via **IAPI**, with the Ministry of Finance « Akuntan Beregister » roll. HIGH.
- Credentials to gloss: SMA (academic upper secondary) / SMK (vocational upper secondary); D1–D3 diplomas; **D4 = Sarjana Terapan, applied bachelor's, treated as equivalent to S1**; S1 bachelor's, S2 master's, S3 doctorate. GPA is **IPK** on 4.0; honours bands « Dengan Pujian » (cum laude, ≥3.51), « Sangat Memuaskan », « Memuaskan ». Verification: **PDDikti** with PISN diploma numbering and the SatuDikti app (Kemdikbud/Kemdiktisaintek line of ministries — the ministry has been renamed more than once; cite the registry, not the ministry name).
- Channels: Jobstreet (dominant), Kalibrr, Glints (junior/tech), LinkedIn (mid-senior), TopKarir. WhatsApp follow-up is widely reported but was not verifiable in a citable source — LOW, label it if it matters.
