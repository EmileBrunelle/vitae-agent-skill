# Market: Singapore

Loaded on demand when the target market is Singapore. Read together with [`overview.md`](overview.md) (cross-country comparison and shared patterns). Singapore is one of six *non-interchangeable* Southeast Asian markets: never carry a finding from this country to a neighbour.

## At a glance
- Paper: a4 (LOW, unsourced).
- Photo: discouraged (TAFEP; Workplace Fairness Act 2025).
- CV language: English, British spelling.
- Personal data: none; no NRIC (PDPC advisory).
- Length: 1 p., 2 p. senior.
- Formality / verb style: Anglo-Saxon baseline (UK/Ireland file); verb-first bullets (LOW, unsourced).

## Application package
- **Closest of the six to Anglo-Saxon practice — read the UK/Ireland file for the baseline and only the deltas here.**
- Photo: discouraged, and heading for statute — TAFEP's Tripartite Guidelines tell employers to drop photo fields, and the **Workplace Fairness Act 2025** (passed Jan 2025, in force around 2027, larger employers first) makes requiring a photo without justification unlawful. Older SG résumé guides still describe a top-right photo; that is a fading norm, not current practice. (LOW, unsourced: direction and how many employers still expect one today)
- Language: English, **British spelling** (organised, optimisation) — same mechanics as the UK/Ireland file. (LOW, unsourced: consistent across local guides, no single authority)
- Length 1 page, 2 for senior. Sections are the standard Western set; nothing local to add.

## Personal data and law
- Personal data: TAFEP tells employers not to ask for age, gender, race, religion, marital/family status, disability, photo or NS liability — so volunteering them is off-convention. NRIC/FIN: PDPC's advisory guidelines say no NRIC is needed for a job application. (LOW, unsourced)

## Protected titles
Regulated-title claims here are the known case, never the exhaustive list — the universal rule in [`references/regional.md`](../../../regional.md) still applies.
- **Professional Engineers Board (PEB)** for "PE" under the Professional Engineers Act 1991 (the title carries the statutory Qualified Person role); Board of Architects; **SMC** for doctors; the legal profession under the Legal Profession Act / Ministry of Law with the Law Society as professional body; **ISCA** confers CA (Singapore) while **ACRA** is the corporate/audit regulator, not the individual-title regulator (LOW, unsourced: that split; verify before writing it into a header).

## Singapore specifics
- Credentials to gloss: PSLE; GCE **O-Level** / **N-Level**; **A-Level** (junior college); **Polytechnic Diploma**; **ITE Nitec / Higher Nitec**; **WSQ / SkillsFuture** for adult credentials; local honours classification. Verification: **OpenCerts** (GovTech, blockchain-backed) covers N/O/A-Level, ITE, polytechnic and WSQ certificates.
- Channels: **MyCareersFuture** (WSG's government portal — and the mandatory advertising channel for ≥14 days before an Employment Pass/S Pass hire under the Fair Consideration Framework, which is why it matters even to a foreign candidate), JobStreet, LinkedIn (primary here). Multinationals screen through mainstream ATS, so [`references/ats.md`](../../../ats.md) binds hardest in this market of the six.
- Work-pass eligibility: a foreign candidate's CV sometimes carries a pass line. EP qualifying-salary thresholds change yearly and scale with age and sector — **never quote a figure from this folder or from memory**; check MOM's current page.
