## Philippines

- **Paper: the exception in Southeast Asia.** "Short bond paper" (8.5×11 in, i.e. us-letter) is the historical and still-common print norm; a4 is increasingly accepted for soft copies. MEDIUM — genuinely mixed, no standards authority. Ask, or ship letter for print.
- Photo: a **2×2 in colour ID photo** (white/light background, formal attire) is still customary and expected — the opposite of Singapore, in the same file. HIGH.
- Personal data: the traditional biodata block was age, birthdate, civil status, height/weight, religion and government ID numbers. Current advice drops religion, height/weight, parents' names and SSS/PhilHealth/TIN, while birthdate and civil status persist in practice. DPA 2012 is the governing law (MEDIUM — no CV-specific guidance located). **Character References with names and contact details** remain a genuinely local expected section.
- Language: English, **American spelling** — the only market in Southeast Asia that is explicitly US-variant; Filipino/Tagalog is used in job-seeking guidance, not in the CV itself. HIGH.
- Length: 1 page fresh graduate, 2 pages at 5+ years — the multi-page reputation belongs to the government form, not the private-sector résumé.
- Local sections: "Seminars and Trainings", "Eligibility" / Civil Service Eligibility, "Character References", "willing to relocate".
- **CS Form 212 (Personal Data Sheet, rev. 2025, Civil Service Commission)** is a mandatory separate form for government employment: passport-size photo (4.5×3.5 cm, taken within 6 months, not photocopied or generated, name tag and signature over the photo) **plus the applicant's right thumbmark and signature**. It is a form, not a CV — do not let its fields leak into the résumé, and do not try to reproduce it in Typst. HIGH.
- Protected titles: the **Professional Regulation Commission (PRC)** is the regulator of "the various professions and occupations under its jurisdiction" (RA 8981, Sec. 7(a), S538) — "Engr.", CPA, "RN" and the rest come from board licensure (not checked here). Two things matter on a CV: **passing the board exam is not the same as holding a current licence** (renewal required, checkable via PRC's LERIS), and the licence number is customarily listed for regulated professions (MEDIUM-HIGH — customary, no canonical mandate found). **Lawyers are outside the PRC's board system**: the Constitution gives the Supreme Court the rule-making power over "the admission to the practice of law, the integrated bar" (1987 Const., Art. VIII, Sec. 5(5), S537); "Atty." follows admission to the Bar (the title itself is not defined in that text).
- Credentials to gloss: K-12 with **Senior High School strands** (Academic: STEM, ABM, HUMSS, GAS; or TVL technical-vocational); **TESDA National Certificates NC I-IV** (competency tiers); **CHED**-recognised degree programmes; Latin honours are a normal CV credential (MEDIUM). Verification: CHED for programmes, TESDA registry for NCs, PRC/LERIS for licences.
- Channels: JobStreet PH (dominant by volume), Kalibrr (graduate hiring), Indeed, LinkedIn (experienced/managerial). **NBI clearance** is an onboarding document, not a CV item.
