## Thailand

- Photo: a **formal studio headshot is standard and expected**, top-right; dark suit or formal attire, neutral background. Omitting it is noticed. Formal, front-facing, plain background (convention; LOW confidence: no source found).
- Personal data: DOB, nationality, religion, marital status and — for male applicants — **military service status** (Thailand conscripts; completion or exemption is commonly stated) are customary on the CV. **But** current PDPA-compliance guidance tells employers not to *request* marital status, religion (sensitive data needing explicit consent) or family plans, to issue a privacy notice, and to delete unsuccessful applicants' data within six months. **Custom (candidate volunteers) and compliance (employer must not ask) genuinely diverge here** — flag it to the user rather than resolving it silently; the candidate decides. HIGH on both halves.
- **Buddhist Era dates (พ.ศ. / B.E. = CE + 543)** appear on Thai-language transcripts, certificates and official forms. Any BE date on a document fed into a foreign-facing CV must be converted to CE (or annotated both ways) — otherwise a graduation year lands 543 years off and an ATS or reader misdates the whole education block. This is the single most important non-obvious convention in this file. HIGH.
- Language: Thai for domestic and government employers, English for multinationals and foreign-owned firms. HIGH.
- Sections: ประวัติส่วนตัว (personal profile) · จุดมุ่งหมายในงาน / เป้าหมายอาชีพ (objective) · ประวัติการทำงาน · ประวัติการศึกษา · ทักษะและความสามารถพิเศษ · บุคคลที่สามารถติดต่อได้ (references). Real Thai templates put **เงินเดือนต้องการ (expected salary)** inside the personal block as a standard field. MEDIUM-HIGH (from actual local templates).
- Protected titles: the **Council of Engineers Thailand (COE / กว.)** under the Engineer Act B.E. 2542 (1999) (Act sections not verified) licenses controlled engineering work. Foreigners are not categorically barred from engineering: a COE regulation of 2023 (Regulation on Registration of Foreign Professional Engineers B.E. 2566, Royal Gazette 21 June 2566, S536) lets foreign engineers registered in a country the COE has announced under a cross-border services agreement register with the COE (art. 3) for cross-border practice, art. 9 sets the duties of registered foreign engineers: comply with Thai law, and practise in collaboration with a local engineer or independently under that agreement; art. 4(1): applicant must not have permanent domicile in Thailand. The architects' position was not verified. Relevant to a foreign candidate's title choice, not just to wording. Council of Architects, Medical Council of Thailand, Lawyers Council of Thailand and TFAC (accountants) exist analogously but were not re-verified (MEDIUM-LOW).
- Credentials to gloss: **Mathayom 6 (M.6)** upper-secondary, or **ปวช. (Por Wor Chor)** vocational certificate, both ≈ high-school completion; **ปวส. (Por Wor Sor)** higher vocational diploma; bachelor's; **GPAX** cumulative GPA on 4.0 (admissions run through TCAS). Registry: **MHESI** (Office of the Permanent Secretary) for recognised institutions and programmes, **ONESQA** for quality-assessment reports.
- Channels: JobsDB and JobThai dominate; LinkedIn is supplementary, networking- and recruiter-facing (MEDIUM on the ranking, no penetration figure found).
- Paper: a4 assumed from regional/ISO practice but **not verified in a Thailand-specific source — LOW-MEDIUM, label the assumption**.
- Legacy conventions (nickname ชื่อเล่น as a CV field; height/weight/blood type on old-style forms) could not be confirmed as current: do not assert either. Transcript attachment is commonly expected for entry-level, government and university-linked applications (MEDIUM-LOW).
