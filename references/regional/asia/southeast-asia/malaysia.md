## Malaysia

- Photo: genuinely contested — current guidance splits, which is itself the finding. Include only if the posting or portal template invites it or the employer is local/government-linked; omit for multinational tracks. MEDIUM.
- Personal data: DOB, gender, marital status were standard and are now read as outdated, especially by multinationals; IC/MyKad never (onboarding only). PDPA 2010 governs the employer's handling, so "leave it off" here is market convention rather than a statutory duty on the candidate — state it that way. Bumiputera/nationality status appears on some public-sector and scholarship-linked postings only. MEDIUM on that last point (thin sourcing).
- Language: English by default for private sector and multinationals — a stronger English default than Indonesia's; Bahasa Malaysia for government (sektor awam), GLCs and some local SMEs.
- Length: **2-3 pages is accepted for experienced hires**, 1 page for fresh graduates. This is the one market in Southeast Asia that tolerates length; do not export it to the neighbours.
- **CGPA is a headline signal**, expected from fresh graduates and read as a credibility marker (3.5+ worth surfacing) — much more so than IPK in Indonesia. Structural convention: summary + key skills block at the top of page 1. References only when the posting asks.
- Protected titles: « Ir. » is conferred by the **Board of Engineers Malaysia (BEM)** under the Registration of Engineers Act 1967 (rev. 2015) after the Professional Interview; « Chartered Accountant (M) » / C.A.(M) by the **Malaysian Institute of Accountants (MIA)** under the Accountants Act 1967, whose restriction on unauthorised use is statutory. HIGH.
- Credentials to gloss: SPM (upper-secondary exit, ~O-Level), STPM (pre-university, ~A-Level) or Matrikulasi (1-year alternative pathway), Diploma (MQF level 4), then bachelor's/master's/PhD under the **Malaysian Qualifications Framework (MQF)**. Verification: the **MQA** register; foreign-degree equivalency for civil-service purposes runs through **JPA** (MEDIUM — not re-verified against a primary source).
