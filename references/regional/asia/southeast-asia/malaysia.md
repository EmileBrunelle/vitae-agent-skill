# Market: Malaysia

Loaded on demand when the target market is Malaysia. Read together with [`overview.md`](overview.md) (cross-country comparison and shared patterns). Malaysia is one of six *non-interchangeable* Southeast Asian markets: never carry a finding from this country to a neighbour.

## At a glance
- Paper: a4 (LOW, unsourced).
- Photo: contested — check the posting (LOW, unsourced).
- CV language: English by default; Bahasa Malaysia (BM) for government and GLCs.
- Personal data: drop DOB, marital status and IC; CGPA expected.
- Length: 1 p. fresh grad, 2-3 p. experienced.
- Formality / verb style: moderately formal (LOW, unsourced); summary + skills block first.

## Application package
- Photo: genuinely contested — current guidance splits, which is itself the finding. Include only if the posting or portal template invites it or the employer is local/government-linked; omit for multinational tracks. (LOW, unsourced)
- Language: English by default for private sector and multinationals — a stronger English default than Indonesia's; Bahasa Malaysia for government (sektor awam), GLCs and some local SMEs.
- Length: **2-3 pages is accepted for experienced hires**, 1 page for fresh graduates. This is the one market in Southeast Asia that tolerates length; do not export it to the neighbours.
- **CGPA is a headline signal**, expected from fresh graduates and read as a credibility marker (3.5+ worth surfacing) — much more so than IPK in Indonesia. Structural convention: summary + key skills block at the top of page 1. References only when the posting asks.

## Personal data and law
- Personal data: DOB, gender, marital status were standard and are now read as outdated, especially by multinationals; IC/MyKad never (onboarding only). PDPA 2010 governs the employer's handling, so "leave it off" here is market convention rather than a statutory duty on the candidate — state it that way. Bumiputera/nationality status appears on some public-sector and scholarship-linked postings only (LOW, unsourced: thin sourcing on that last point).

## Protected titles
Regulated-title claims here are the known case, never the exhaustive list — the universal rule in [`references/regional.md`](../../../regional.md) still applies.
- « Ir. » is conferred by the **Board of Engineers Malaysia (BEM)** under the Registration of Engineers Act 1967 (rev. 2015) after the Professional Interview; « Chartered Accountant (M) » / C.A.(M) by the **Malaysian Institute of Accountants (MIA)** under the Accountants Act 1967, whose restriction on unauthorised use is statutory. (LOW, unsourced)

## Malaysia specifics
- Credentials to gloss: SPM (upper-secondary exit, ~O-Level), STPM (pre-university, ~A-Level) or Matrikulasi (1-year alternative pathway), Diploma (MQF level 4), then bachelor's/master's/PhD under the **Malaysian Qualifications Framework (MQF)**. Verification: the **MQA** register; foreign-degree equivalency for civil-service purposes runs through **JPA** (LOW, unsourced: not re-verified against a primary source).
