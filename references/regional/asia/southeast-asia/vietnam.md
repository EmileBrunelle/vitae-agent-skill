## Vietnam

- **Two documents, never conflated.** The modern **CV** carries an optional informal headshot and ordinary contact data. The **sơ yếu lý lịch** is a standardised self-declared biographical form **certified/stamped by the ward or commune People's Committee** — no equivalent anywhere else in Southeast Asia — and is still commonly demanded inside the paper « hồ sơ xin việc » dossier by state-sector employers and SOEs, alongside a health certificate and copies of ID and diplomas. Private and FDI employers increasingly ask for the CV alone. HIGH on the distinction, MEDIUM on who still demands the form.
- The form's field list is the personal-data sink: gender, DOB, place of birth, **quê quán (ancestral hometown, a distinct field from current residence)**, permanent residence, CCCD number, ethnicity, religion, marital status, and organisation membership dates. Keep all of it off the CV. Note the **sổ hộ khẩu household-registration booklet was abolished 1 Jan 2023** (data moved to the national population database) even though forms still print a « hộ khẩu thường trú » field. HIGH.
- Data protection: **Law 91/2025/QH15 (PDPL)**, effective 1 Jan 2026, with implementing Decree 356/2025, replaces Decree 13/2023. No CV-specific guidance has emerged yet — say so rather than inferring one. HIGH on the instrument, LOW on CV consequences.
- Language: Vietnamese for local/government/SOE, English for FDI and multinationals; **bilingual VN+EN is a real practice** for multinational targets. Gregorian calendar throughout, DD/MM/YYYY. MEDIUM.
- Sections: Thông tin cá nhân · Mục tiêu nghề nghiệp · Kinh nghiệm làm việc · Học vấn (Trình độ học vấn) · Kỹ năng · Hoạt động/Chứng chỉ · Người tham chiếu.
- Protected titles / practice certificates are **ministry-issued per sector**, not council-issued: construction practice certificate (chứng chỉ hành nghề xây dựng) from the Ministry of Construction (rank I) or provincial department (ranks II-III), required of project managers, chief designers and supervisors — **and reportedly slated for abolition in a 2025 policy push, so verify its status before writing it**; medical practice certificate (CCHN KCB) under the Ministry of Health per Law 15/2023; a **separate** pharmacy certificate (CCHND); the legal profession via a Ministry of Justice certificate plus admission to a provincial bar under the national lawyers' federation; CPA via the Ministry of Finance with VACPA as the professional body (MEDIUM — not re-verified).
- Credentials to gloss: THPT (upper-secondary diploma) → trung cấp / cao đẳng (vocational / college) → **Cử nhân** bachelor's, with **Kỹ sư** as the engineering-degree title → **Thạc sĩ** master's → **Tiến sĩ** doctorate. Grades run on a **10-point scale** (a 4.0 scale is also used with its own bands) — a bare "8.2" will be misread by a foreign ATS or reader, so always mark the scale. MOET sets policy; **VN-NARIC** handles foreign-credential recognition.
- Channels: VietnamWorks (dominant), TopCV, CareerBuilder.vn, CareerViet, JobsGO. LinkedIn is growing fast but ≈10% population penetration (end-2025). Facebook recruitment groups are heavily used (MEDIUM — general knowledge, not re-verified).
