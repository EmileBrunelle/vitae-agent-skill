# Korea: official-source facts only

Loaded on demand, in addition to the target market's file, when the CV goes to a Korean employer (including a foreign applicant moving to Korea), or when the candidate's home habits are Korean and the target is elsewhere. There is no full market file: only what an official source supports is below; everything else (layout, tone, length, Korean 이력서 format) is unverified: ask the user or a current local source, and label the assumption.

## Employer documents (Fair Hiring Procedures Act)
- The Fair Hiring Procedures Act (채용절차의 공정화에 관한 법률), from 2019-07-17, bars an employer from requiring job seekers to state, or from collecting proof of, items not needed for the job: appearance, height, weight; region of origin, marital status, property; and the education, occupation and property of direct ascendants, descendants and siblings. The ban applies only to those listed items, and an ID photo may be attached to confirm identity (MOEL, « 7월 17일부터 채용의 공정성을 침해하거나 직무와 관계 없는 개인 정보를 요구하지 못한다 » (2019-07-16); S225). MOEL's card news adds that the information may still be requested if « 직무의 수행에 반드시 필요 » and that fines go up to 5 million won (« [카드뉴스] 채용절차법 안내 » (2019-11-08); S226).
- For the CV: height, weight, marital status, birthplace and family details are not needed on a Korean CV and an employer cannot require them for a job where they are not essential; a photo is permitted for identity; the sources do not say it is required (S225). Both sources are 2019 and MOEL pages; the current statute text did not load, so amendments after 2019 are unchecked, and the Korean 이력서 layout itself is unsourced.

## Foreign applicant moving to Korea
- Professional work visa. E-7 covers professional (E-7-1), semi-professional (E-7-2), general (E-7-3) and skilled (E-7-4) work, stay 3 years, extendable (Korea Immigration Service, « Visa Navigator » Ver 2023.05; S227). The Ministry of Justice wage floors for 2026-02-01 to 2026-12-31 are 31.12 million won a year for E-7-1, 25.89 million for E-7-2 and E-7-3, 26.0 million for E-7-4, unless the Minister set another figure for an occupation (MOJ notice 2025-406; S228). An offer must clear the floor; the occupation list and the degree/career conditions per occupation were not found in a readable official source.
- Gated professions. Law, accounting and medicine are a separate status, E-5, not E-7 (S227; single source).
- Degree route. Foreign graduates in a high-technology field of a university in the Times Higher Education top 200 or the QS top 500 (the guide lists both and does not say whether either suffices) may enter as D-10 interns within 3 years of the degree; after more than 1 year of internship and a job paying at least the previous year's GNI per capita, the E-7 education and experience requirements are waived (S227; high-technology fields only).
- Foreign documents. A Seoul Immigration Office leaflet (PDF created 2021) says documents issued abroad, such as a criminal record certificate or degree, must carry the apostille of the home government or the Korean consulate's confirmation, and that originals issued within 3 months are the rule (S229; immigration applications in general, not hiring: LOW for a hiring context).
- TOPIK. TOPIK I covers levels 1-2 and TOPIK II levels 3-6; paper and IBT versions use different score bands, so a raw score needs its test type (NIIED; S230). The 2026 test notice says scores are valid for 2 years from the announcement date (MOE notice 2025-317; S231), so give the level with its result date. NIIED lists applying to companies in Korea or abroad among the uses of the test (S230).
