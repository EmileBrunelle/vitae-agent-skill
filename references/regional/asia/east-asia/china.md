# Market: China (mainland, domestic employers)

Loaded on demand when the CV goes to an employer in the PRC (Chinese or foreign candidate), or when the candidate's home habits are mainland-Chinese and the target is elsewhere; read in addition to the target market's file. There is no full market file: only what a source read supports is below. Everything else (layout, tone, section order, photo specifications) is not checked: ask the user or a current local source, and label the assumption. Chinese credentials aimed at a market outside China: [`../outbound/china.md`](../outbound/china.md).

## At a glance
- Paper: A4 is the paper size recommended for Chinese résumés, by a US business-school career guide (S809); no PRC official source read.
- Photo: career guides expect a formal ID-style photo and warn against selfies and casual shots (S809, S811); one Chinese university centre calls it optional (« 如果贴上照片，会为你减分，则不贴 », S810). No law read requires or bans it: when the user is unsure, ask whether the employer is state-owned or foreign (S809 says political status is required for state-owned firms, optional for foreign ones).
- CV language: not covered here (not checked). Mainland Chinese is written in simplified characters (LOW, unsourced: general knowledge).
- Personal data: the PIPL governs how the employer handles a CV; the laws read bar discriminatory recruitment but none lists forbidden CV fields (S800, S801, S802, S803): see below.
- Length: one page is the usual recommendation (S809, S811); S810 sets no page limit. Two-page CVs for senior candidates are not covered by a source read.
- Spelling/script: simplified Chinese. Typst flags `--lang zh --region cn` for `pick_design.py` (written into `#set text(lang:, region:)`). The local Typst sees Noto Sans CJK SC and Noto Serif CJK SC; S809 suggests a standard font (宋体, 微软雅黑), so check the font choice against the fonts available at render time.

## Personal data and what employers may ask
- Labor Contract Law: the employer « 有权了解劳动者与劳动合同直接相关的基本情况 » and the worker must state it truthfully (art. 8); fraud makes a contract void (art. 26, point 1); the employer may not hold the identity card or other documents, nor ask for a deposit or guarantee (art. 9) (NPC Standing Committee, « 中华人民共和国劳动合同法 », 2007-06-29, amended 2012-12-28, consulted 2026-11-26, S800). The wording limits the employer's right to know to information directly tied to the contract; it does not itemize CV fields.
- Employment Promotion Law: employers must offer equal opportunity and not discriminate (art. 26); may not refuse a woman for her sex or set a higher standard for her, nor write marriage or childbirth restrictions into the contract (art. 27); may not discriminate against disabled people (art. 29) or refuse someone for being a carrier of an infectious disease (art. 30); must not set discriminatory limits on rural workers (art. 31); victims may sue (art. 62) (NPC Standing Committee, « 中华人民共和国就业促进法 », 2007-08-30, amended 2015-04-24, consulted 2026-11-26, S801; the page read is the 2015 text, later amendments not checked).
- Recruitment ads: employers' postings must be truthful and lawful and may not carry discriminatory content on ethnicity, race, sex or religion; the fine is 10,000 to 50,000 yuan (State Council, « 人力资源市场暂行条例 », 国务院令第700号, 2018-06-29, in force 2018-10-01, art. 24 and 43, consulted 2026-11-26, S803). A 2024 MOHRSS and CAC notice adds age and education to the discriminatory content to be screened out of postings, and plans credit sanctions for serious falsification of identity, education or experience by job seekers (MOHRSS and Cyberspace Administration of China, « 人力资源社会保障部 中央网信办关于进一步加强人力资源市场规范管理的通知 », 人社部发〔2024〕62号, 2024-08-23, consulted 2026-11-26, S804). The CV consequence: do not invent or inflate credentials.
- Women: a Beijing nine-department notice bars employers and agencies from asking women about marriage and childbearing, from making pregnancy tests part of pre-employment medicals, and from setting sex preferences (Beijing Daily via Xinhua, « 北京九部门联合印发通知：招聘时禁问妇女婚育情况 », 2019-06-28, consulted 2026-11-26, S805; a Beijing local notice reported by the press, the national notice text was not read).
- PIPL (effective 2021-11-01): processing must have a clear, reasonable purpose directly related to it, with the least impact on the person (art. 6); consent is not the only basis, HR management under lawful rules and contracts is another (art. 13, point 2); sensitive information (biometrics, religion, specific identity, medical health, financial accounts, location, minors under 14) needs the person's separate consent (art. 28-29) (NPC Standing Committee, « 中华人民共和国个人信息保护法 », 2021-08-20, in force 2021-11-01, consulted 2026-11-26, S802). For a CV this means: leave out ID-card number, home address, health and religion unless the employer asks in a form (LOW, unsourced: prudence).
- Field conventions, from career guides only (no official source): S811 lists name, gender, date of birth, phone, email, job objective and photo as basic; political status, ID number, marital status and current address depend on the employer, and state enterprises care about political status. S810 lists name, phone, email and objective as core, with height, political status and place of origin (生源地) as optional advantages, only for a return to the home region. S809 lists name, contact, date of birth, headshot and political status (required for state-owned firms, optional for foreign companies). The guides disagree on gender and on native place; no law read forbids them, so follow the target employer's form and the user's comfort.

## Education, dates and credentials
- S809: education in reverse chronological order, no high school, institution's official name translation checked, GPA as on the transcript. S810: give enrolment and graduation dates in full, never « 08级 ». S811 gives phone numbers as 3-4-4 (130-0000-0000) and recommends a short, concrete 自我评价 section (no source calls it mandatory).
- Verifying degrees: employers and individuals can check a CHSI online verification report with its code; see the outbound file (S567, S568, S569). Conversely, a foreign degree used for a PRC work permit goes through a Chinese authentication (below).
- Hukou, party membership details, 档案 transfer, graduate-employment contracts (三方协议): not covered here (not checked).

## Regulated professions (protected titles)
- Physician: a doctor is one who holds the qualification and is registered at a medical institution (art. 2); a qualification exam (art. 8) and a registration (art. 13) are required; practising without being a physician is fined from two to ten times the gain (art. 59) (NPC Standing Committee, « 中华人民共和国医师法 », 2021-08-20, in force 2022-03-01, consulted 2026-11-26, S806). A foreign-trained or unregistered doctor must not write « 执业医师 » or equivalent.
- Lawyer: a lawyer is one who holds a practice licence (art. 2); calling oneself a lawyer without it draws an order to stop, confiscation and a fine of one to five times the gain (art. 55) (NPC Standing Committee, « 中华人民共和国律师法 », 2007-10-28, third amendment 2017-09-01, consulted 2026-11-26, S807; the 2017 text names the old judicial exam, the current qualification route was not checked).
- Teachers, accountants, engineers, architects, nurses, pharmacists, other titles: not covered here (not checked).

## Foreign applicant working in China
- Work permit categories (SAFEA, trial guide D 50001, issued 2017-03-29): class B professionals hold a bachelor's degree or higher and 2 years or more of related experience, aged at most 60, with relaxations for innovators, skilled workers, outstanding foreign graduates, points-based and treaty cases; class A high-end talent has no age, degree or experience limit (State Administration of Foreign Experts Affairs, « 外国人来华工作许可服务指南（暂行） », 编号 D 50001, 2017-03-29, consulted 2026-11-26, S808). The guide asks for the highest degree certificate (a degree earned abroad must be authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate, by the embassy of the degree's country in China, or by a Chinese credential-authentication body), a criminal record certificate, Chinese translations stamped by the employer, and its model checklist names the CSCSE (the Ministry of Education's overseas-study service centre) authentication copy of the highest degree (S808). The CV's degrees, dates and employers must match these documents (LOW, unsourced: inference). A 2017 trial version: later versions exist and were not read.
- Not found: official current work-permit guide text after 2017, Chinese-language requirements, CV expectations of foreign-invested employers, the CSCSE authentication rules from a CSCSE or MOE page.
