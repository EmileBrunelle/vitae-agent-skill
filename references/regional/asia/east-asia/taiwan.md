# Market: Taiwan (foreign professionals as applicants)

Official-source facts only, from the agencies that decide a foreign professional's status; no full market file. CV conventions of Taiwanese employers (photo, length, Traditional-Chinese layout, job boards' fields) are NOT sourced here: say so, do not infer from Japan or Korea ([`japan.md`](japan.md), [`korea.md`](korea.md)) or from the mainland, and ask for a current local posting or recruiter. Chinese-language CV: not sourced.

## Work permit: write the CV to prove the qualification
- The employer signs a contract and applies to the Ministry of Labor (Contact Taiwan, FAQ « Work Permits », undated; S232). For specialised or technical work (Employment Service Act Art. 46 para 1 item 1) the foreigner must also meet one of: a professional-exam credential; **a master's or higher in a related department, or a bachelor's in a related department plus more than two years' related experience**; more than one year in a multinational before assignment to Taiwan; more than five years' related experience with originality and outstanding performance (MOL, Qualifications and Criteria Standards, Art. 5, amended 2024-08-01, Chinese original checked; S233). The salary floor is « the amount published by the central competent authority » (Art. 8): no figure in the law, quote none.
- Experience counts only if obtained after graduation; a student part-time job does not qualify. The two-year requirement is waived for a bachelor's or higher from a university on the Ministry of Education's list of the world's top 1,500, or when the employer holds a headquarters or R&D-centre letter, is in a Science Park or is a semiconductor manufacturer (S232, Q5-Q6; one source). So: dated entries (month and year), post-graduation roles first, each tied to the field of the degree and the target job; do not pad with internships to reach "two years".
- The CV's titles and dates should match the employment certificates (LOW: inferred from the Gold Card rule below); tell the user to request them from former employers early.

## Gold Card (Employment Gold Card, National Development Council)
- Twelve fields, one field and one regulation per application (S234; the site also says « ten » elsewhere). The CV is itself a listed document: « Personal CV and proof of relevant domestic or overseas work » (S235). Science-and-technology salary route: most recent monthly salary of at least NTD 160,000, shown by an annual tax statement issued within 3 years of the application or an employer certificate. Other routes: three or more years' related experience plus patents, technology transfer, publications or other proof (S235). CV consequence: list patents, publications and awards in their own dated section; keep salary out of the CV text (the tax statement is the evidence).
- Employment proof is a certificate of service or departure with « full name, company name, position, actual duration of employment » and a stamp or signature; **« an offering letter is not accepted »** (S235). Documents not in Mandarin or English need a translation: an English CV is acceptable here; for work-permit files the rule is not sourced.

## Overseas documents
- The Standards for hiring foreign workers say the authority « may require » verification by a ROC overseas mission for documents made abroad (Art. 9, Chinese original: 得要求; the English translation says « will require »; S236). Contact Taiwan lists 25 countries whose documents must be certified at a Taiwan representative office (among them Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, Indonesia, Sri Lanka); experience letters from a parent or branch company in an internal transfer need no certification (S232, Q3; sole source for the list). Credentials from these countries: warn the candidate to start certification before the offer.

## Mandarin level
- TOCFL: Bands A, B, C, six levels, « a certificate for each sub-test » (Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing), scores valid two years (SC-TOP, TOCFL Guidelines 2021; S237). Write « TOCFL Level 4, Listening and Reading, 2025 » per skill actually passed, with test year; never one global level. A preliminary Band Novice exists. Do not write a CEFR equivalent: the guide says the descriptors are « based on » the CEFR but gives no per-level table.
- Certificates can be checked in the Ministry of Education's Digital Certificate Verification System (S238).

## What an employer may not ask
- Employment Service Act Art. 5(I): no discrimination against applicants on race, class, language, thought, religion, political party, place of origin, birth place, gender, sexual orientation, age, marital status, appearance, facial features, disability, astrological sign, blood type or former union membership (amended 2025-01-20; Chinese original checked, 性傾向 = sexual orientation; S239). Art. 5(II) also bars requesting private data not needed for the job. The article says nothing about photos or CV contents: do not claim a photo ban. Do not volunteer age, marital status or a photo unless the posting asks (LOW: inferred from the list).
