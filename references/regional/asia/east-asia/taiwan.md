# Foreign workers: Taiwan

Loaded on demand when a foreign professional applies to a Taiwanese employer, or when a CV goes to one. Official-source facts only, from the agencies that decide a foreign professional's status; there is no full market file.

## Not covered here
- CV conventions of Taiwanese employers (photo, length, Traditional-Chinese layout, job boards' fields) are not covered here (not checked): say so, do not infer from Japan or Korea ([`japan.md`](japan.md), [`korea.md`](korea.md)) or from the mainland, and ask for a current local posting or recruiter. Chinese-language CV: not checked.

## Work permit: write the CV to prove the qualification
- The employer signs a contract and applies to the Ministry of Labor (Ministry of Economic Affairs, Contact Taiwan, « FOREIGN TALENTS - Work Permits » (FAQ, undated), consulted 2026-11-07, S232). For specialised or technical work (Employment Service Act Art. 46 para 1 item 1) the foreigner must also meet one of: a professional-exam credential; **a master's or higher in a related department, or a bachelor's in a related department plus more than two years' related experience**; more than one year in a multinational before assignment to Taiwan; more than five years' related experience with originality and outstanding performance (Ministry of Labor, Taiwan (Laws & Regulations Database), « Qualifications and Criteria Standards for Foreigners Undertaking the Jobs Specified Under Subparagraph 1 to 6 of Paragraph 1 of Article 46 of the Employment Service Act », Art. 5, amended 2024-08-01, consulted 2026-11-07, S233; Chinese original checked). The salary floor is « the amount published by the central competent authority » (Art. 8): no figure in the law, quote none.
- Experience counts only if obtained after graduation; a student part-time job does not qualify. The two-year requirement is waived for a bachelor's or higher from a university on the Ministry of Education's list of the world's top 1,500, or when the employer holds a headquarters or R&D-centre letter, is in a Science Park or is a semiconductor manufacturer (Ministry of Economic Affairs, Contact Taiwan, « FOREIGN TALENTS - Work Permits » (FAQ, undated), consulted 2026-11-07, S232, Q5-Q6; one source). So: dated entries (month and year), post-graduation roles first, each tied to the field of the degree and the target job; do not pad with internships to reach "two years".
- The CV's titles and dates should match the employment certificates (LOW, unsourced: inference from the Gold Card rule below); tell the user to request them from former employers early.

## Gold Card (Employment Gold Card, National Development Council)
- Twelve fields, one field and one regulation per application (National Development Council, Taiwan Employment Gold Card Office, « Qualifications », consulted 2026-11-07, S234; the site also says « ten » elsewhere). The CV is itself a listed document: « Personal CV and proof of relevant domestic or overseas work » (Taiwan Employment Gold Card Office, « Field of Science & Technology », consulted 2026-11-07, S235). Science-and-technology salary route: most recent monthly salary of at least NTD 160,000, shown by an annual tax statement issued within 3 years of the application or an employer certificate. Other routes: three or more years' related experience plus patents, technology transfer, publications or other proof (S235). CV consequence: list patents, publications and awards in their own dated section; keep salary out of the CV text (the tax statement is the evidence).
- Employment proof is a certificate of service or departure with « full name, company name, position, actual duration of employment » and a stamp or signature; **« an offering letter is not accepted »** (Taiwan Employment Gold Card Office, « Field of Science & Technology », consulted 2026-11-07, S235). Documents not in Mandarin or English need a translation: an English CV is acceptable here; for work-permit files the rule is not checked.

## Overseas documents
- The Standards for hiring foreign workers say the authority « may require » verification by a ROC overseas mission for documents made abroad (Ministry of Labor, Taiwan (Laws & Regulations Database), « Regulations on the Permission and Administration of the Employment of Foreign Workers », Art. 9, amended 2025-12-30, consulted 2026-11-07, S236; Chinese original: 得要求; the English translation says « will require »). Contact Taiwan lists 25 countries whose documents must be certified at a Taiwan representative office (among them Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, Indonesia, Sri Lanka); experience letters from a parent or branch company in an internal transfer need no certification (Ministry of Economic Affairs, Contact Taiwan, « FOREIGN TALENTS - Work Permits » (FAQ, undated), consulted 2026-11-07, S232, Q3; sole source for the list). Credentials from these countries: warn the candidate to start certification before the offer.

## Mandarin level
- TOCFL: Bands A, B, C, six levels, « a certificate for each sub-test » (Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing), scores valid two years (SC-TOP, « TOCFL Guidelines for 2021 », 2021, consulted 2026-11-07, S237). Write « TOCFL Level 4, Listening and Reading, 2025 » per skill actually passed, with test year; never one global level. A preliminary Band Novice exists. Do not write a CEFR equivalent: the guide says the descriptors are « based on » the CEFR but gives no per-level table.
- Certificates can be checked in the Ministry of Education's Digital Certificate Verification System (SC-TOP, TOCFL home page, notice « Important Notice for TOCFL Test Takers », consulted 2026-11-07, S238).

## What an employer may not ask
- Employment Service Act Art. 5(I): no discrimination against applicants on race, class, language, thought, religion, political party, place of origin, birth place, gender, sexual orientation, age, marital status, appearance, facial features, disability, astrological sign, blood type or former union membership (Ministry of Labor, Taiwan (Laws & Regulations Database), « Employment Service Act », amended 2025-01-20, consulted 2026-11-07, S239; Chinese original checked, 性傾向 = sexual orientation). Art. 5(II) also bars requesting private data not needed for the job. The article says nothing about photos or CV contents: do not claim a photo ban. Do not volunteer age, marital status or a photo unless the posting asks (LOW, unsourced: inference from S239).
