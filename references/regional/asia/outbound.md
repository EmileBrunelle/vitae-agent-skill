# Asian credentials, other markets (excluding India)

Loaded on demand, in addition to the target market's file, when the candidate holds credentials from Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, the Philippines or China and targets a market outside their home country. India: [`india/to-anglosphere.md`](../india/to-anglosphere.md). The target market's file still applies; this add-on covers only what is specific to these credentials.

## Rules that hold for every country below
- Write the degree as printed; state a length only when the transcript shows it; never write "equivalent to a US/4-year degree". With an evaluation report, add its conclusion in its own words naming the evaluator; without one, claim nothing ([`india/to-anglosphere.md`](../india/to-anglosphere.md) rule). Grades as printed, never converted: [`references/education-cases.md`](../../education-cases.md).
- Nuffic distinguishes Pass from Honours and 2-, 3- and 4-year lengths, and adds that the result depends on the programme's content: keep both on the degree line. Nuffic's mappings are for Dutch levels only (last section); never restate them as a US/Canadian/UK outcome.

## Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal
- The length on the transcript decides, not the title. Nuffic lists Pakistani Pass degrees (BA/BSc, 2 years; BBA/BCom/Technology), 3-year Honours and 4-year Honours separately; Bangladesh pass (3 years) and honours; Nepal 3- and 4-year bachelor's (Nuffic, « Higher education », Pakistan, Bangladesh and Nepal pages (undated), consulted 2026-11; S185, S186, S187). No two-year Bangladeshi or Nepali bachelor's appeared in the pages loaded.
- NOT confirmed: phase-out dates of the 2-year BA/BSc and the HEC Associate Degree/bridging route (HEC pages did not load); WES, ECE, UGC Bangladesh and UGC Nepal were not loaded. Ask for degree, graduation year and the evaluation report; state no recognition status.

## Philippines: K-12 and pre-reform degrees
- « As of 2012, primary and secondary education have been reformed through the 'K to 12 Basic Education Program' ». Before it, « secondary education lasted 4 years, so 2 years shorter than now »; Senior High School (grades 11-12) was introduced in 2016 and its diploma first awarded in 2018 (Nuffic, « Primary and secondary education », Philippines (undated), consulted 2026-11; S188). Nuffic's bachelor's comparison splits at programme start before/after 2018 (S189): give enrolment and graduation years on the degree line; claim no equivalence.
- Local CV habits: [`southeast-asia/philippines.md`](southeast-asia/philippines.md).

## China: CHSI and CSSD verification
- China Daily republishing CHSI, 2017 (MEDIUM): CHSI online verification reports are for « employers, individuals and other related institutions » and cover « student records, qualification certificates, and admission information »; a third party checks one by entering the verification code on the CHSI page, and holders can extend validity (China Daily gov. channel, « Brief introduction to online verification report » (2017-07-19), consulted 2026-11; S190). Fees and procedure may have changed: verify before telling the candidate.
- In general, WES refers applicants to CSSD (Center for Student Services and Development, formerly CHESICC): a report in Chinese needs its translation sent « in CHSI Archive » via the International Cooperative Application portal, and for Sino-foreign programmes WES says « contact CSSD (formerly CHESICC) to verify the documents » (WES US Help Center, FAQs « The CDGDC degree verification report… » and « Which documents should I provide if I attended or completed a Sino-foreign program »; both undated, consulted 2026-11; S191, S192). The names CDGDC, CSSD, CHESICC and CHSI appear in these sources as the bodies involved; no loaded source explains how they relate beyond "CSSD (formerly CHESICC)".
- Convention (LOW): mention a verification on the CV only if the candidate holds a valid report; never print the code.

## Netherlands target only
Nuffic mappings, read with the Netherlands section of [`india/to-europe/common.md`](../india/to-europe/common.md): Pakistan: BA/BSc (Pass) « a VWO diploma »; BBA/BCom/Technology (Pass) « an associate degree or 2 years of HBO »; 3-year BA/BSc Honours « 3 years of HBO or 1 year of WO »; 4-year BA/BSc Honours « an HBO bachelor's degree or 2 years of WO »; 3-year BBA/BCom Honours « 3 years of HBO »; 4-year BBA/BCom Honours « an HBO bachelor's degree » (S185). Bangladesh: pass (3 years) « 3 years of HBO or 1 year of WO »; honours « an HBO bachelor's degree or 2 years of WO » (S186). Nepal: 3 years « 3 years HBO or 1 year WO »; 4 years « an HBO bachelor's degree or 2 years WO » (S187). Philippines: started before 2018 « an associate degree or 2 years of HBO »; 2018 or later « an HBO bachelor's degree or 2 years of WO » (S189).
