# Algeria: diplomas in the home system

Part of the Maghreb set ([`maghreb-common.md`](../maghreb-common.md)); market file: [`market.md`](market.md). Official titles and lengths as the texts state them; print the title exactly as on the diploma. Lengths, ministry labels and evaluator wording for candidates going abroad: [`maghreb-outbound/common.md`](../maghreb-outbound/common.md) (Algeria S271-S272 not repeated here). GPA and mention display on a Québec résumé: [`north-america/quebec/education.md`](../../north-america/quebec/education.md).

- Licence « nouveau régime »: three years, six semestres (décret exécutif 04-371 of 21 November 2004, art. 2, JORADP n° 75, 24 November 2004, S620). Repealed by décret exécutif 08-265 of 19 August 2008 (art. 20), which sets the licence, master and doctorat regime: master 120 credits, 30 per semester (art. 12), 180 credits when prepared in an école outside the university (art. 13); the doctorat « sanctionne la formation de troisième cycle » (art. 19) (JORADP n° 48, 24 August 2008, S621; amendments not checked).
- Ingénieur d'État, magister and the old titles (licence classique, DES, DEUA, Technicien Supérieur): lengths from the ministry's table in S271 (see the outbound file). The legal text re-establishing the ingénieur d'État in 2021-2022 was not found.
- Language of instruction: the minister stated the goal of extending « l'utilisation de la langue anglaise » (the quoted sentence, as printed, omits the language word in « dispensées en langue par des enseignants qualifiés »; it does not establish that 80 % of specialities are taught in English) (radio forum of 10 February 2025, MESRS, S622). On 9 July 2026 the MESRS announced the first English-taught cohorts graduating at Université d'Alger 3 (information-communication, political science, economics-management, sports), « the vanguard of the nationwide expansion » (S623). No decision text found; scope (which programmes, mandatory or progressive) and diploma language not verified. Print the diploma title as issued.
