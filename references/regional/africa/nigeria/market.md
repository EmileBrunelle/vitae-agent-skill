## NYSC certificate
- NYSC Act, s. 12(1): it is « the duty of every prospective employer to demand and obtain » a copy of the person's Certificate of National Service, any exemption certificate and other prescribed particulars; s. 12(2): the employer must produce them on demand to a police officer of a specified rank. s. 13(3): contravening a provision of the Act is an offence (fine N5,000, up to three years, or both); s. 13(4): false statements and forged certificates carry the same penalty. s. 17(3): an exempted person is issued a certificate of exemption (S843: a lawyer's blog reproduction of the Act, 2025-01-31; no enactment date or chapter number on it; the penalty amounts are those printed there, later revisions not checked).
- The NYSC Director-General said it is « mandatory to demand the Certificate of National Service or Exemption Certificate » before hiring any degree or HND holder, public or private sector, citing s. 12(1)-(2) (The Sun (Nigeria), « Request for NYSC exception, service certificate before employment, DG tells employers of labour », 18 October 2023, consulted 2026-11-26, S844; press report of a speech).
- For the CV: a Nigerian degree or HND holder will be asked for the certificate, so list service as completed (or exempted) only if the candidate holds the matching certificate; never write « NYSC » service that has not been completed or exempted. Whether to print the certificate number or dates on the CV is not covered by the sources (not checked).
- Not checked: who is exempt or excluded (s. 17 criteria), age limits, foreign-trained Nigerians, what employers accept in practice.

## Personal data (NDPA 2023)
- Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (copy via DataGuidance, S842; date of assent and later amendments not read). s. 24(1): personal data must be processed fairly, lawfully and transparently, collected for specified purposes, and « adequate, relevant, and limited to the minimum necessary » for those purposes. s. 65: « sensitive personal data » covers race or ethnic origin, religious or similar beliefs, health status, sex life, political opinions or affiliations, trade union membership, and genetic and biometric data for identification (S842). s. 30(1): sensitive data may be processed only on a listed basis, such as the data subject's consent for the specific purpose or obligations under employment or social security laws (S842).
- For the CV: the Act regulates the employer (controller), not the candidate. It does not say what a CV may contain. As a minimisation default for the candidate: leave out the sensitive categories above (religion, ethnic origin, health, political or union affiliation) unless the job requires them (LOW, unsourced: inference from S842). The regulator's guidance (NDPC GAID 2025) was not read.
