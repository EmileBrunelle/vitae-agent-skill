## Employment Equity Act (what it says about applicants)
- Act No. 55 of 1998, text as enacted (Government Gazette No. 19370, 19 October 1998, S840; later amendments were not read, so the grounds and definitions below may have changed).
- s. 6(1): no person may unfairly discriminate, directly or indirectly, against an employee in any employment policy or practice, on grounds « including race, gender, sex, pregnancy, marital status, family responsibility, ethnic or social origin, colour, sexual orientation, age, disability, religion, HIV status, conscience, belief, political opinion, culture, language and birth ». s. 9: for ss. 6, 7 and 8, « employee » includes an applicant for employment (S840).
- s. 6(2): it is not unfair discrimination to take affirmative action measures consistent with the Act, or to distinguish, exclude or prefer a person on an inherent requirement of a job (S840).
- « Designated groups » (s. 1, as enacted): « black people, women and people with disabilities » (S840).
- For the CV: the Act bars the employer from discriminating on those grounds; the text read does not say a CV may not state them and does not require a candidate to declare race, gender or disability on a CV. Do not add race, marital status, religion or a disability line by default. Whether a company's own application form asks for them for its equity reporting is not covered by the text read: leave it to the form and the candidate (LOW, unsourced: inference from S840).
- Not checked: the Employment Equity Regulations and forms (EEA1), the 2013 amendments, how « designated employer » reporting uses applicant data.

## Qualifications: SAQA and the NQF
- SAQA's Directorate of Foreign Qualifications Evaluation and Advisory Services evaluates a foreign qualification « to determine the level of the National Qualifications Framework at which foreign qualifications should be recognised ». Some work-permit applications, institutions and professional bodies require the SAQA certificate of evaluation (Government of South Africa, gov.za, « Evaluate foreign qualifications », undated page, copyright 2026, consulted 2026-11-26, S841).
- For a foreign-qualified candidate: do not write an NQF level or « SAQA-equivalent » on the CV unless the candidate holds the SAQA certificate; then give the level exactly as the certificate states it (LOW, unsourced: inference from S841). The page read does not list the NQF levels or describe the certificate's content; SAQA's own pages did not load (not checked).
