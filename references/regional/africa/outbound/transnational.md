# Outbound: Maghreb credentials, campus abroad of a foreign school and double diplomas

Loaded on demand when a credential was earned on a campus abroad of a foreign school, delivered under a foreign partnership, or is a double or joint diploma (LaSalle Maroc/Tunis, delocalised Québec AEC, Dauphine Tunis). Read together with [`common.md`](common.md) and the target market's file.

## Campus abroad of a foreign school (Dauphine Tunis, Collège LaSalle, delocalized cégep AEC)
- One rule: print each credential as the parchment issues it, with its own issuer, then the campus city and country. « Reconnu par » is not « décerné par »: Collège LaSalle's Morocco and Tunis pages say graduates get a diploma accredited by the host state and a diploma « reconnu par Collège LaSalle Montréal » (Collège LaSalle Maroc (LCI Education), « À propos », snapshot 2026-03-11, S285; Collège LaSalle Tunis (LCI Education), « À propos », snapshot 2025-10-13, S286), so that is two credentials, and neither is stated to be a DEC.
- **Collège LaSalle, Morocco and Tunis** (private schools of the LCI network, not Québec colleges). Print the host title as issued: `Diplôme de technicien (DT)`, `Diplôme de technicien spécialisé (DTS)` (Collège LaSalle Maroc (LCI Education), « Nos programmes en formation initiale », snapshot 2026-03-11, S287) or the 12-month « Bachelor » (title only, no level label: no issuer is stated); `Brevet de Technicien Supérieur (BTS)` or `Brevet de Technicien Professionnel (BTP)` in Tunisia (Collège LaSalle Tunis (LCI Education), « Programmes et cours », snapshot 2026-01-15, S288). Write « accredited by the state » only if the parchment carries the ministry visa: MIFI's 2015 internal note says only visaed diplomas of accredited programs are evaluable, accreditation lasting at most 5 years (Gouvernement du Québec, MIFI, « Accréditation des écoles privées au Maroc », 2015-02-12, consulted 2026-11-07, S289; the ministry's 2013 list is per program and dated, Ministère de l'Éducation nationale et de la formation professionnelle (Maroc), « Répertoire des Établissements de Formation Professionnelle Privée Accrédités par région », June 2013, consulted 2026-11-07, S290), so the candidate checks the visa before relying on it.
- A Tunis « préparation DEC/AEC » (four sessions in Tunis, then completion at Collège LaSalle Montréal to obtain the DEC or AEC; the school's FAQ, Collège LaSalle Tunis (LCI Education), « Comprendre les Diplômes au Collège LaSalle Tunis », snapshot 2026-02-15, S291; absent from the program list of 2026-01-15, Collège LaSalle Tunis (LCI Education), « Programmes et cours », snapshot 2026-01-15, S288, so ask whether it still runs): a candidate who completed the Montréal part prints the DEC or AEC as `Collège LaSalle Montréal`, and the Tunis sessions as a separate studies line (see `education-cases.md`). Without the Montréal parchment, only the Tunis studies.
- Never write « DEC », « AEC », « diplôme québécois » or « Canadian credential » unless the parchment says so. A Québec AEC delivered abroad exists as a funded type of project (Programme Québec-Francophonie en formation technique, with Moroccan, Tunisian and Algerian partners; the call closed 15 November 2023 and the page is marked « Terminé »; Gouvernement du Québec (MRIF), « Programme Québec-Francophonie en formation technique », 2025-04-03, consulted 2026-11-07, S283): if the candidate's parchment says AEC, write the AEC, its issuer and the partner campus, nothing more. The program's guide says it does not influence the immigration process (Gouvernement du Québec (MRIF), « Guide de présentation d'un projet » (FAQ q.14), consulted 2026-11-07, S284).
