## Public sector

- Follow the avis. Tanitjobs: « Ici, le CV "libre" compte peu : c'est le dossier de candidature exigé par l'avis qui fait foi … Si l'avis demande un CV, respecte exactement le format, la langue et les rubriques indiqués » (S599).
- Language: Constitution 2022 art. 6, « Tunisia is part of the Arab nation and the official language is Arabic » (English translation on constituteproject.org, Arabic original not read, S598). No text read requires a CV or application in Arabic, and no circular on Arabic in administration was found. The concours.gov.tn portal headlines (3-6 November 2026) are in Arabic; no notice was opened (S624); the INS notice is in French and lets candidates take the written exam « en arabe ou en français, selon le choix du candidat » (S596).
- Dossier versus CV: practice varies. Décret 2012-833 of 20 July 2012 applies loi 2012-4 « portant dispositions dérogatoires pour le recrutement dans le secteur public »: a derogatory regime, not the common one, and art. 18 ends it when loi 2012-4 stops applying (copy hosted on legislation-securite.tn, JORT copy not read, still in force not verified, S595). Its art. 11-A lists six pieces at application: an online form, a CNI copy, « un curriculum vitae », a diploma copy (with the equivalence attestation for foreign diplomas), a certificate of registration at the employment office, and proof of professional activity or a payslip where it does not match the diplomas; other pieces may be required if the decision of art. 10 says so. The INS notice (JORT n° 110, 26 November 2021, S596) lists a demande, a CNI copy and a diploma copy, no CV. The 2026 Justice avis (29 administrateurs des greffes) requires registering on a platform to download the registration form, then a complete dossier; press summary only, the arrêté and its piece list not read (La Presse de Tunisie, 24 May 2026, S597).
