## Country notes

**Algeria.** The outlier: longer CVs (2-3 pages) and the fullest personal-data custom of any market in this skill — closer to pre-2010 French practice. Anti-discrimination minimalism has NOT landed here; the universal omit-by-default rule still applies, but warn the user that local recruiters (per Emploitic guidance) may expect birth data, marital status and service national, and let the candidate decide. Public sector wants Arabic.
