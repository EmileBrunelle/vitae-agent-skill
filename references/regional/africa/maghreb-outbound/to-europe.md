# Maghreb credentials, Europe (France first)

Add-on to [`common.md`](common.md) and [`europe/france.md`](../../europe/france.md) (levels, « Bac+N », protected titles incl. « ingénieur diplômé »: not repeated). Only France is sourced; for Belgium, Spain and Germany, see the last section. Attestation de comparabilité wording and concours: [`north-america/quebec/outbound-france.md`](../../north-america/quebec/outbound-france.md) and [`india/to-europe/france.md`](../../india/to-europe/france.md) (same sources, not Maghreb-specific).

## France
- No legal equivalence: « Le principe juridique d'équivalence n'existe pas en France » (Euroguidance France, « Titulaire d’un diplôme étranger », consulted 2026-11-07, S281). Non-regulated job: it is up to the employer (or the administration running a concours) « d’apprécier les qualifications ou diplômes présentés et le niveau professionnel », and « il se peut que celui-ci demande une Attestation de comparabilité » (S281). So the CV gives the title, institution, country and years; the employer does the comparing.
- Attestation de comparabilité: France Éducation international (ENIC-NARIC France) is the only issuer (S281). Service-public.fr (« To have a diploma obtained abroad recognized in France », English version, verified 17 April 2025, S183): « no legal value » and « not compulsory » (said in the study-admission section; the final decision rests with the institution), only diplomas recognized by the issuing country, diploma courses only, diplomas giving access to a regulated profession not concerned. Fee and delay differ between the two pages (€120 on Service-public.fr; « trois ou quatre mois pour un coût de 70€ » on Euroguidance): state neither, tell the candidate to check the Centre's page. Regulated professions need an exercise authorization from the competent authority (S281).
- Without an attestation, no « Bac+N » claim for a foreign title; Algerian ministry labels (« Bac+05 ») are the home system's own and are not French levels.
- Mixed path (e.g. Moroccan bac + Tunisian licence + French master): one entry per diploma, never merged (`references/education-cases.md`); foreign ones keep the full title and years so a reader sees 3-year vs 4-year licences. Whether one attestation covers several diplomas is unclear (Service-public speaks of diplomas « examined », the Centre's own page was not readable): ask the Centre.
- « Ingénieur diplômé »: on the CTI page read, the Code de l'éducation (art. L642-1, L642-4) reserves the diploma to CTI-accredited institutions; that page addresses institutions, not foreign diploma holders, and whether a foreign diploma may carry the title was not verified. So print `Diplôme d'Ingénieur d'État, <école>, <pays>` as issued and add nothing; ask the CTI if the candidate wants more.
- Double diplôme from a French university's campus in the Maghreb: Université Paris Dauphine-PSL says its Tunis campus « délivre un double diplôme, français et tunisien, en licence et en master » (Dauphine Tunis, home page, consulted 2026-11-07, S282). Write two lines, one per issuer (`Licence, Université Paris Dauphine-PSL, campus de Tunis, Tunisie` and the Tunisian diploma with its own title and issuer), not one merged line; if the parchment is a single joint one (« diplôme conjoint »), one line naming both issuers (`references/education-cases.md`). Read the parchment, not the brochure, to tell which. No source loaded says whether a French diploma delivered in Tunisia needs an attestation de comparabilité in France: do not claim either way.
