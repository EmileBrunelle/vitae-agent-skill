# Maghreb credentials: campus abroad of a foreign school, double diplomas

Loaded on demand, in addition to [`common.md`](common.md) and the target market's file, when a credential was earned on a campus abroad of a foreign school, delivered under a foreign partnership, or is a double or joint diploma (LaSalle Maroc/Tunis, delocalised Québec AEC, Dauphine Tunis).

## Campus abroad of a foreign school (Dauphine Tunis, Collège LaSalle, delocalized cégep AEC)
- One rule: print each credential as the parchment issues it, with its own issuer, then the campus city and country. « Reconnu par » is not « décerné par »: Collège LaSalle's Morocco and Tunis pages say graduates get a diploma accredited by the host state and a diploma « reconnu par Collège LaSalle Montréal » (the school's own pages, Wayback captures of 2026-03-11 and 2025-10-13, S285, S286), so that is two credentials, and neither is stated to be a DEC.
- **Collège LaSalle, Morocco and Tunis** (private schools of the LCI network, not Québec colleges). Print the host title as issued: `Diplôme de technicien (DT)`, `Diplôme de technicien spécialisé (DTS)` (S287) or the 12-month « Bachelor » (title only, no level label: no issuer is stated); `Brevet de Technicien Supérieur (BTS)` or `Brevet de Technicien Professionnel (BTP)` in Tunisia (S288). Write « accredited by the state » only if the parchment carries the ministry visa: MIFI's 2015 internal note says only visaed diplomas of accredited programs are evaluable, accreditation lasting at most 5 years (S289; the ministry's 2013 list is per program and dated, S290), so the candidate checks the visa before relying on it.
- A Tunis « préparation DEC/AEC » (four sessions in Tunis, then completion at Collège LaSalle Montréal to obtain the DEC or AEC; the school's FAQ of 2026-02-15, S291; absent from the program list of 2026-01-15, so ask whether it still runs): a candidate who completed the Montréal part prints the DEC or AEC as `Collège LaSalle Montréal`, and the Tunis sessions as a separate studies line (see `education-cases.md`). Without the Montréal parchment, only the Tunis studies.
- Never write « DEC », « AEC », « diplôme québécois » or « Canadian credential » unless the parchment says so. A Québec AEC delivered abroad exists as a funded type of project (Programme Québec-Francophonie en formation technique, with Moroccan, Tunisian and Algerian partners; the call closed 15 November 2023 and the page is marked « Terminé », S283): if the candidate's parchment says AEC, write the AEC, its issuer and the partner campus, nothing more. The program's guide says it does not influence the immigration process (S284).
- Market-specific wording and evaluation: [`to-quebec-canada.md`](to-quebec-canada.md), [`to-europe.md`](to-europe.md).
