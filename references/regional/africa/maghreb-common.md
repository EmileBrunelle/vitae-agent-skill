# Market: Maghreb — common baseline (Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia)

Common baseline for three *non-interchangeable* markets; always read with the target country's file ([`morocco.md`](morocco.md), [`algeria.md`](algeria.md), [`tunisia.md`](tunisia.md)), never on its own. Diplomas: [`maghreb-education.md`](maghreb-education.md). Each claim carries its source (S### ids, see `docs/sources.md`); LOW means unsourced.

Paper: a4 (all three; convention, LOW, unsourced).

## Shared baseline (all three countries)

- **CV language.** Private sector: French is the norm (banking, audit, multinationals) and English is rising in tech/finance/multinationals (LOW, unsourced; the only sourced point is Tunisian PME, S599: CV « en français », see [`tunisia.md`](tunisia.md)); the posting's language dictates the CV's. **Public sector:** follow the avis. Arabic is the official language in all three constitutions (Morocco art. 5 with Tamazight, Algeria arts. 3-4 with Tamazight, Tunisia art. 6), and loi 91-05 (Algeria) is the only statute read on concours language; no text read says a candidate's CV must be in Arabic. Detail and sources in the country files.
- **Photo:** the custom differs per country (see the country file); no law read requires one for a private-sector CV. Tell the user the local custom; the candidate decides.
- French CV mechanics (convention, LOW, unsourced for the Maghreb; see [`europe/france.md`](../europe/france.md)): espace insécable before `:`, typographic apostrophes, months lowercase, noun-phrase or past-participle bullets. Dates: month/year (sourced for Morocco, [`morocco.md`](morocco.md)).
- A **« titre de CV »** (a targeted role headline under the name): sourced for Morocco only ([`morocco.md`](morocco.md)); unsourced for the other two, harmless everywhere.
- Job boards (LOW, unsourced; « dominant » is an unverified popularity claim): Morocco — Rekrute, Emploi.ma, Anapec (public); Algeria — Emploitic (dominant), Ouedkniss, Emploi Partner; Tunisia — Tanitjobs (dominant), Keejob, ANETI (state sector). LinkedIn for cadres everywhere.
- Regulated titles: expertise comptable is ordre-regulated in Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia (texts below); Morocco: « Nul ne peut ... porter le titre d'expert comptable, s'il n'est inscrit à l'Ordre des experts comptables » (loi 15-89, art. 3; penalties arts. 100-101; text as hosted by Upsilon Consulting, Bulletin officiel copy not read, S531). Algeria: usurping the title expert-comptable is treated as illegal practice (loi 10-01 du 29 juin 2010, art. 74, JORADP n° 42, 2010, amendments not checked, S532); the body is the « ordre national des experts-comptables ». Tunisia: « Nul ne peut exercer la profession d'expert comptable s'il n'est inscrit au tableau de l'ordre des experts comptables de Tunisie » (loi 88-108 du 18 août 1988, art. 3; illegal practice and abusive use of the titles is a délit, art. 26, penalties by reference to art. 159 of the code pénal; text as hosted by the OECT, JORT copy and amendments not checked, S561). Check the ordre before writing any regulated-sounding title (universal rule in [`references/regional.md`](../../regional.md)).
