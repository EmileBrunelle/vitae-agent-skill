# Market: Maghreb — common baseline (Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia)

Common baseline for three *non-interchangeable* markets; always read with the target country's file ([`morocco.md`](morocco.md), [`algeria.md`](algeria.md), [`tunisia.md`](tunisia.md)), never on its own. Verified against local sources 2026-08 (Jobsquare, Rekrute, Stagiaires.ma; Emploitic, Africarrières; Tanitjobs, Keejob). Where a point could not be confirmed by a local primary source it is labelled LOW confidence.

Paper: a4 (all three).

## Shared baseline (all three countries)

- **CV language: French is the private-sector norm** (banking, audit, multinationals); Arabic mainly for the public sector; English rising fast in tech/finance/multinationals and often required bilingually in international finance hubs. The posting's language dictates the CV's.
- **Photo: still culturally expected** in local practice (formal, front-facing, neutral background — convention, no official source; LOW confidence), though not legally required and slowly declining in multinationals. Tell the user the local custom; the candidate decides.
- French CV mechanics apply (see [`europe/france.md`](../europe/france.md)): espace insécable before `:`, typographic apostrophes, months lowercase, noun-phrase or past-participle bullets. Dates: month/year.
- A **« titre de CV »** (a targeted role headline under the name) is expected and worth retailoring per application (verified for Morocco; LOW for the other two, but harmless everywhere).
- Job boards: Morocco — Rekrute, Emploi.ma, Anapec (public); Algeria — Emploitic (dominant), Ouedkniss, Emploi Partner; Tunisia — Tanitjobs (dominant), Keejob, ANETI (state sector). LinkedIn for cadres everywhere.
- Regulated titles: expertise comptable is ordre-regulated in Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia (texts below); Morocco: « Nul ne peut ... porter le titre d'expert comptable, s'il n'est inscrit à l'Ordre des experts comptables » (loi 15-89, art. 3; penalties arts. 100-101; text as hosted by Upsilon Consulting, Bulletin officiel copy not read, S531). Algeria: usurping the title expert-comptable is treated as illegal practice (loi 10-01 du 29 juin 2010, art. 74, JORADP n° 42, 2010, amendments not checked, S532); the body is the « ordre national des experts-comptables ». Tunisia: « Nul ne peut exercer la profession d'expert comptable s'il n'est inscrit au tableau de l'ordre des experts comptables de Tunisie » (loi 88-108 du 18 août 1988, art. 3; illegal practice and abusive use of the titles is a délit, art. 26, penalties by reference to art. 159 of the code pénal; text as hosted by the OECT, JORT copy and amendments not checked, S561). Check the ordre before writing any regulated-sounding title (universal rule in [`references/regional.md`](../../regional.md)).
