# Academic: Morocco

Loaded on demand when the CV or academic CV lists a Moroccan university rank or a school-teaching post. Read together with [`education.md`](education.md). Rank names below are the Arabic wording of the Bulletin officiel; no French edition was read, so French equivalents are not verified: print the French wording from the candidate's own decision or attestation, and ask when there is none.

## Ranks

- Enseignants-chercheurs: décret 2.23.545 of 2 August 2023 (statut particulier), BO 7220, Arabic edition (S653). Art. 2: three frames, « أستاذ التعليم العالي », « أستاذ محاضر مؤهل », « أستاذ محاضر ». Grades: professeur A to D (art. 13), habilité A to C (art. 16), محاضر A to D (art. 18). Art. 19: the محاضر is recruited by concours among doctorate holders (registration on a national list required from 1 January 2025). Art. 17: the habilité is appointed from محاضر holding the « التأهيل الجامعي » who meet one of two conditions: at least 4 years as محاضر, or at least step 3 of grade A for those coming from the teaching frames named in art. 20; the habilitation conditions are set by a separate regulatory text (not read). Art. 14: among other routes, the professeur comes from the habilités with at least 6 years, after a scientific file defended before a committee; art. 15: up to 5% of the posts opened for محاضر recruitment may instead recruit professeurs by concours among outside candidates meeting the art. 15 conditions. The décret abrogates 2.96.793 of 19 February 1997; its application texts stay in force until replaced.
- School teachers: décret 2.23.819 of 6 October 2023 (statut des personnels de l'éducation nationale), BO 7237; copy hosted by mmsp.gov.ma, not the BO; a 2024 amendment of concours articles was reported and not read (S654). Arts. 10 and 12: frames « أستاذ التعليم الابتدائي », « أستاذ التعليم الثانوي » (sub-frames « الإعدادي » and « التأهيلي »), « أستاذ مبرز للتربية الوطنية »; each has the grades « الثانية », « الأولى », « الممتازة ». Art. 34: entry at the second grade for holders of the « شهادة التأهيل التربوي » issued by the regional centres of education and training (CRMEF), after a concours.
- CRMEF trainers (art. 29): frames « أستاذ مساعد », « أستاذ مؤهل », « أستاذ مؤطر », mapped by art. 30 to « أستاذ محاضر », « أستاذ محاضر مؤهل », « أستاذ التعليم العالي » (S654).
