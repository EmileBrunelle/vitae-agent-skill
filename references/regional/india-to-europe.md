# Indian credentials, European markets

Loaded on demand, in addition to the target market's file ([`dach.md`](dach.md) for Germany, [`uk-ireland.md`](uk-ireland.md) for Ireland, [`france.md`](france.md) for France), when the candidate holds Indian credentials and targets Germany, Ireland, the Netherlands or France. This add-on is the only home of Ireland for Indian credentials. The market file gives the CV format; this add-on covers only degree evaluation and what the CV says about it. Indian-format blocks to drop and degree wording: [`india-outbound.md`](india-outbound.md) and [`india.md`](india.md). The Netherlands has no market file: do not infer its CV format; verify or ask, and label the assumption.

## Rules for every market below
- Degree wording, grades as printed with scale (never convert), evaluator conclusion in the report's own words with the evaluator named, exact month + year dates: rules in [`india-outbound.md`](india-outbound.md) (Degree wording, Three-year bachelor's). Never write « equivalent to a German/Irish/Dutch/French degree ». No source loaded gives CV wording for an evaluator line: every `Evaluator: ...` format below is LOW confidence.
- ZAB itself says its statement « does not convert international grades into German grades » (ZAB, « Statement of Comparability », consulted 2026-11-07, S174). Under a German heading, the grade pattern from [`education-cases.md`](../education-cases.md) applies (« Abschlussnote: 8,4 von 10 »).
- The report, the permit route, salary thresholds and the evaluation procedure belong in the attachments, the application form or the cover letter, not the CV. Never invent a permit or work-authorisation status ([`dach.md`](dach.md)).
- Thresholds, fees and processing times change yearly and are not stated here: check the official page.

## Germany
- EU Blue Card: the visa « requires you to hold a German, a recognised or a comparable foreign academic degree », relevant to the job. The degree and the institution are checked in anabin; if the degree is not listed « entspricht » or « gleichwertig », or the institution is not « H+ », a ZAB Statement of Comparability is the alternative (German Embassy London / Auswärtiges Amt, « D-Visas: EU Blue Card », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S176). ZAB: the statement « proves that your university degree is comparable to a German university degree » (ZAB, « Statement of Comparability for an EU Blue Card », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S175).
- The ZAB statement names the degree, program and university, the program's length, mode, and graduation date and place, and the matching German level (bachelor's or master's); it « does not entitle its holder to university admission, continuing education, or to work in a regulated profession » (S174). Regulated professions need their own recognition; « Ingenieur »: [`dach.md`](dach.md).
- CV: `Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.), Informatik, <awarding university>, Indien, 2015-2019, Abschlussnote: 8,4 von 10 (CGPA)`, then, only with the report in hand, `Zeugnisbewertung ZAB: <Niveau laut Zeugnisbewertung>` (LOW confidence on wording). An anabin status (« H+ ») is a lookup result, not a credential: leave it to the form unless the candidate asks. Scans of diplomas go in the attachments ([`dach.md`](dach.md)).

## Ireland
- Critical Skills Employment Permit, per the Department's checklist: « A degree qualification or higher in the relevant field is required » for roles on the Critical Skills Occupations List at the minimum remuneration of €40,904; at the €68,911 tier, the worker « must have the necessary level of experience if they do not hold a degree qualification », so experience can replace the degree; the Graduate CSEP applies only where the worker « graduated in the last 12 months », with « a level 8 or higher degree – or equivalent » relevant to the role (Department of Enterprise, Tourism and Employment, « Critical Skills Employment Permit Checklist », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S177). Thresholds change: check the current page. Experience-based routes make exact month + year dates matter.
- NARIC Ireland (QQI) « recognises foreign qualifications by providing online comparability statements », « often required for further study, employment, or work permits »: advice, not a legal document, generic and non-personalised, comparing to an Irish award type and level; « The decision to recognise a qualification is the responsibility of the employer » (QQI, « Recognition of foreign qualifications », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S178). Because the statement is generic, quote it only when the candidate holds it, as `NARIC Ireland: <comparison as stated>` (LOW confidence on wording), never as a personal ruling.
- CV format, NFQ level 8, full degree title: [`uk-ireland.md`](uk-ireland.md). Irish public-service competitions use the form, not the CV: evidence qualifications there.

## Netherlands
- Highly skilled migrant: the IND page loaded states no diploma condition, only a recognised sponsor and a salary in line with the market rate (IND, « Highly skilled migrant », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S182). Do not write a diploma-evaluation line for this route unless the candidate or employer requests it.
- Nuffic credential evaluation (applied for through IDW): a comparison of a foreign diploma with a Dutch one, showing its level in the Netherlands. It is « not legally binding » and « Dutch employers determine who they hire » (Nuffic, « Applying for a credential evaluation », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S181).
- Where it is required: the EU Blue Card sponsor application asks for the diploma plus the original Nuffic evaluation, or, for someone without a diploma, a statement of skills, a CV and the vacancy text (IND, « Application for the purpose of residence 'holder of the EU Blue Card' (sponsor) », form 7517-2026/3, consulted 2026-11-07, S180). The orientation-year permit: « All foreign diplomas have to be evaluated by Nuffic before they can be accepted » (IND, « Residence permit for orientation year », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S179).
- CV: degree as awarded; `Nuffic: <level as stated>` only with the report in hand (LOW confidence on wording). The evaluation itself goes to the employer or the IND, not the CV.

## France
- Attestation de comparabilité (ENIC-NARIC France): it « describes the level of study of the foreigners diploma and compares it to the level of French studies »; it « shall have no legal value » and « shall not be compulsory », but an institution or employer may ask for it; for non-regulated professions « it is your future employer who evaluates the qualifications » (Service-Public, « To have a diploma obtained abroad recognized in France », English version, verified 17 April 2025, S183). It is not issued for diplomas that give access to a regulated profession (S183).
- Carte talent (Service-Public, « Carte talent : carte de séjour pluriannuelle d'un étranger en France », verified 1 June 2026, consulted 2026-11-07, S184; the page says it is « en cours de mise à jour » and announces changes from 30 October 2026 to decision deadlines, carte bleue refusal or withdrawal and some salary conditions, none concerning diplomas as listed: re-check). Only « salarié qualifié » requires a diploma « obtenu en France » (« Avoir obtenu en France un diplôme au moins équivalent au master »), so an Indian degree does not meet it. « Chercheur » (« Avoir un diplôme au moins équivalent au master ») and « porteur de projet, création d'entreprise » (same diploma, or « 5 années d'expérience professionnelle de niveau comparable ») set no France condition. « Carte bleue européenne »: « un diplôme sanctionnant au moins 3 années d'études supérieures » or 5 years of relevant experience. The other categories on the page (innovative-company and mission employees, medical and pharmacy professions, mandataire social, artistic and cultural, renowned persons, the other porteur de projet routes) show no diploma condition.
- CV: [`france.md`](france.md) for format and levels; write the real diploma name and institution and country, grade as printed with its Indian scale (« CGPA 8,4/10 »), no « Bac+N » gloss from years of study. Add `Attestation de comparabilité ENIC-NARIC France : <niveau tel qu'indiqué>` only if held (LOW confidence on wording); the quoted wording stays the attestation's own. Everything about the titre de séjour goes in the application, not the CV.
