# Market: Southeast Asia (ID · MY · SG · PH · VN · TH)

Six markets in one file because they are *not* a bloc. Verified country by
country (current sources, 2025-2026); the divergences below are larger than
the ones between France and the UK. **Never carry a finding from one country
to its neighbour** — Singapore is close to Anglo-Saxon practice while Thailand
expects a formal studio photo, and both are in this file.

Shared across all six: paper **a4** (exception: the Philippines, below; and
Thailand's a4 is inferred, not verified), and reverse-chronological order
everywhere. Every regulated-title claim here is
the known case, never the exhaustive list — the universal rule in
`regional.md` still applies.

## Cross-cutting patterns (then read the country section)

- **Photo splits three ways, not two**: expected/customary (ID printed &
  public sector, PH, TH), contested/check the posting (MY, VN modern CV),
  discouraged and heading for statute (SG). Where a photo is expected the
  mechanics are the same as the DACH file (formal, front-facing, plain
  background) — the difference is that ID and PH also specify a *print size*.
- **Personal-data culture is generally richer than in Europe and is
  contracting**, driven by recent data-protection statutes (ID UU PDP
  27/2022; MY PDPA 2010; SG PDPA + TAFEP/WFA; PH DPA 2012; VN Law 91/2025
  PDPL in force 1 Jan 2026, replacing Decree 13/2023; TH PDPA). Two of these
  regulate what the *employer* may collect, not what the candidate may
  volunteer (MY, TH explicitly) — so custom still runs ahead of the statute.
  Default: omit, and tell the user the local custom (universal rule).
- **A separate administrative form is often the real personal-data sink**:
  ID « surat lamaran » + KTP/ijazah scans, PH CS Form 212, VN sơ yếu lý lịch.
  Keep those fields off the CV; they belong to the form.
- **National ID numbers never go on the CV** in any of the six (ID NIK/KTP,
  MY IC/MyKad, SG NRIC/FIN, PH SSS/TIN/PhilHealth, VN CCCD, TH ID) —
  identity-fraud risk, and SG's PDPC advisory says a job application needs no
  NRIC at all. HIGH confidence.
- **Language rule that holds in all six: match the language of the posting.**
  Local language ⇒ local employer, government, SOE/GLC; English ⇒
  multinational, startup, English-mediated role. Bilingual delivery is a real
  practice only in VN (VN+EN for multinational targets); elsewhere ship one
  version matching the ad.
- **Expected/last-drawn salary and notice period** are commonly *requested*
  fields (SG, TH, MY, VN, PH) — include only when the posting or the local
  template asks. TH templates carry it as a standard field of the personal
  block; a Western reader would not expect that.
- **Local job boards outrank LinkedIn everywhere except Singapore.** LinkedIn
  is mid-to-senior and multinational-facing in ID/MY/PH/VN/TH (VN
  penetration ≈ 10% of population, end-2025); it is a primary channel in SG.

## Indonesia

- Photo: « pas foto » still customary, decisively so for printed packets,
  government and BUMN (state-owned) recruitment; optional/de-emphasised in
  private tech and startups. Print sizes 3×4 cm or 4×6 cm; red or blue
  background survives in government/BUMN practice, white/light elsewhere;
  formal attire. Sector, not seniority, decides. HIGH (norm) / MEDIUM
  (private-sector optionality).
- Personal data: the traditional block (tempat/tanggal lahir, jenis kelamin,
  agama, status pernikahan) is now actively discouraged by current local
  career sources, anchored to UU PDP 27/2022; NIK never. Government/BUMN
  portals may still demand the full biodata on their own forms — that is the
  form, not the CV. HIGH on the advice, MEDIUM on practice.
- Language: Bahasa Indonesia for local/government/BUMN, English for
  multinationals and startups. Dates DD Month YYYY (« 12 Agustus 2026 ») or
  DD/MM/YYYY — never MM/DD.
- Sections: Data Pribadi · Riwayat Pendidikan · Pengalaman Kerja ·
  Keahlian · Pelatihan · Bahasa · Hobi/Minat. The document is « Daftar
  Riwayat Hidup »; **« Surat Lamaran Kerja » remains a separate expected
  cover letter**, not merged into the CV as in the US. Hobbies are still
  normal here — closer to France than to the US/UK.
- Protected titles: « Ir. » (Insinyur) is a *professional* title, not a degree
  outcome — UU 11/2014 Keinsinyuran requires the Program Profesi Insinyur
  plus **STRI** registration, administered by **PII** (Persatuan Insinyur
  Indonesia). Accountants: « Ak. »/CA via **IAI**, public accountants
  (CPA Indonesia) via **IAPI**, with the Ministry of Finance
  « Akuntan Beregister » roll. HIGH.
- Credentials to gloss: SMA (academic upper secondary) / SMK (vocational
  upper secondary); D1–D3 diplomas; **D4 = Sarjana Terapan, applied
  bachelor's, treated as equivalent to S1**; S1 bachelor's, S2 master's,
  S3 doctorate. GPA is **IPK** on 4.0; honours bands « Dengan Pujian »
  (cum laude, ≥3.51), « Sangat Memuaskan », « Memuaskan ». Verification:
  **PDDikti** with PISN diploma numbering and the SatuDikti app
  (Kemdikbud/Kemdiktisaintek line of ministries — the ministry has been
  renamed more than once; cite the registry, not the ministry name).
- Channels: Jobstreet (dominant), Kalibrr, Glints (junior/tech), LinkedIn
  (mid-senior), TopKarir. WhatsApp follow-up is widely reported but was not
  verifiable in a citable source — LOW, label it if it matters.

## Malaysia

- Photo: genuinely contested — current guidance splits, which is itself the
  finding. Include only if the posting or portal template invites it or the
  employer is local/government-linked; omit for multinational tracks. MEDIUM.
- Personal data: DOB, gender, marital status were standard and are now read as
  outdated, especially by multinationals; IC/MyKad never (onboarding only).
  PDPA 2010 governs the employer's handling, so "leave it off" here is market
  convention rather than a statutory duty on the candidate — state it that
  way. Bumiputera/nationality status appears on some public-sector and
  scholarship-linked postings only. MEDIUM on that last point (thin sourcing).
- Language: English by default for private sector and multinationals — a
  stronger English default than Indonesia's; Bahasa Malaysia for
  government (sektor awam), GLCs and some local SMEs.
- Length: **2-3 pages is accepted for experienced hires**, 1 page for fresh
  graduates. This is the one market in the file that tolerates length; do not
  export it to the neighbours.
- **CGPA is a headline signal**, expected from fresh graduates and read as a
  credibility marker (3.5+ worth surfacing) — much more so than IPK in
  Indonesia. Structural convention: summary + key skills block at the top of
  page 1. References only when the posting asks.
- Protected titles: « Ir. » is conferred by the **Board of Engineers Malaysia
  (BEM)** under the Registration of Engineers Act 1967 (rev. 2015) after the
  Professional Interview; « Chartered Accountant (M) » / C.A.(M) by the
  **Malaysian Institute of Accountants (MIA)** under the Accountants Act
  1967, whose restriction on unauthorised use is statutory. HIGH.
- Credentials to gloss: SPM (upper-secondary exit, ~O-Level), STPM
  (pre-university, ~A-Level) or Matrikulasi (1-year alternative pathway),
  Diploma (MQF level 4), then bachelor's/master's/PhD under the **Malaysian
  Qualifications Framework (MQF)**. Verification: the **MQA** register;
  foreign-degree equivalency for civil-service purposes runs through **JPA**
  (MEDIUM — not re-verified against a primary source).

## Singapore

- **Closest of the six to Anglo-Saxon practice — read the UK/Ireland file for
  the baseline and only the deltas here.** Photo: discouraged, and heading
  for statute — TAFEP's Tripartite Guidelines tell employers to drop photo
  fields, and the **Workplace Fairness Act 2025** (passed Jan 2025, in force
  around 2027, larger employers first) makes requiring a photo without
  justification unlawful. Older SG résumé guides still describe a top-right
  photo; that is a fading norm, not current practice. HIGH on direction,
  MEDIUM on how many employers still expect one today.
- Personal data: TAFEP tells employers not to ask for age, gender, race,
  religion, marital/family status, disability, photo or NS liability — so
  volunteering them is off-convention. NRIC/FIN: PDPC's advisory guidelines
  say no NRIC is needed for a job application. HIGH.
- Language: English, **British spelling** (organised, optimisation) — same
  mechanics as the UK/Ireland file. MEDIUM-HIGH (consistent across local
  guides, no single authority). Length 1 page, 2 for senior. Sections are the
  standard Western set; nothing local to add.
- Protected titles: **Professional Engineers Board (PEB)** for "PE" under the
  Professional Engineers Act 1991 (the title carries the statutory Qualified
  Person role); Board of Architects; **SMC** for doctors; the legal profession
  under the Legal Profession Act / Ministry of Law with the Law Society as
  professional body; **ISCA** confers CA (Singapore) while **ACRA** is the
  corporate/audit regulator, not the individual-title regulator (MEDIUM on
  that split — verify before writing it into a header).
- Credentials to gloss: PSLE; GCE **O-Level** / **N-Level**; **A-Level**
  (junior college); **Polytechnic Diploma**; **ITE Nitec / Higher Nitec**;
  **WSQ / SkillsFuture** for adult credentials; local honours classification.
  Verification: **OpenCerts** (GovTech, blockchain-backed) covers N/O/A-Level,
  ITE, polytechnic and WSQ certificates.
- Channels: **MyCareersFuture** (WSG's government portal — and the mandatory
  advertising channel for ≥14 days before an Employment Pass/S Pass hire
  under the Fair Consideration Framework, which is why it matters even to a
  foreign candidate), JobStreet, LinkedIn (primary here). Multinationals
  screen through mainstream ATS, so `references/ats.md` binds hardest in this
  market of the six.
- Work-pass eligibility: a foreign candidate's CV sometimes carries a pass
  line. EP qualifying-salary thresholds change yearly and scale with age and
  sector — **never quote a figure from this file or from memory**; check MOM's
  current page.

## Philippines

- **Paper: the exception in this file.** "Short bond paper" (8.5×11 in, i.e.
  us-letter) is the historical and still-common print norm; a4 is
  increasingly accepted for soft copies. MEDIUM — genuinely mixed, no
  standards authority. Ask, or ship letter for print.
- Photo: a **2×2 in colour ID photo** (white/light background, formal attire)
  is still customary and expected — the opposite of Singapore, in the same
  file. HIGH.
- Personal data: the traditional biodata block was age, birthdate, civil
  status, height/weight, religion and government ID numbers. Current advice
  drops religion, height/weight, parents' names and SSS/PhilHealth/TIN, while
  birthdate and civil status persist in practice. DPA 2012 is the governing
  law (MEDIUM — no CV-specific guidance located). **Character References with
  names and contact details** remain a genuinely local expected section.
- Language: English, **American spelling** — the only market in this file that
  is explicitly US-variant; Filipino/Tagalog is used in job-seeking guidance,
  not in the CV itself. HIGH.
- Length: 1 page fresh graduate, 2 pages at 5+ years — the multi-page
  reputation belongs to the government form, not the private-sector résumé.
- Local sections: "Seminars and Trainings", "Eligibility" / Civil Service
  Eligibility, "Character References", "willing to relocate".
- **CS Form 212 (Personal Data Sheet, rev. 2025, Civil Service Commission)**
  is a mandatory separate form for government employment: passport-size photo
  (4.5×3.5 cm, taken within 6 months, not photocopied or generated, name tag
  and signature over the photo) **plus the applicant's right thumbmark and
  signature**. It is a form, not a CV — do not let its fields leak into the
  résumé, and do not try to reproduce it in Typst. HIGH.
- Protected titles: the **Professional Regulation Commission (PRC)** is a
  single umbrella regulator for most licensed professions — "Engr.", CPA,
  "RN" and the rest come from board licensure. Two things matter on a CV:
  **passing the board exam is not the same as holding a current licence**
  (renewal required, checkable via PRC's LERIS), and the licence number is
  customarily listed for regulated professions (MEDIUM-HIGH — customary, no
  canonical mandate found). **Lawyers are the exception**: "Atty." sits with
  the Supreme Court / Integrated Bar, not the PRC (LOW-MEDIUM — verify).
- Credentials to gloss: K-12 with **Senior High School strands** (Academic:
  STEM, ABM, HUMSS, GAS; or TVL technical-vocational); **TESDA National
  Certificates NC I-IV** (competency tiers); **CHED**-recognised degree
  programmes; Latin honours are a normal CV credential (MEDIUM). Verification:
  CHED for programmes, TESDA registry for NCs, PRC/LERIS for licences.
- Channels: JobStreet PH (dominant by volume), Kalibrr (graduate hiring),
  Indeed, LinkedIn (experienced/managerial). **NBI clearance** is an
  onboarding document, not a CV item.

## Vietnam

- **Two documents, never conflated.** The modern **CV** carries an optional
  informal headshot and ordinary contact data. The **sơ yếu lý lịch** is a
  standardised self-declared biographical form **certified/stamped by the
  ward or commune People's Committee** — no equivalent anywhere else in this
  file — and is still commonly demanded inside the paper « hồ sơ xin việc »
  dossier by state-sector employers and SOEs, alongside a health certificate
  and copies of ID and diplomas. Private and FDI employers increasingly ask
  for the CV alone. HIGH on the distinction, MEDIUM on who still demands the
  form.
- The form's field list is the personal-data sink: gender, DOB, place of
  birth, **quê quán (ancestral hometown, a distinct field from current
  residence)**, permanent residence, CCCD number, ethnicity, religion,
  marital status, and organisation membership dates. Keep all of it off the
  CV. Note the **sổ hộ khẩu household-registration booklet was abolished
  1 Jan 2023** (data moved to the national population database) even though
  forms still print a « hộ khẩu thường trú » field. HIGH.
- Data protection: **Law 91/2025/QH15 (PDPL)**, effective 1 Jan 2026, with
  implementing Decree 356/2025, replaces Decree 13/2023. No CV-specific
  guidance has emerged yet — say so rather than inferring one. HIGH on the
  instrument, LOW on CV consequences.
- Language: Vietnamese for local/government/SOE, English for FDI and
  multinationals; **bilingual VN+EN is a real practice** for multinational
  targets. Gregorian calendar throughout, DD/MM/YYYY. MEDIUM.
- Sections: Thông tin cá nhân · Mục tiêu nghề nghiệp · Kinh nghiệm làm việc ·
  Học vấn (Trình độ học vấn) · Kỹ năng · Hoạt động/Chứng chỉ ·
  Người tham chiếu.
- Protected titles / practice certificates are **ministry-issued per sector**,
  not council-issued: construction practice certificate (chứng chỉ hành nghề
  xây dựng) from the Ministry of Construction (rank I) or provincial
  department (ranks II-III), required of project managers, chief designers and
  supervisors — **and reportedly slated for abolition in a 2025 policy push,
  so verify its status before writing it**; medical practice certificate
  (CCHN KCB) under the Ministry of Health per Law 15/2023; a **separate**
  pharmacy certificate (CCHND); the legal profession via a Ministry of
  Justice certificate plus admission to a provincial bar under the national
  lawyers' federation; CPA via the Ministry of Finance with VACPA as the
  professional body (MEDIUM — not re-verified).
- Credentials to gloss: THPT (upper-secondary diploma) → trung cấp /
  cao đẳng (vocational / college) → **Cử nhân** bachelor's, with **Kỹ sư**
  as the engineering-degree title → **Thạc sĩ** master's → **Tiến sĩ**
  doctorate. Grades run on a **10-point scale** (a 4.0 scale is also used with
  its own bands) — a bare "8.2" will be misread by a foreign ATS or reader, so
  always mark the scale. MOET sets policy; **VN-NARIC** handles
  foreign-credential recognition.
- Channels: VietnamWorks (dominant), TopCV, CareerBuilder.vn, CareerViet,
  JobsGO. LinkedIn is growing fast but ≈10% population penetration
  (end-2025). Facebook recruitment groups are heavily used (MEDIUM — general
  knowledge, not re-verified).

## Thailand

- Photo: a **formal studio headshot is standard and expected**, top-right;
  dark suit or formal attire, neutral background. Omitting it is noticed.
  Same mechanics as the DACH file, stronger expectation. HIGH.
- Personal data: DOB, nationality, religion, marital status and — for male
  applicants — **military service status** (Thailand conscripts; completion or
  exemption is commonly stated) are customary on the CV. **But** current
  PDPA-compliance guidance tells employers not to *request* marital status,
  religion (sensitive data needing explicit consent) or family plans, to issue
  a privacy notice, and to delete unsuccessful applicants' data within six
  months. **Custom (candidate volunteers) and compliance (employer must not
  ask) genuinely diverge here** — flag it to the user rather than resolving it
  silently; the candidate decides. HIGH on both halves.
- **Buddhist Era dates (พ.ศ. / B.E. = CE + 543)** appear on Thai-language
  transcripts, certificates and official forms. Any BE date on a document fed
  into a foreign-facing CV must be converted to CE (or annotated both ways) —
  otherwise a graduation year lands 543 years off and an ATS or reader
  misdates the whole education block. This is the single most important
  non-obvious convention in this file. HIGH.
- Language: Thai for domestic and government employers, English for
  multinationals and foreign-owned firms. HIGH.
- Sections: ประวัติส่วนตัว (personal profile) · จุดมุ่งหมายในงาน /
  เป้าหมายอาชีพ (objective) · ประวัติการทำงาน · ประวัติการศึกษา ·
  ทักษะและความสามารถพิเศษ · บุคคลที่สามารถติดต่อได้ (references). Real Thai
  templates put **เงินเดือนต้องการ (expected salary)** inside the personal
  block as a standard field. MEDIUM-HIGH (from actual local templates).
- Protected titles: the **Council of Engineers Thailand (COE / กว.)** under
  the Engineer Act B.E. 2542 (1999) licenses controlled engineering work, and
  foreigners are generally barred from practising as licensed engineers or
  architects — relevant to a foreign candidate's title choice, not just to
  wording. Council of Architects, Medical Council of Thailand, Lawyers
  Council of Thailand and TFAC (accountants) exist analogously but were not
  re-verified (MEDIUM-LOW).
- Credentials to gloss: **Mathayom 6 (M.6)** upper-secondary, or
  **ปวช. (Por Wor Chor)** vocational certificate, both ≈ high-school
  completion; **ปวส. (Por Wor Sor)** higher vocational diploma; bachelor's;
  **GPAX** cumulative GPA on 4.0 (admissions run through TCAS). Registry:
  **MHESI** (Office of the Permanent Secretary) for recognised institutions
  and programmes, **ONESQA** for quality-assessment reports.
- Channels: JobsDB and JobThai dominate; LinkedIn is supplementary,
  networking- and recruiter-facing (MEDIUM on the ranking, no penetration
  figure found).
- Paper: a4 assumed from regional/ISO practice but **not verified in a
  Thailand-specific source — LOW-MEDIUM, label the assumption**.
- Legacy conventions (nickname ชื่อเล่น as a CV field; height/weight/blood
  type on old-style forms) could not be confirmed as current: do not assert
  either. Transcript attachment is commonly expected for entry-level,
  government and university-linked applications (MEDIUM-LOW).
