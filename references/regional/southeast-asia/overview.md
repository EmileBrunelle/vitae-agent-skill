# Market: Southeast Asia — overview (ID · MY · SG · PH · VN · TH)

One file per country in this folder: [`indonesia.md`](indonesia.md), [`malaysia.md`](malaysia.md), [`singapore.md`](singapore.md), [`philippines.md`](philippines.md), [`vietnam.md`](vietnam.md), [`thailand.md`](thailand.md). Read the target country's file; read this overview only for the cross-country comparison.

Six markets that are *not* a bloc. Verified country by country (current sources, 2025-2026); the divergences below are larger than the ones between France and the UK. **Never carry a finding from one country to its neighbour** — Singapore is close to Anglo-Saxon practice while Thailand expects a formal studio photo, and both are in this region.

Shared across all six: paper **a4** (exception: the Philippines, see [`philippines.md`](philippines.md); and Thailand's a4 is inferred, not verified), and reverse-chronological order everywhere. Every regulated-title claim here is the known case, never the exhaustive list — the universal rule in [`regional.md`](../../regional.md) still applies.

## Cross-cutting patterns (then read the country section)

- **Photo splits three ways, not two**: expected/customary (ID printed & public sector, PH, TH), contested/check the posting (MY, VN modern CV), discouraged and heading for statute (SG). Where a photo is expected the mechanics are the same as [`dach.md`](../dach.md) (formal, front-facing, plain background) — the difference is that ID and PH also specify a *print size*.
- **Personal-data culture is generally richer than in Europe and is contracting**, driven by recent data-protection statutes (ID UU PDP 27/2022; MY PDPA 2010; SG PDPA + TAFEP/WFA; PH DPA 2012; VN Law 91/2025 PDPL in force 1 Jan 2026, replacing Decree 13/2023; TH PDPA). Two of these regulate what the *employer* may collect, not what the candidate may volunteer (MY, TH explicitly) — so custom still runs ahead of the statute. Default: omit, and tell the user the local custom (universal rule).
- **A separate administrative form is often the real personal-data sink**: ID « surat lamaran » + KTP/ijazah scans, PH CS Form 212, VN sơ yếu lý lịch. Keep those fields off the CV; they belong to the form.
- **National ID numbers never go on the CV** in any of the six (ID NIK/KTP, MY IC/MyKad, SG NRIC/FIN, PH SSS/TIN/PhilHealth, VN CCCD, TH ID) — identity-fraud risk, and SG's PDPC advisory says a job application needs no NRIC at all. HIGH confidence.
- **Language rule that holds in all six: match the language of the posting.** Local language ⇒ local employer, government, SOE/GLC; English ⇒ multinational, startup, English-mediated role. Bilingual delivery is a real practice only in VN (VN+EN for multinational targets); elsewhere ship one version matching the ad.
- **Expected/last-drawn salary and notice period** are commonly *requested* fields (SG, TH, MY, VN, PH) — include only when the posting or the local template asks. TH templates carry it as a standard field of the personal block; a Western reader would not expect that.
- **Local job boards outrank LinkedIn everywhere except Singapore.** LinkedIn is mid-to-senior and multinational-facing in ID/MY/PH/VN/TH (VN penetration ≈ 10% of population, end-2025); it is a primary channel in SG.
