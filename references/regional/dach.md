# Market: Germany / Austria / Switzerland (DACH)

Paper: a4. Length: 1-2 pages plus an attachments culture (certificates,
references — « Zeugnisse » — often expected with the application).

- Photo: legally optional since AGG (2006), but still culturally expected by
  roughly two-thirds of employers outside tech — ask the candidate's target
  sector before omitting.
- Birth date: still commonly included.
- Style: tabular CV (Lebenslauf), noun phrases rather than verb-first bullets.
- Protected titles: "Ingenieur" is legally protected (degree-based). Many
  other titles regulated — verify per profession.
