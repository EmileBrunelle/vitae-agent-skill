# Market: UK / Ireland

Paper: a4. Photo: never. Age/marital: never.

- Length: 1 page early-career (under ~5 years), 2 pages standard beyond.
- Bullets: past tense; current role in present tense.
- Spelling: British throughout (organised, analysed, optimisation) — a US
  spelling on a UK CV reads as carelessness.
- Titles: "engineer" generally unregulated for software; chartered
  designations (CEng, ACA…) and solicitor/barrister remain regulated —
  check per field.
- "CV" is the standard word (not "résumé").
