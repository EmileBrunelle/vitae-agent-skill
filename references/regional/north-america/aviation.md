# Regulated professions: pilot licences, permits, certificates and ratings (Canada, USA)

Loaded on demand when the CV lists a pilot credential. Print it as [`../../certifications/rules.md`](../../certifications/rules.md) says (what the document shows; validity). A pilot credential is a government licence, so it is filed here, not under certifications. Each fact is what the regulator's page or text says (consulted 2026-11-07).

## Canada (Transport Canada, "TC")

- Names, as Transport Canada lists them: "Student Pilot Permit", "Pilot Permit Ultra-light Aeroplane", "Pilot Permit Gyroplane", "Pilot Permit Recreational Aeroplane", "Pilot Licence - Glider", "Pilot Licence - Balloon", "Private Pilot Licence - Aeroplane" and "- Helicopter", "Commercial Pilot Licence - Aeroplane" and "- Helicopter", "Airline Transport Pilot Licence - Aeroplane" and "- Helicopter", "Flight Engineer Licence" (Transport Canada, « General information on pilot licences and permits », consulted 2026-11-07, S438). The qualifications page words some of them differently ("Gyroplane Pilot Permit", "Glider Pilot Licence", "Balloon Licence"; Transport Canada, « Qualifications for pilot licences and permits », consulted 2026-11-07, S439): copy the word and the category from the document.
- Ratings are endorsed on the permit or licence. TC's pages name aeroplane class ratings, "Individual Aircraft Type Ratings" shown by "an aircraft type designator endorsed on the permit or licence", and, on the qualifications page, Night, Land, Sea, Multi Engine, Instrument, VFR Over the Top, Type, Second Officer, Aerobatic (Class II, I) and Flight Instructor (Class IV to I) ratings (Transport Canada, « General information on pilot licences and permits », consulted 2026-11-07, S438; Transport Canada, « Qualifications for pilot licences and permits », consulted 2026-11-07, S439). Print ratings as endorsed, with class or type designator.
- Helicopter type ratings: "Each new type of helicopter requires its own type rating"; where a helicopter's flight crew requirement is two pilots, the pilot-in-command must have a valid ATPL-Helicopter (Transport Canada, « General information on pilot licences and permits », consulted 2026-11-07, S438).
- The medical certificate is separate: TC says medical fitness is normally established by an examination by a Civil Aviation Medical Examiner (a "Civil Aviation Medical Declaration" for glider, ultra-light and recreational), and "the valid to date is calculated to the first day of the month following the date of medical or declaration and it is entered on the permit or licence in the format 'year/month/day'" (Transport Canada, « General information on pilot licences and permits », consulted 2026-11-07, S438).
- Applicants "may be credited with flying experience recorded in a personal log book or other certified record of flying experience" (Transport Canada, « General information on pilot licences and permits », consulted 2026-11-07, S438); print hours as that record shows.
- A foreign licence does not make a Canadian licence holder: a "Foreign Licence Validation Certificate" (for an applicant who permanently resides outside Canada) is "normally valid for a period of one year subject to the validity of the foreign licence" or the Canadian medical, and a "verification letter" confirms authenticity; TC says they differ in purpose and validity (Transport Canada, « General information on pilot licences and permits », consulted 2026-11-07, S438; Transport Canada, « Get a licence verification letter », consulted 2026-11-07, S440). Converting an FAA certificate to a Transport Canada licence (or back) goes through the US-Canada agreement (IPL), which covers the private, commercial and airline transport levels (aeroplane, rotorcraft-helicopter) and type, class and instrument ratings (Transport Canada, « Converting to a U.S. or Canadian pilot licence », consulted 2026-11-07, S441). Print "Transport Canada licence" only if issued.

## USA (FAA)

- 14 CFR 61.5 lists the pilot certificates: student pilot, sport pilot, recreational pilot, private pilot, commercial pilot, airline transport pilot; plus flight instructor and ground instructor certificates (eCFR (US), « 14 CFR 61.5, Certificates and ratings issued under this part », consulted 2026-11-07, S442). Ratings are separate items placed on the pilot certificate (other than student pilot): aircraft category (airplane, rotorcraft, glider, lighter-than-air, powered-lift, powered parachute, weight-shift-control), airplane class (single- or multiengine, land or sea), rotorcraft class (helicopter, gyroplane), aircraft type, instrument ratings (airplane, helicopter, powered-lift; on private and commercial certificates only) and a second-in-command pilot type rating (eCFR (US), « 14 CFR 61.5, Certificates and ratings issued under this part », consulted 2026-11-07, S442).
- Print certificate and ratings as two things, ratings as on the certificate; no source prescribes a CV format.
- Duration: a pilot certificate "is issued without an expiration date"; a flight instructor certificate issued on or after December 1, 2024 is also issued without one (earlier ones expire 24 calendar months from issue or renewal); the privileges of a certificate based on a foreign licence last only while that foreign licence is effective (eCFR (US), « 14 CFR 61.19, Duration of pilot and instructor certificates and privileges », consulted 2026-11-07, S443).
- The medical certificate is a separate document under 14 CFR part 67. A required pilot flight crewmember must hold it, with listed exceptions (for example glider, balloon, some sport-pilot privileges); the class depends on the privilege exercised: first class for airline transport pilot-in-command privileges, at least second class for commercial privileges (other than balloon or glider), at least third class for private, recreational and student privileges, except under the conditions of 14 CFR 61.113(i) (eCFR (US), « 14 CFR 61.3, Requirement for certificates, ratings, privileges, and authorizations », consulted 2026-11-07, S444; eCFR (US), « 14 CFR 61.23, Medical certificates: Requirement and duration », consulted 2026-11-07, S445). Print the medical class and date from the document only.

## Print

- Wording (suggested, not from a source): "Private Pilot Licence - Aeroplane (Transport Canada), night and instrument ratings; Category 3 medical valid to 2027/06/01" or "FAA commercial pilot certificate, airplane multiengine land, instrument airplane; second-class medical".
- ICAO: Transport Canada says its standards are "in most respects, in accordance with ICAO Standards and Recommended Practices" and that interchangeability between states "has not yet been achieved" (Transport Canada, « General information on pilot licences and permits », consulted 2026-11-07, S438); the pages name no "ICAO licence" document: print the licence of the issuing state.
