# Health professions: United States (state licences)

Read IN ADDITION to [`../../certifications/health.md`](../../certifications/health.md) (decision rule: exam passed, licence held, certification) when the CV is for a regulated health profession in the US: state nursing licence, NCLEX, Nurse Licensure Compact. Certifications (AHA, AACN CCRN, NREMT, PTCB): same file; NREMT is tied to state authorisation in some states, and its reviewed sentence is there. Each fact is what the body's own page says (consulted 2026-11-07) and holds for that body only. Not read: state boards' own pages, Nursys's own site (blocked).

## Nursing licence
- Passing the NCLEX is one requirement, not the licence: a licence by exam is « issued by a state upon completion of all requirements including passing the NCLEX » (NLC, FAQs, S349). « Official exam results are available only from the nursing regulatory bodies », and each body has « its own eligibility requirements » to take the exam (NCSBN, NCLEX FAQs, S348). Write « Passed the NCLEX-RN » until a state licence is held; then « RN, [State] » with the state.
- Multistate: an RN or LPN/VN licence « issued by a home state licensing board that authorizes the licensed nurse to practice in all compact states »; a single-state licence is « valid for practice only in the state of issuance »; « You may only hold a compact license if you legally declare residency in a compact state ». The NLC covers « RN and LPN/VN licenses only »: an APRN needs an individual licence in each state (S349). Write « multistate (NLC) » only if the board shows it; the NLC points to Nursys « Quick Confirm » (free) to tell single from multistate, and calls Nursys « the national nurse database » of licensure and discipline (S349).
