## US federal jobs (USAJOBS)
- Since 27 September 2025 USAJOBS restricts résumés to 2 pages for Title 5 competitive and excepted service announcements: "If the only resume received by an applicant is longer than two pages, the applicant is ineligible for further consideration." (OPM, « Agency Guidance on the Two-Page Limit on Resume Length » (2025-09-12), consulted 2026-11-07, S148).
- Per job: job title and employer (series and grade for federal positions), start and end dates month/year (e.g. 05/2019 – 08/2022), hours per week. Education: school, completion date, degree type, cumulative GPA. Licences and certifications: type and date. LOW, unsourced.
- Positions needing a CV (medical, research) may take it as an "other document" (LOW, unsourced). The Merit Hiring Plan "prohibits the current longform Federal resume structure" (OPM, « Agency Guidance on the Two-Page Limit on Resume Length » (2025-09-12), consulted 2026-11-07, S148).
