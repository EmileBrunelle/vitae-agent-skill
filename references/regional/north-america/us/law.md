# Law: United States (bar admission, status)

Loaded on demand when the candidate lists a JD, a bar exam, a bar admission or "Esq." for a US target. Read together with [`us/market.md`](market.md#protected-titles) (protected-title overview and the engineering example). Admission is state by state; only California's State Bar and the ABA Model Rule 5.5 were read, so write no other state's rule: say "check the state bar" and ask the candidate.

## Admitted in a state, not "US-licensed"
- ABA Model Rule 5.5 (b): « A lawyer who is not admitted to practice in this jurisdiction shall not: ... (2) hold out to the public or otherwise represent that the lawyer is admitted to practice law in this jurisdiction » (American Bar Association, « Model Rules of Professional Conduct, Rule 5.5: Unauthorized Practice of Law; Multijurisdictional Practice of Law » (page dated 2019-04-17), consulted 2026-11-07, S410). It is the ABA's model text (page dated 17 April 2019), not a state rule: the target state's own rule governs and was not checked.
- California Business and Professions Code § 6125: « No person shall practice law in California unless the person is an active licensee of the State Bar » (State of California, Legislative Counsel, « Business and Professions Code § 6125 », consulted 2026-11-07, S411).
- Rule for the skill (derived from the two texts above, not quoted): write the jurisdiction with the admission (example wording, the skill's: "Admitted to the New York bar, 2022"), never "US attorney", "licensed in the US" or an admission in a state the candidate is not admitted in. A candidate admitted in one state who targets another writes the state(s) where admitted and says nothing that suggests the target state.

## JD, exam passed, oath, admission (California facts)
- A JD is a degree; no source loaded makes it an admission. California: « Taking the attorney's oath is not just a ritual. It is required for admission to practice law in California » and the candidate « cannot be sworn in before your certification for admission has been accepted by the Supreme Court of California and without having received the required form » (State Bar of California, « Attorney’s Oath », consulted 2026-11-07, S412).
- The California Bar posts exam results and a pass list (State Bar of California, « California Bar Exam Results », consulted 2026-11-07, S413); a pass is not the admission step above. Print "Passed the [State] bar examination ([year]); admission pending" (suggested wording, the skill's) until the candidate's bar record shows admission (the oath page says the oath is required, not that it is the only step); then "Admitted to the [State] bar, [year]".
- Never "JD, Esq." to mean a graduate who is not admitted. For "Esq." itself no bar or court source was found that says who may use it: don't rule on it, don't add it on the candidate's behalf; copy what the candidate's own bar profile and employer use.

## Status: active, inactive, not eligible, resigned (California)
- The State Bar says: « Attorneys who are not practicing law and are otherwise eligible may file a form to transfer to inactive »; « There is no "retired" status »; « the only voluntary statuses are active, inactive, or resigned »; « If you simply stop paying annual fees, your license will be suspended » (State Bar of California, « Inactive and “Not Eligible to Practice” », consulted 2026-11-07, S414; State Bar of California, « Attorney Status Changes », consulted 2026-11-07, S415).
- « An attorney may be placed on administrative inactive status (not eligible to practice law) for failure to comply with the MCLE requirement » (State Bar of California, « Inactive and “Not Eligible to Practice” », consulted 2026-11-07, S414): "inactive" on the record can be voluntary or administrative; the CV must match the bar's record.
- Rule for the skill: print the status as the bar's search shows it today. An inactive, not-eligible or suspended attorney does not write "licensed attorney" as a current fact; "Retired attorney" is not a California status. Past admission, suggested wording (the skill's): "Admitted to the California bar, 2015; inactive status since 2024".
- Check: the State Bar offers a search by « Attorney Name or Bar Number » (State Bar of California, « Attorney Search », consulted 2026-11-07, S416). Printing the bar number on a CV: no source loaded addresses it; only if the candidate gives it.

## Not covered
- Other states, federal court admission, pro hac vice, limited licences and foreign-qualified lawyers: not loaded.
