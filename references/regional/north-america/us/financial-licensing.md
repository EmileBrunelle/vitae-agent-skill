# Regulated professions: United States (FINRA, NASAA, insurance)

Loaded on demand when the CV lists a FINRA registration or Series exam, a state securities licence, or a state insurance producer licence. Read together with [`../../certifications/rules.md`](../../../certifications/rules.md) (exam passed is not registered); designations (CPA, CFA, ACCA) are in [`../../certifications/finance-accounting.md`](../../../certifications/finance-accounting.md) and reserved professional titles in [`us/market.md`](market.md#protected-titles). Not read: US state insurance rules.

## United States
- FINRA: "To become registered, securities professionals must pass qualifying exams administered by FINRA" (FINRA, « Qualification Exams », consulted 2026-11-07, S385). After the SIE "the next step is becoming associated with a FINRA member firm and taking one of the qualification exams" (FINRA, « Securities Industry Essentials Exam (SIE) », consulted 2026-11-07, S386).
- Lapse: a General Securities Representative registration "will lapse two years from the date of termination on your Form U5" (FINRA, « Registration requirements FAQ », consulted 2026-11-07, S387). NASAA: "Passing an exam is a prerequisite to a license, not a license in and of itself"; after termination the individual "has two years to be re-employed and re-registered or the exam will show as ‘expired’ in the Central Registration Depository (CRD)", though a state may waive the re-take (NASAA, « Series 63/65/66 exam FAQ », consulted 2026-11-07, S388).
- Insurance: "State insurance regulators license producers" and anyone selling, soliciting or negotiating insurance "must be licensed as a ‘producer’" (NAIC, « Producer Licensing » (last updated 2025-02-10), consulted 2026-11-07, S389); NIPR: "Licensed individuals and entities can download a Producer Report to verify license data in any state" (NIPR, « Verify existing insurance licenses », consulted 2026-11-07, S390).
- Held now: "Registered representative (Series 7, 63), [firm], 2019–present".
