# Financial-services licences: United States (FINRA, NASAA, insurance)

Read IN ADDITION to [`../../certifications/rules.md`](../../../certifications/rules.md) (exam passed is not registered) when the CV lists a FINRA registration or Series exam, a state securities licence, or a state insurance producer licence. Designations (CPA, CFA, ACCA): [`../../certifications/finance-accounting.md`](../../../certifications/finance-accounting.md); reserved professional titles: [`us/market.md`](market.md#protected-titles). Not read: US state insurance rules.

## United States
- FINRA: "To become registered, securities professionals must pass qualifying exams administered by FINRA" (FINRA, « Qualification Exams », S385). After the SIE "the next step is becoming associated with a FINRA member firm and taking one of the qualification exams" (FINRA, « SIE », S386).
- Lapse: a General Securities Representative registration "will lapse two years from the date of termination on your Form U5" (FINRA, « Registration requirements FAQ », S387). NASAA: "Passing an exam is a prerequisite to a license, not a license in and of itself"; after termination the individual "has two years to be re-employed and re-registered or the exam will show as ‘expired’ in the Central Registration Depository (CRD)", though a state may waive the re-take (NASAA, Series 63/65/66 FAQ, S388).
- Insurance: "State insurance regulators license producers" and anyone selling, soliciting or negotiating insurance "must be licensed as a ‘producer’" (NAIC, « Producer Licensing », 2025-02-10, S389); NIPR: "Licensed individuals and entities can download a Producer Report to verify license data in any state" (NIPR, S390).
- Held now: "Registered representative (Series 7, 63), [firm], 2019–present".
