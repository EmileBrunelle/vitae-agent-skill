# Field pack: trades, United States

Load this file with [`fields/trades.md`](../../../fields/trades.md) when the trades CV targets US work (state licences, registered apprenticeship).

- The Certificate of Completion is issued by the Office of Apprenticeship or a State Apprenticeship Agency; copies and replacements come from them (S332).
- For electricians "Requirements vary by state" and most states require a licence; print a state licence only with its state and number (S333).
