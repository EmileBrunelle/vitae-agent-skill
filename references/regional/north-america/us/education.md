# Education: United States

Loaded on demand when the education section has credits, a part-time job beside studies, or an MBA. Generic rules: [`references/education-cases.md`](../../../education-cases.md); this file holds the US wordings and sources.

## Credits
- Print "US credit hours" as the unit and system.

## Return to studies with work alongside
- A US federal résumé requires hours per week per job (OPM, « Agency Guidance on the Two-Page Limit on Resume Length », 2025; see [`government.md`](government.md)).

## MBA and other professional master's
- Length: one page for a school résumé book (Wharton, « 2018 Resume Tips and Resume Book Process »; Smith, « The Smith MBA Resume », 2017: under eight years of experience); Booth: one page preferred, two understood (2025).
- Within Education, current degree first (Smith). Alumni with long experience usually list experience first (Kellogg, « Resumes and cover letters »).
- GMAT/GRE, GPA: include when the target asks (consulting, finance recruiting) or the score is strong, with its scale (Smith, US 4.0: GPA above 3.5, GMAT above 700; Wharton: GMAT >720, GPA >3.7).
