# Academic: Canada

Loaded on demand when the document is an academic CV for a Canadian target (read [`../quebec/academic.md`](../quebec/academic.md) too for Québec). Generic genre rules: [`references/fields/academic.md`](../../../fields/academic.md). *(LOW, unsourced for the section structure, supported by the specimen the field pack was built from; the agency facts below carry S ids.)*

- Funding (#7) and supervision (#8) are mandatory and the most scrutinized sections: they are what agencies count. Never fold them into a generic "other activities" section.
- Amounts in CAD, explicit role, full period.
- No personal data, no photo (same rule as industry mode).

## Federal agency formats
As of September 2026: NSERC, CIHR and SSHRC are moving gradually from the CCV to a narrative-style tri-agency CV, started in 2025, rolling out competition by competition (e.g. NSERC Discovery Horizons 2027) (CIHR, « Transition to the Tri-agency CV », consulted 2026-11-07, S35; SSHRC, « Tri-agency CV », consulted 2026-11-07, S36; NSERC, « NSERC to introduce the tri-agency CV for two funding opportunities », consulted 2026-11-07, S37).
