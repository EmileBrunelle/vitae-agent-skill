## Protected titles
- "Engineer" / « ingénieur » and designations ("P.Eng.", « ing. ») are reserved by provincial engineering acts in ALL provinces — OIQ (Québec), PEO (Ontario), EGBC (BC), APEGA (Alberta), etc. Degree names are always legal (B.Eng., B. Ing.).
- "Software engineer" as a job title: enforcement varies by province, mostly regulator cease-and-desist letters, rarely court-tested. Safest default: "software developer" (title in the CV's language) unless the employer's own posting uses the engineer title and the role is in the USA (see the USA line in [`us/market.md`](../us/market.md#protected-titles)). In Canada the title requires a licence regardless of the employer: the 12 Canadian engineering regulators restrict "software engineer" and similar IT titles to licensed engineers (Engineers Canada, « Engineering regulators reiterate licensure requirements for those using “software engineer” and other IT titles » (2022), consulted 2026-11-07, S5), and the OIQ says an employer-given title is still an offence.
- Lawyer, paralegal, notary, teacher: [`law.md`](law.md) and [`teaching.md`](teaching.md) here, [`../quebec/law.md`](../quebec/law.md) and [`../quebec/teaching.md`](../quebec/teaching.md) for Québec.
