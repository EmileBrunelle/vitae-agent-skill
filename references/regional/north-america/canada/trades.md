# Field pack: trades, Canada outside Québec

Load this file with [`fields/trades.md`](../../../fields/trades.md) when the trades CV targets a Canadian province or territory other than Québec, or lists a Red Seal endorsement. Québec: [`quebec/trades.md`](../quebec/trades.md).

## Provinces and Red Seal

- Provinces and territories certify trades; Red Seal is an endorsement on their certificate, not a certification by itself (S323).
- The Red Seal endorsement is "affixed to a provincial or territorial certificate of qualification" ("mention Sceau rouge" on the "certificat d'aptitude professionnelle"); it "indicates only that a tradesperson has passed the Red Seal Exam for a trade" and "does not indicate that a person is certified by a province or territory to carry on any trade" (S323, S324).
- Print "Certificate of qualification, <trade>, Red Seal endorsement, <province>". "RSE" after a name "should only be used by journeypersons who have obtained a Red Seal endorsement on their provincial or territorial trade certificate"; anyone using it "should have a trade certificate with the Red Seal endorsement available for verification purposes" (S323).
- Verify with the provincial or territorial apprenticeship authority that issued it (S323). Not every trade is Red Seal: the CCQ speaks of "une liste de métiers et occupations" designated (S322).

## Ontario

- Ontario: in compulsory trades a Certificate of Qualification "is needed to work legally in Ontario and needs to be renewed at regular intervals"; in non-compulsory trades it does not need renewing (S330). A provisional certificate or an apprentice's registered training agreement also authorizes the work; the Skilled Trades Ontario public register is for verification but does not list every authorized person (S331).

## Interprovincial mobility

- Hours in a recognised Canadian apprenticeship scheme outside Québec fall under the interprovincial apprentice mobility agreement, which allows the combined work and training hours to be recognised at 100 %; keep the home authority's progress document (S318).
