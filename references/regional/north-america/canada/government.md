## Government of Canada jobs (GC Jobs)
- The résumé is pasted unformatted ("no bullets, underlines or bold") into the GC Jobs profile, and the screening questions decide: give details and examples, "Use the exact keywords and skills from the screening question in your answer. Do not use synonyms.", and with "and" demonstrate every element (Public Service Commission of Canada, « Applying for Government of Canada jobs: How to apply » (2026-09-28), consulted 2026-11-07, S149). Formatting is stripped on paste.
