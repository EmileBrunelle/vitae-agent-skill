## Canada (CIRO, CSA)
- « Registered and approved with CIRO » is required for individuals dealing or advising through a member Investment Dealer or Mutual Fund Dealer; « must be sponsored by a firm that is a CIRO member » (CIRO, « Becoming a registered individual », snapshot 2026-05-04, S382).
- Canadian Securities Course (CSC): CSI lists it as a way to "Meet proficiency requirements to deal in Mutual Funds" and says "This course is no longer acceptable for the purposes of CIRO approval with an Investment Dealer" since 2026-01-01 (CSI, CSC page, consulted 2026-11-07, S383). Registration also needs a sponsoring firm (S382): the course is not registration. Print "Completed the Canadian Securities Course" with year, never "CIRO registered".
- CSA: "anyone in the business of trading securities or advising clients on securities must be registered with the securities regulator in each province or territory where they do business, unless an exemption applies"; the National Registration Search covers individuals and firms registered with these authorities (Canadian Securities Administrators, « Registration », snapshot 2026-01-14, S384).
