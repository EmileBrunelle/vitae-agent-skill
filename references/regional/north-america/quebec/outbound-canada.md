# Outbound: Québec credentials, English Canada

Loaded on demand when a candidate with Québec credentials targets English Canada. Read together with [`outbound-common.md`](outbound-common.md), which still applies.

- An OIQ licence is not valid elsewhere: each province needs its own licence, though the Canadian Free Trade Agreement makes transfer largely automatic (Engineers Canada, « Mobility across Canada », consulted 2026-11-07, S6).

## Degree length: identify the path first
- Identify which path led to the bachelor's before writing the education line; never assume. Usually the CV already shows it: a listed DEC and its program name; read it there. Ask only when no DEC is listed (omitted, or path C) or the DEC's type is unclear. Paths (convention, LOW, unsourced): (A) 2-year pre-university DEC, then a 3-year bachelor's; (B) 3-year technical DEC, then a bachelor's; (C) no DEC (adult/experience admission). UQAM's admission bases are « Base DEC » and « Base Expérience » (UQAM, « Bases d'admissibilité 1er cycle », consulted 2026-11-08, S475). A genuinely 4-year Québec bachelor's (e.g. engineering): print "4-year" with the degree, no further sentence.
- Never print the Québec credit count or "3-year" alone. A Québec credit is 45 hours of work at Laval (Université Laval, « Règlement des études », consulted 2026-11-08, S493), UQAM (UQAM, « Règlement no 5, Règlement des études de premier cycle », consulted 2026-11-07, S129), HEC Montréal (HEC Montréal, « Règlement pédagogique », consulted 2026-11-08, S495), Université de Montréal (Université de Montréal, Secrétariat général, « Règlement des études de premier cycle », consulted 2026-11-08, S505) ("Un crédit représente 45 heures" / "quarante-cinq heures d'activités d'apprentissage, y compris l'évaluation" / "Le crédit représente 45 heures" / "Le crédit représente 45 heures consacrées par un étudiant à l’atteinte des objectifs d’un cours"), "normally" 45 at Sherbrooke (Université de Sherbrooke, « Règlement des études, 1. Introduction », consulted 2026-11-08, S476) and "environ" 45 at UQAR (UQAR, « Règlement 5 », consulted 2026-11-08, S494); no Québec-wide text exists, so name the institution. Ontario schools publish a 90-credit 3-year general degree beside the 120-credit 4-year one (York Urban Studies (York University (Faculty of LA&PS, Urban Studies), « Degree options & requirements », consulted 2026-11-08, S477), uOttawa (University of Ottawa, « Regulations », consulted 2026-11-08, S478), Nipissing (Nipissing University, « Common degree structure », consulted 2026-11-08, S479)). That a Québec "90 credits / 3 years" reads as that general degree is inference (LOW, unsourced). Three-year computer science degrees exist in Ontario: York (BA and BSc, "usually completed in three years", at least 90 credits, declared major in computer science, 2026-2027 calendar) (York University, « 2026-2027 Undergraduate Academic Calendar, Lassonde School of Engineering, degree programs », consulted 2026-11-08, S496); Laurentian ("Three-year programs lead to a Bachelor of Arts or Science in Computer Science", Total 90 credits) (Laurentian University, « Computer Science », consulted 2026-11-08, S497); Trent (BSc General, 15.0 credits to graduate; the calendar gives no year count) (Trent University, « Undergraduate Academic Calendar 2025-2026 », consulted 2026-11-08, S498); Western ("3 Year BSc", "Major in Computer Science", 7.0 courses at Western for Fanshawe Computer Programmer Analyst diploma holders, 2024 articulation sheet) (Western University, Faculty of Science, « Articulation Agreement Degree Completion Checklist, Fanshawe, Computer Programmer Analyst, 3 Year BSc Major in Computer Science », consulted 2026-11-08, S499). Cover-letter use: "such degrees exist at York, Laurentian, Trent, Western"; it does not make a Québec degree equivalent.
- Never write "equivalent to a four-year degree" (see `outbound-common.md`): no ministry or regulator states it.

### A. Pre-university DEC + 3-year bachelor's
- Only when the posting or form requires four years (mirror its wording): `3-year program following the 2-year pre-university DEC (CEGEP); 16 years of study, as for a four-year degree after Grade 12`. Arithmetic: Québec students "complete 13 years of study before starting university" vs 12 elsewhere (Concordia University, « Program length », consulted 2026-11-08, S480); the bachelor's is then three years (CICIC (Gov. of Canada), « Postsecondary education in Quebec », consulted 2026-11-08, S481). Candidate confirms the path first. Otherwise degree name only.
- List the DEC as its own line: `Diploma of College Studies (DEC), Pre-University, <CEGEP>` — it carries the arithmetic. Glossing: [`outbound-common.md`](outbound-common.md).
- Cover letter or form comment (never CV padding): "Some programs state it (Carleton's MSW: « CEGEP and a university degree, which is usually 3 years, is considered equivalent to a 4 year degree » (Carleton University, School of Social Work, « MSW FAQs », consulted 2026-11-08, S482); U of T MScAC: Québec applicants "will qualify with a two-year CEGEP and a three-year bachelor's degree", eligibility decided on a complete application (University of Toronto, MScAC (Master of Science in Applied Computing), « Apply », consulted 2026-11-08, S483))." Name only the programs the candidate applies to or cites; no generalisation (York FGS requires "an honours degree or equivalent" and is silent on Québec (York University, Faculty of Graduate Studies, « Entry requirements », consulted 2026-11-08, S484); UBC graduate studies' Canada page says « From Quebec's French universities, a three-year Bachelor's degree is acceptable provided the Diplome d'Etudes Collegiales (DEC) has been previously awarded » (University of British Columbia, Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies, « Canada », consulted 2026-11-08, S570): a whole-graduate-school statement, French universities only, not a statement on English-language ones). Do not argue from credits (a Québec credit is normally 45 h; other systems differ: "90 = 90" is coincidence). Do not say the DEC "is first-year university" (rule; the transfer-credit facts below are not a year-level equivalence). Institution-specific facts that support the arithmetic (cover letter, name the institution the candidate applies to, never "X recognizes your degree as 4 years"): Concordia: "Quebec CEGEP graduates complete 13 years of study before starting university", CEGEP applicants: three years (90 credits) in many programs, longer for exceptions (all Engineering, Early Childhood and Elementary Education, TESL, Art Education, Exercise Science/Athletic Therapy) (Concordia University, « Program length », consulted 2026-11-08, S480); McGill Science (SOUSA): CEGEP DEC gives U1 and 30 credits of advanced standing, "You will be in U1 if you have been admitted with 24 or more credits of advanced standing" (McGill University, Science Office for Undergraduate Student Advising (SOUSA), « Advanced standing & transfer credit », consulted 2026-11-08, S500); UBC Vancouver: transfer credit "for up to one full year of study" for a DEC (UBC Vancouver Academic Calendar, « Applicants from a CEGEP », consulted 2026-11-08, S501); Waterloo: up to 10 units (two years) of transfer credit, course by course (70% or better), if admitted to an honours or four-year general program, and program-dependent (University of Waterloo, « Transfer credits for CEGEP students », consulted 2026-11-08, S502). These are credit-transfer rules for incoming students, not degree-level equivalence.

### B. Technical DEC (3 years) + bachelor's
- Facts: the technical DEC is its own credential, "generally lasts three years" (Gouvernement du Québec, « Education system », consulted 2026-11-08, S485); it includes the general-education courses (Gouvernement du Québec, « Étudier au cégep », consulted 2026-11-08, S486); universities may add make-up math (UQAM: MAT0341 « hors programme », first term (UQAM, « Baccalauréat en informatique et génie logiciel (7316) », consulted 2026-11-08, S487); Laval: MAT-0150/0260/0130 at the start (Université Laval, « Entente DEC-BAC: Baccalauréat en informatique / 420.B0 Techniques de l'informatique, Cégep Édouard-Montpetit », consulted 2026-11-08, S488)); a DEC-BAC passerelle credits part of the DEC and shortens the bachelor's (Laval 420.B0: 24 to 27 credits, DEC no older than 5 years (Université Laval, « Entente DEC-BAC: Baccalauréat en informatique / 420.B0 Techniques de l'informatique, Cégep Édouard-Montpetit », consulted 2026-11-08, S488); UQAM (UQAM, « Baccalauréat en informatique et génie logiciel (7316) », consulted 2026-11-08, S487)).
- No source gives a total length or equivalence for this path: no arithmetic line, no equivalence line (the 13-year argument of A does not apply).
- CV: the DEC on its own line with length and field: `Diploma of College Studies (DEC), Computer Science Technology, <CEGEP> — 3-year technical program`; the bachelor's by name. Add `(DEC-BAC pathway)` only if the candidate confirms a passerelle. No Ontario college-diploma comparison: no source of correspondence, facts only. If the candidate applies to an Ontario college, the OQF fact may be stated as such: an Ontario Advanced Diploma is six semesters or 1800 to 2100 instructional hours (Government of Ontario, « Ontario Qualifications Framework », consulted 2026-11-08, S504).

### C. No DEC (adult/experience admission)
- Print the degree name and institution only: no credit count, no length, no arithmetic or equivalence line. Sources: UQAM « Base Expérience »: age 21+, « connaissances appropriées », 2 years of computer-related work for the 7316 bachelor's, make-up courses MAT0600 and MAT0341 within the first year (UQAM, « Bases d'admissibilité 1er cycle », consulted 2026-11-08, S475; UQAM, « Baccalauréat en informatique et génie logiciel (7316) », consulted 2026-11-08, S487); Concordia: Secondary V alone needs a DEC or Mature Entry (Concordia University, « Admission requirements: Canada », consulted 2026-11-08, S489), Mature Entry is 108 credits, not 90 (Concordia University, « Understanding your degree credit requirements », consulted 2026-11-08, S490).

### D. Highest credential is not a bachelor's
- Read it from the CV first; ask only if absent or unclear. One education line per credential: its English type name (CICIC (CICIC, « The Education System of Quebec », consulted 2026-11-08, S513; CICIC (Gov. of Canada), « Postsecondary education in Quebec », consulted 2026-11-08, S481); the institution's own English name if it publishes one), the program name as issued, the length as the institution's program page states it, no more. Never "associate degree", "Ontario college diploma", "Advanced Diploma", "high school equivalent" or "equivalent to…": no source states a correspondence (rule; the OQF fact in B stays facts only). Evaluator equivalence only per "Grades and evaluation" below.
- Technical DEC: the line of B, `Diploma of College Studies (DEC), <program>, <CEGEP> — 3-year technical program` (CICIC diagram: DCS Technical, 3 years (CICIC, « The Education System of Quebec », consulted 2026-11-08, S513; Gouvernement du Québec, « Education system », consulted 2026-11-08, S485)). The English heading and line format is a convention (LOW, unsourced); program lengths (CICIC, « The Education System of Quebec », consulted 2026-11-08, S513; Gouvernement du Québec, « Education system », consulted 2026-11-08, S485).
- Pre-university DEC only: `Diploma of College Studies (DEC), Pre-University, <CEGEP> — 2-year program` (CICIC diagram: DCS Pre-university, 2 years (CICIC, « The Education System of Quebec », consulted 2026-11-08, S513; CICIC (Gov. of Canada), « Postsecondary education in Quebec », consulted 2026-11-08, S481): leads to university admission). It is a college credential, not "some university"; no credit or year-of-university claim. The English heading and line format is a convention (LOW, unsourced); program length (CICIC, « The Education System of Quebec », consulted 2026-11-08, S513; CICIC (Gov. of Canada), « Postsecondary education in Quebec », consulted 2026-11-08, S481).
- DEP: `Diploma of Vocational Studies (DEP), <program>, <school>` (CICIC legend: DVS = Diploma of Vocational Studies (CICIC, « The Education System of Quebec », consulted 2026-11-08, S513)). Length only from the program page, in hours as stated; CICIC's 600 to 1,800 hours is the diagram's range for the credential, not the candidate's program (CICIC, « The Education System of Quebec », consulted 2026-11-08, S513).
- DES (Secondary V): `Secondary School Diploma (SSD), <school>, <year>` — Québec's own English name, five secondary years after six elementary (Gouvernement du Québec, « Education system », consulted 2026-11-08, S485). Never "Grade 12 diploma" or "high school diploma" as the heading (no source maps the DES to either for employment; rule). Fact for admissions only, never for jobs: U of T states "the DES (Secondary V) Diploma is not an accepted basis for admission" and asks Québec applicants for the DEC route (UTM: "basis of admission") (University of Toronto, « Requirements: Canadian High Schools », consulted 2026-11-08, S552; University of Toronto Mississauga, « Admission Requirements », consulted 2026-11-08, S553). Forms with a level menu: "high school / secondary school" only if the form's own definition fits; otherwise the comment field. The form-menu answer is a convention (LOW, unsourced).
- DEP in truck driving: `Diploma of Vocational Studies (DEP), Trucking (Transport par camion; programs 5291/5791), <school>` with 615 h ("615 heures", (Inforoute FPT (ministère de l'Éducation du Québec), « Transport par camion », consulted 2026-11-08, S554)) (prerequisite: meet SAAQ requirements for a class 1 licence) (Inforoute FPT (ministère de l'Éducation du Québec), « Transport par camion », consulted 2026-11-08, S554; Inforoute FPT (ministère de l'Éducation du Québec), « Trucking », consulted 2026-11-08, S555). The licence is a separate line, not part of the DEP: [`../driving.md`](../driving.md#canadian-and-québec-licences). Same education line whether the target is Ontario, another province or the US.
- AEC: `Attestation of College Studies (AEC), <program>, <CEGEP>` (CICIC (Gov. of Canada), « Postsecondary education in Quebec », consulted 2026-11-08, S481; CICIC, « The Education System of Quebec », consulted 2026-11-08, S513); never DEC, never rewritten as "certificate" or "diploma"; length as the college states it (CICIC's diagram shows ACS as 1 year (CICIC, « The Education System of Quebec », consulted 2026-11-08, S513); certificate programs are "of variable length").
- University certificate: `Certificate in <title>, <university>` with the university's own English title; if it publishes none, keep the French title as issued. Credits only with unit and system ("30 credits (Québec)", `education-cases.md`); Québec university certificates normally require 30 credits (CICIC (Gov. of Canada), « Postsecondary education in Quebec », consulted 2026-11-08, S481), e.g. Université de Montréal: « La mineure et le certificat comportent des cours totalisant 30 crédits » (art. 2.2 b) (Université de Montréal, Secrétariat général, « Règlement des études de premier cycle », consulted 2026-11-08, S505). A microprogramme is not a certificate: keep its issued title (UdeM: attestation). Never under a bachelor's heading, never as a degree.
- Studies stopped before a degree: [`education.md`](education.md#studies-stopped-without-the-diploma) and [`education-cases.md`](../../../education-cases.md#studies-stopped-without-the-diploma) in English (`Studies toward a Bachelor of …`, credits with unit); if a DEC or AEC was completed, that is the highest credential and gets its line above.
- Forms: "Bachelor's / four-year degree required?" is answered "No" for all of these. For a level menu (high school, college diploma, trade certificate, associate, bachelor's…), pick an option only when the form's own definition fits the credential as held; otherwise use the comment or "other" field; never pick "associate" or "bachelor's". "Bachelor's or equivalent experience": answer from the candidate's actual years of experience, never by claiming the credential is equivalent. Convention (LOW, unsourced). "Studies stopped": never "degree completed". Convention (LOW, unsourced); truthful answers, a knockout answered "No" truthfully beats an application that misstates.

## Application-form questions ("Do you have a four-year degree?")
- Disclose, never hide: the CV education line shows the format (A: DEC + 3-year line above).
- A: use a comment field or an "equivalent" option first, with the arithmetic sentence; answer "Yes" only when nothing else is offered, with the arithmetic line on the CV and the candidate's agreement. Convention (LOW, unsourced).
- B: comment field or "equivalent/other"; "Yes" only if the program was in fact four years. C: never "Yes"; if no comment or "equivalent" option exists, tell the candidate the form will likely reject.
- AI screening reads the CV as text, so the arithmetic sentence goes on the CV itself, not only in a cover letter (LOW, unsourced, inference).
- Graduate school: general policies say "four years in North America" (U of T SGS (University of Toronto SGS, « FAQ », consulted 2026-11-08, S491)) or "honours degree or equivalent" (York (York University, Faculty of Graduate Studies, « Entry requirements », consulted 2026-11-08, S484)) without mentioning Québec; uOttawa Regulation C-2 requires a « four-year bachelor's degree (120 units) or equivalent », with a qualifying program possible for some master's programs, and is silent on Québec (University of Ottawa, « C-2 Admission to Graduate Programs », consulted 2026-11-08, S571); Carleton Graduate Studies' minimum requirements ask for a « 4-year (honours) bachelor's degree », with a qualifying-year program otherwise, and are silent on Québec (Carleton University, Graduate Studies, « Minimum Requirements », consulted 2026-11-08, S573) (only the MSW states the Québec equivalence, (Carleton University, School of Social Work, « MSW FAQs », consulted 2026-11-08, S482)); individual programs may (Carleton University, School of Social Work, « MSW FAQs », consulted 2026-11-08, S482; University of Toronto, MScAC (Master of Science in Applied Computing), « Apply », consulted 2026-11-08, S483; University of British Columbia, Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies, « Canada », consulted 2026-11-08, S570). Check the program's own page.
- Federal public service: the Treasury Board core qualification standard says « the term "degree" refers to a baccalaureate as established by educational authorities », with no length (Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat, « Qualification standards for the core public administration by occupational group or classification », consulted 2026-11-08, S572). For a federal posting requiring a degree, print the Québec bachelor's by name as awarded: no 4-year or 16-years line. Agency-specific standards may differ: check the posting (the TBS text, (Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat, « Qualification standards for the core public administration by occupational group or classification », consulted 2026-11-08, S572); applying it to a Québec bachelor's is inference, LOW, unsourced).

## Grades and evaluation
- GPA: print with its scale, never converted; rules in [`education.md`](education.md#gpa-moyenne-cumulative).
- Credential evaluation (WES, ICAS, IQAS): state an evaluator equivalence only if the candidate holds an official evaluation report in their name, quoted in the report's own words with the evaluator named; never predict it. Otherwise facts only: degree, institution, years, credits. Individual evaluation is required for a DEC or a 3-year Québec bachelor's (rule; no published evaluator equivalence found). Fact: IQAS "evaluates formal academic or technical education" (Government of Alberta, IQAS, « Assessment for work or study – How to apply », consulted 2026-11-08, S503).
