# Québec credentials, English Canada

Add-on to [`outbound-common.md`](outbound-common.md), which still applies.

- An OIQ licence is not valid elsewhere: each province needs its own licence, though the Canadian Free Trade Agreement makes transfer largely automatic (Engineers Canada, « Mobility across Canada », consulted 2026-11).

## Degree length: identify the path first
- Identify which path led to the bachelor's before writing the education line; never assume. Usually the CV already shows it: a listed DEC and its program name; read it there. Ask only when no DEC is listed (omitted, or path C) or the DEC's type is unclear. Paths (convention, LOW): (A) 2-year pre-university DEC, then a 3-year bachelor's; (B) 3-year technical DEC, then a bachelor's; (C) no DEC (adult/experience admission). UQAM's admission bases are « Base DEC » and « Base Expérience » S475 (HIGH). A genuinely 4-year Québec bachelor's (e.g. engineering): print "4-year" with the degree, no further sentence.
- Never print the Québec credit count or "3-year" alone. A Québec credit is 45 hours of work at Laval S493, UQAM S129, HEC Montréal S495, Université de Montréal S505 ("Un crédit représente 45 heures" / "quarante-cinq heures d'activités d'apprentissage, y compris l'évaluation" / "Le crédit représente 45 heures" / "Le crédit représente 45 heures consacrées par un étudiant à l’atteinte des objectifs d’un cours"), "normally" 45 at Sherbrooke S476 and "environ" 45 at UQAR S494; no Québec-wide text exists, so name the institution (HIGH for each). Ontario schools publish a 90-credit 3-year general degree beside the 120-credit 4-year one (York Urban Studies S477, uOttawa S478, Nipissing S479; HIGH). That a Québec "90 credits / 3 years" reads as that general degree is inference (LOW). Three-year computer science degrees exist in Ontario: York (BA and BSc, "usually completed in three years", at least 90 credits, declared major in computer science, 2026-2027 calendar) S496; Laurentian ("Three-year programs lead to a Bachelor of Arts or Science in Computer Science", Total 90 credits) S497; Trent (BSc General, 15.0 credits to graduate; the calendar gives no year count) S498; Western ("3 Year BSc", "Major in Computer Science", 7.0 courses at Western for Fanshawe Computer Programmer Analyst diploma holders, 2024 articulation sheet) S499 (HIGH for each). Cover-letter use: "such degrees exist at York, Laurentian, Trent, Western"; it does not make a Québec degree equivalent.
- Never write "equivalent to a four-year degree" (see `outbound-common.md`): no ministry or regulator states it.

### A. Pre-university DEC + 3-year bachelor's
- Only when the posting or form requires four years (mirror its wording): `3-year program following the 2-year pre-university DEC (CEGEP); 16 years of study, as for a four-year degree after Grade 12`. Arithmetic: Québec students "complete 13 years of study before starting university" vs 12 elsewhere S480; the bachelor's is then three years S481 (HIGH). Candidate confirms the path first. Otherwise degree name only.
- List the DEC as its own line: `Diploma of College Studies (DEC), Pre-University, <CEGEP>` — it carries the arithmetic. Glossing: [`outbound-common.md`](outbound-common.md).
- Cover letter or form comment (never CV padding): "Some programs state it (Carleton's MSW: « CEGEP and a university degree, which is usually 3 years, is considered equivalent to a 4 year degree » S482; U of T MScAC: Québec applicants "will qualify with a two-year CEGEP and a three-year bachelor's degree", eligibility decided on a complete application S483)." Name only the programs the candidate applies to or cites; no generalisation (HIGH for those programs; York FGS requires "an honours degree or equivalent" and is silent on Québec S484). Do not argue from credits (a Québec credit is normally 45 h; other systems differ: "90 = 90" is coincidence). Do not say the DEC "is first-year university" (rule; the transfer-credit facts below are not a year-level equivalence). Institution-specific facts that support the arithmetic (cover letter, name the institution the candidate applies to, never "X recognizes your degree as 4 years"): Concordia: "Quebec CEGEP graduates complete 13 years of study before starting university", CEGEP applicants: three years (90 credits) in many programs, longer for exceptions (all Engineering, Early Childhood and Elementary Education, TESL, Art Education, Exercise Science/Athletic Therapy) S480; McGill Science (SOUSA): CEGEP DEC gives U1 and 30 credits of advanced standing, "You will be in U1 if you have been admitted with 24 or more credits of advanced standing" S500; UBC Vancouver: transfer credit "for up to one full year of study" for a DEC S501; Waterloo: up to 10 units (two years) of transfer credit, course by course (70% or better), if admitted to an honours or four-year general program, and program-dependent S502. These are credit-transfer rules for incoming students, not degree-level equivalence.

### B. Technical DEC (3 years) + bachelor's
- Facts: the technical DEC is its own credential, "generally lasts three years" S485; it includes the general-education courses S486; universities may add make-up math (UQAM: MAT0341 « hors programme », first term S487; Laval: MAT-0150/0260/0130 at the start S488); a DEC-BAC passerelle credits part of the DEC and shortens the bachelor's (Laval 420.B0: 24 to 27 credits, DEC no older than 5 years S488; UQAM S487). HIGH.
- No source gives a total length or equivalence for this path: no arithmetic line, no equivalence line (the 13-year argument of A does not apply).
- CV: the DEC on its own line with length and field: `Diploma of College Studies (DEC), Computer Science Technology, <CEGEP> — 3-year technical program`; the bachelor's by name. Add `(DEC-BAC pathway)` only if the candidate confirms a passerelle. No Ontario college-diploma comparison: no source of correspondence, facts only. If the candidate applies to an Ontario college, the OQF fact may be stated as such: an Ontario Advanced Diploma is six semesters or 1800 to 2100 instructional hours S504.

### C. No DEC (adult/experience admission)
- Print the degree name and institution only: no credit count, no length, no arithmetic or equivalence line. Sources: UQAM « Base Expérience »: age 21+, « connaissances appropriées », 2 years of computer-related work for the 7316 bachelor's, make-up courses MAT0600 and MAT0341 within the first year S475, S487; Concordia: Secondary V alone needs a DEC or Mature Entry S489, Mature Entry is 108 credits, not 90 S490. HIGH.

## Application-form questions ("Do you have a four-year degree?")
- Disclose, never hide: the CV education line shows the format (A: DEC + 3-year line above).
- A: use a comment field or an "equivalent" option first, with the arithmetic sentence; answer "Yes" only when nothing else is offered, with the arithmetic line on the CV and the candidate's agreement. Convention (LOW).
- B: comment field or "equivalent/other"; "Yes" only if the program was in fact four years. C: never "Yes"; if no comment or "equivalent" option exists, tell the candidate the form will likely reject.
- AI screening reads the CV as text, so the arithmetic sentence goes on the CV itself, not only in a cover letter (LOW, inference).
- Graduate school: general policies say "four years in North America" (U of T SGS S491) or "honours degree or equivalent" (York S484) without mentioning Québec; individual programs may (S482, S483). Check the program's own page.

## Grades and evaluation
- GPA: print with its scale, never converted; rules in [`education.md`](education.md#gpa-moyenne-cumulative).
- Credential evaluation (WES, ICAS, IQAS): state an evaluator equivalence only if the candidate holds an official evaluation report in their name, quoted in the report's own words with the evaluator named (same rule as [`india/to-anglosphere.md`](../../india/to-anglosphere.md)); never predict it. Otherwise facts only: degree, institution, years, credits. Individual evaluation is required for a DEC or a 3-year Québec bachelor's (rule; no published evaluator equivalence found). Fact: IQAS "evaluates formal academic or technical education" S503.
