# Field pack: trades, Québec site-safety training (ASP Construction, SIMDUT)

Loaded on demand when the Québec trades CV lists an ASP Construction card, a 30-hour site-safety course or SIMDUT (WHMIS) training. Read together with [`trades.md`](trades.md) (with [`fields/trades.md`](../../../fields/trades.md)). Print safety training as the rules in [`rules.md`](../../../certifications/rules.md) say for validity. Each fact is what the issuing body's own page says (consulted 2026-11-07).

## ASP Construction card

- Course "Santé et sécurité générale sur les chantiers de construction", 30 hours, given by school service centres or employer and union associations (S316); the card is the "carte de l'ASP Construction" and "le cours 30 h est obligatoire" to work on sites (S325).
- List the course with its year: ASP says it is mandatory to work on sites, and the CCQ requires it with the CCA and CCC applications (S325, S317, S316).
- Print the course title and year. ASP Construction says its cards have no expiry date, "bonnes à vie", the 30 h card included, but some specific-training cards carry a validity or update period of 2 to 5 years. A lost card is replaced through ASP Construction, for a fee (S325).
- Fall protection, confined space, aerial lift: no regulator page on these was read. ASP Construction lists a 5-year update for aerial lift platforms ("PEMP") (S325).
- Forklift (RSST arts. 256.2 and 256.3), workplace first aid (CNESST) and OSHA cards: [`../workplace-safety.md`](../workplace-safety.md). US CDL: [`../driving.md`](../driving.md).

## WHMIS / SIMDUT

- The law "n'exige pas que les travailleurs détiennent un certificat, une carte ou tout autre document"; certificates "ne sont décernés aux travailleurs ni par Santé Canada, ni par aucun organisme de réglementation du SIMDUT". The CNESST accredits no trainer and cannot reissue a card: ask the provider (S326).
- No regulator issues a card or certificate, and the employer must train its workers (S326). List it as training received, with provider and year, only if the candidate has proof.
- Print "SIMDUT (WHMIS) training, <provider>, <year>", never "WHMIS certified" or "WHMIS licensed". SIMDUT 1988 training is not valid for SIMDUT 2015; between 2015 and the 2022 update the CNESST says retraining is needed only where the classification of the products used changed (S326).
