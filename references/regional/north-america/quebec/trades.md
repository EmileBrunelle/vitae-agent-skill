# Field pack: trades, Québec

Load this file with [`fields/trades.md`](../../../fields/trades.md) when the trades CV targets the Québec construction industry (CCQ, RBQ, SAAQ). ASP Construction cards and SIMDUT: [`trades-safety.md`](trades-safety.md), read only when the CV lists them. Red Seal: one line below, detail in [`canada/trades.md`](../canada/trades.md).

## CCQ certificates (Québec construction)

- Working in the Québec construction industry covered by the "Loi R-20" requires a "certificat de compétence". Three types: "Certificat de compétence apprenti (CCA)", "compagnon (CCC)", "occupation (CCO)" (S315). Print the type and the métier as issued: the CCA "indique le métier", the CCC "indique le métier et, s'il y a lieu, la spécialité ou les activités du métier" (S317, S316).
- Print the status the CCQ issued, in its words: apprenti (CCA), compagnon (CCC) or occupation (CCO); the CCA and the CCC state the métier (S315, S317, S316).
- Validity: the CCQ "émet des certificats de compétence valides pendant 12 mois" (the student CCA below is the exception read); a compagnon active in the 14 months before the renewal date is renewed automatically, otherwise on request (S316, S319). Print the validity as [`rules.md`](../../../certifications/rules.md) says, with the dates on the certificate.
- Exam is not the certificate: an apprentice who passes the provincial qualification exam obtains the CCC automatically; a non-apprentice who passes "doit en faire la demande à la CCQ et acquitter les frais" (S316).
- A MTESS qualification certificate (électricien, système frigorifique, plomberie, chauffage) lets a candidate "obtenir" the CCC only with, in addition, a CCQ-recognised programme and at least 8,000 hours of experience in the trade, training credits included (S316). The MTESS certificate alone is not a CCC.
- Student CCA: temporary, 6 months, not renewable, cancelled if the student leaves or finishes the programme, though a new application is possible while still enrolled (S317). Print it as a student apprentice certificate with dates.
- Qualification "opérateur de camion-flèche": added to the compagnon's certificate after the 90-hour module and the exam; maintained by proving 50 hours' work a year (S316).
- A Red Seal holder applies separately to the CCQ for a certificat de compétence (S322). Red Seal itself: [`canada/trades.md`](../canada/trades.md).

## Entries and hours

- The CCQ asks an employer's letter to state these fields for hours worked outside construction, in the application for a compagnon certificate on an MTESS qualification: company name and address, "la description détaillée des tâches réellement effectuées", "s'il y a lieu, le type de machinerie utilisée", the sector (and the dwelling type if residential), "les périodes d'emploi et le total exact des heures travaillées" per year (S316). Collect them so the CV matches any letter later filed.
- Hours: an apprentice's hours are recorded in the "carnet d'apprentissage" from the employers' monthly reports for sites covered by the Québec construction law ("loi R-20"); hours from work not covered enter it only after CCQ analysis (S318).
- Apprenticeship runs in periods of 2,000 hours, "d'une à cinq" depending on the trade (S315, S318). The pages read give no print format for the period: write it only as the certificate or carnet shows it.

## RBQ licence (contractors)

- The RBQ licence is for a business: "délivrée aux personnes qui s'occupent de la gestion d'une entreprise de construction", concerning "le droit de l'entreprise à exécuter ou à faire exécuter" work; it lists subclasses and "répondants" (S327, S328). A worker's trade card is not an RBQ licence.
- Print the licence number and subclass only for a candidate who is the contractor or "répondant"; an employee never lists the employer's licence as their own. Verify in the RBQ's "Registre des détenteurs de licence" (S328).

## Equipment and SAAQ licence classes

- "Grutier", "Opérateur d'équipement lourd", "Opérateur de pelles mécaniques" are CCQ métiers (S321); "opérateur de camion-flèche" is a qualification added to a compagnon's certificate after a 90-hour module and an exam, and it requires a valid class 3 licence (S316).
- Classes as defined by the SAAQ: 1 (ensemble de véhicules routiers), 2 (autobus de plus de 24 passagers), 3 (camion porteur), 5 (véhicule de promenade, including the "véhicule-outil"); mentions F and M (heavy vehicle with air brakes, with manual transmission) and T (grand train routier) (S329). Print class and mention as on the licence.

## French trade names

- On a French CV for the Québec construction industry use the CCQ name of the métier: Charpentier-menuisier, Électricien, Tuyauteur, Frigoriste, Ferblantier, Mécanicien de chantier, Opérateur d'équipement lourd, Peintre, Plâtrier, Grutier and the others on its list (S321). That list has no entry named "plombier": plumbing training is the programme "Plomberie-chauffage" under Tuyauteur. Soudeur and Soudeur en tuyauterie are listed as occupations spécialisées, not métiers; the CCQ counts 25 métiers and 6 occupations spécialisées (S321, S320).
