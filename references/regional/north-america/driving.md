# Driving: US commercial driver's licence (CDL)

Read only when the job requires driving a heavy or commercial vehicle and the candidate lists a driver credential. Québec classes (1, 2, 3, 5; mentions F, M, T): [`quebec/trades.md`](quebec/trades.md) (SAAQ). A licence is issued by a government for a jurisdiction; it is not a training card. Each fact is what the regulator's own page or text says (consulted 2026-11-07).

## Who issues what

- "FMCSA does not issue Commercial Driver's Licenses (CDLs). State governments are responsible for issuing CDLs" (S452). Name the state in the CV line; the licence number is not needed (suggested).
- A CDL is "a license issued to an individual by a State or other jurisdiction of domicile ... which authorizes the individual to operate a class of a commercial motor vehicle" (S453). A "commercial learner's permit (CLP)" only allows accompanied behind-the-wheel training: a CLP is not a CDL (S453).
- Every CDL shows its date of issue and expiry and the group and endorsements (S454): print validity as [`../../certifications/rules.md`](../../certifications/rules.md) says, with the dates on the licence. Federal text sets no period in the sections read.

## Class (group) and endorsements

- Federal text mostly says "group": "Combination vehicle (Group A)", "Heavy Straight Vehicle (Group B)", "Small Vehicle (Group C)", shown on the CDL as A, B, C (S455, S454); the ELDT rule and some restriction codes write "Class A CDL", "Class A passenger vehicle" (S456, S454). Write "Class A CDL" or "Group A" as the state licence reads.
- Group A: combination with a GCWR of 26,001 lb or more where the towed unit exceeds 10,000 lb GVWR. Group B: a single vehicle of 26,001 lb or more, or one towing no more than 10,000 lb. Group C: neither, but designed for 16 or more passengers including the driver, or used to transport hazardous materials as § 383.5 defines them (S455).
- A Group A holder who passed the tests "may operate a heavy straight vehicle (Group B) or a small vehicle (Group C), provided that he/she possesses the requisite endorsement(s)"; Group B covers Group C the same way (S455). Do not write "Class B" as extra when the CDL is Class A.
- Endorsement codes: T double/triple trailers, P passenger, N tank vehicle, H hazardous materials, X tank and hazardous materials combined, S school bus (S454). Each is earned by tests: knowledge test for T, N, H; knowledge and skills test for P and S (S457). An endorsement the licence does not show is not held.
- Restrictions on the licence (for example L "No Air brake equipped CMV", E "No Manual transmission equipped CMV", O "No Tractor-trailer CMV") limit what the CV may claim: do not list a vehicle type or transmission the restriction code excludes (S454).

## Training is not the licence

- Entry-level driver training (ELDT) applies to an "entry-level driver": one who must pass the CDL tests for a first CDL, an upgrade to Class A or B, or a first P, S or H endorsement, with listed exceptions (for example military CMV experience, restricted CDLs). Drivers issued a Class A or B CDL or a P, S or H endorsement before February 7, 2022 are not required to comply for that CDL or endorsement; those who obtain a CLP on or after that date are. Training must come from "an entity listed on FMCSA's Training Provider Registry" (S456). Completed training is a course: print "CDL training, <provider>, <year>" (suggested), not as the licence.

## Cross-border

- A footnote to 49 CFR 383.23 records the Administrator's determination that commercial licences issued by Canadian provinces and territories "in conformity with the Canadian National Safety Code" are in accordance with the US standards, and a driver holding one "is prohibited from obtaining a non-domiciled CDL, or any other type of driver's license" from a US state (S458). Print the Canadian licence as issued (province, class); do not describe it as a "US CDL".
- Canadian provinces other than Québec: no official page loaded; not covered here.
