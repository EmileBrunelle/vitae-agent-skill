# Health professions: Canada outside Québec (colleges)

Read IN ADDITION to [`../../certifications/health.md`](../../certifications/health.md) (decision rule: exam passed, licence held, certification) when the CV is for a regulated health profession in a Canadian province other than Québec. Québec: [`quebec/health.md`](quebec/health.md). Each fact is what the body's own page says (consulted 2026-11-07) and holds for that body only. Not read: colleges outside Ontario.

## Ontario nursing (CNO)
- « All nurses must be currently registered with CNO to practice nursing in Ontario »; holding oneself out as « a nurse, Registered Nurse, Registered Practical Nurse, or Nurse Practitioner » without being registered is the case CNO says it receives reports about (CNO, « Unregistered Practitioners », S342). Public register « Find a Nurse », searchable by registration number (CNO, « Confirm a Nurse’s Status », S343). Other provinces: ask for the college's register label.
