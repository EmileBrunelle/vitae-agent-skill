# Québec credentials, other markets

Loaded on demand, in addition to the target market's file, when the candidate holds Québec credentials (DEC, AEC, DEP, Québec university degree, Québec order membership) and targets ANY market other than Québec.

Also read the "Protected titles" section of `quebec.md` (that section only): never write or translate a job title into a reserved one (ingénieur/engineer, architecte, CPA…) without confirmed membership — the same caution applies in every target market.

## Education: state facts, not equivalences
- Transfers, stopped studies, in-progress degrees, bootcamps: `references/education-cases.md`.
- The Québec path is 11 years of secondary + a 2-year pre-university DEC (or 3-year technical DEC) + a bachelor's of typically 3 years (90 credits) — CICIC (Government of Canada): https://www.cicic.ca/1174/postsecondary_education_in_quebec.canada . No official source states that a Québec 3-year bachelor's "equals" a 4-year degree elsewhere: never write "equivalent to a 4-year degree". Write the durations so the reader can do the arithmetic, e.g. "B.Sc. Computer Science (3-year program following a 2-year pre-university DEC)".
- English names (CICIC): DEC = "Diploma of College Studies", AEC = "Attestation of College Studies". DEP: no official English name confirmed; gloss below.

## English Canada and USA
- An OIQ licence is not valid elsewhere: each province needs its own licence, though the Canadian Free Trade Agreement makes transfer largely automatic (https://engineerscanada.ca/become-an-engineer/mobility-across-canada). A Canadian licence is not a US PE licence (state boards license; https://ncees.org/licensure/).
- In the USA the bare word "engineer" is regulated by state, not uniformly (e.g. Texas restricts it; https://pels.texas.gov/nm/2024/pao-65-response.pdf). Don't claim a rule for a state without a source; flag it.
- US credential evaluation (WES, ECE) depends on evaluator and program; ECE: "Not all international degrees are automatically considered equivalent to a U.S. bachelor's degree" (https://www.ece.org/Blogs/Bachelors-Degree-Recognized.htm). Never promise the outcome.
- Work authorization: Canadian citizens can work in the USA under TN status for listed professions (Engineer: bachelor's or provincial licence; 8 CFR 214.6, https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/8/214.6). No authoritative source requires stating it on the résumé — ask the candidate; don't add it by default.

## France
- France has no legal diploma equivalence: « on ne reconnaît pas le principe d'équivalence de diplôme sur le plan juridique. On utilise plutôt la notion de comparabilité » (Euroguidance France, May 2025, https://www.euroguidance-france.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/la-reconnaissance-de-diplome.pdf). For a non-regulated job, the employer judges, and may ask for an ENIC-NARIC « attestation de comparabilité » — optional, 120 €, about 4 months; it compares the diploma to « un niveau de diplôme français » (https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F463). Regulated professions follow their own procedure (for Québec: the Entente Québec-France ARMs, e.g. engineers OIQ↔CTI; list: https://www.quebec.ca/emploi/travailler-hors-quebec/faire-reconnaitre-competences-etranger/entente-quebec-france/liste-metiers-professions-quebec). In France « ingénieur diplômé » is reserved to CTI-accredited diplomas (https://cdn-contenu.quebec.ca/cdn-contenu/adm/min/relations-internationales/entente-quebec-france/ARM-refonte-admin-Ingenieurs-MRIF.pdf; OIQ↔CTI ARM, 2020 avenant: https://www.iesf.fr/offres/doc_inline_src/752/201023_CP_ARM_France-Quebec_2020_CTI_IESF_OIQ.pdf).
- False friend: in France the baccalauréat ends secondary school; in Québec it is the first university degree (OQLF). On a French CV never write a bare « Baccalauréat en informatique »: give the duration and level wording the reader knows, and if the candidate has an attestation de comparabilité, quote its level verbatim. French postings express education as « Bac+N » (Apec fiche métier, e.g. chef de projet informatique décisionnelle, https://www.apec.fr/tous-nos-metiers/informatique/) — only state a Bac+N figure that an attestation (or the candidate's French co-diploma) supports; otherwise give durations.

## Double degrees (Québec–France)
- Double-degree agreements award two separate national diplomas, each issued by its own university (e.g. the UQAR baccalauréat en informatique program + Université de Bretagne Occidentale's Licence en informatique: UBO deliberation 2022-006, art. 1–2, https://ged-ubo.univ-brest.fr/nuxeo/nxfile/default/d689105d-e670-4ccc-bd98-3785f1ae6d9e/blobholder:0/2022-006_Vote%20sur%20le%20protocole%20d%E2%80%99entente%20sp%C3%A9cifique%20de%20double-dipl%C3%B4me%20international%20Licence%20d%E2%80%99Informatique%20(UBO)%20et%20Majeure%20en%20Informatique%20UQAR%20(Canada).pdf). List them as two education entries, each with its institution and city, and mention the agreement once (« double diplomation UQAR–UBO »). Order by the target market: the French diploma first for France (it needs no explanation there), the Québec one first for Québec. Use the exact diploma names from the transcripts (the UQAR program is the baccalauréat en informatique; the diploma awarded is the « Baccalauréat ès sciences appliquées (B. Sc. A.) », https://www.uqar.ca/programmes-domaines-detudes/baccalaureat-en-informatique/).

## Gloss for readers outside Québec
- **CEGEP**, not « Cégep » or « CÉGEP »: in English it's written all-caps, unaccented — native anglophone-cégep usage, not a translation. Never gloss it as "technical college" (not the institution's name, and in North American English it reads as a trade school, collapsing the distinction with the DEP/DVS below) or as "community college" (in the US an alternate/remedial path; in Québec the cégep is the mandatory bridge between high school and university — the analogy undersells the candidate). Same for « BAC+2 »: means nothing to this readership.
- DEC → "Diploma of College Studies (DEC)" — the sanctioned English term; "College Diploma (DEC)" is approximate. The useful distinguishing fact is length: "three-year Career/Technical programs" vs. "two-year Pre-university programs". Recommended descriptor: "CEGEP — Québec public higher education, three-year technical program".
- DEP → "Vocational Diploma (DEP/DVS — Diploma of Vocational Studies)".
- This applies to every credential name, not only the examples listed here — never expand an unfamiliar acronym from memory; verify with the institution or the candidate (rule 1).
- ATS: from the English CV, a Québec DEC / DEP becomes an inexact equivalent ("Diploma…", "DVS/DEP"); a French CV keeps `DEC, Informatique de Gestion` intact.
