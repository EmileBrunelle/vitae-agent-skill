# Market: Spain

Spain only; never reuse for Latin America (Spanish-language CV conventions differ by country).

## At a glance
- Paper: a4.
- Photo: optional; SEPE says « Se puede incluir una foto » and, in its advice for Spain, « Es recomendable incluir una foto, de formato “carnet” o de medio cuerpo, que sea de calidad (evitar selfies …), sobre un fondo neutro » (SEPE, « Recomendaciones generales para Currículum y Carta de presentación », 2025; S540). The candidate decides; if included, formal and neutral background.
- CV language: Spanish; the posting's language wins. Catalan, Galician, Basque or Valencian only when the posting or the region's employer asks.
- Personal data: national ID (DNI/NIE number) never by default (rule); birth date, marital status, children and nationality: ask the candidate. SEPE: « No incluir en el CV expectativas salariales ni detalles personales (estado civil, hijos, religión, política) » (S540).
- Formality / verb style: no authoritative local norm found; follow the posting's register.
- Length: aim for one page, two at most with a lot of experience (SEPE, 2025: « Intente que el CV no supere una página, máximo dos si tiene ya mucha experiencia »; a recommendation, not a rule; S540).

## Personal data and law
- Non-discrimination in hiring is statutory: the Estatuto de los Trabajadores (RDL 2/2015, art. 4.2.c and 17.1) lists estado civil, edad, discapacidad and « lengua dentro del Estado español » among the grounds. This is why the data above is omitted by default; it does not forbid volunteering it.
- Do NOT add a « consentimiento LOPDGDD / autorizo el tratamiento de mis datos » clause by default. The AEPD states that processing a candidate's data during selection « no exige el consentimiento de la persona candidata »; the legal basis is RGPD art. 6.1.b (pre-contractual measures), and the duty to inform falls on the employer. Consent is needed only for keeping the CV after a failed process (bolsa de trabajo), and the employer collects it. A clause is harmless but redundant; if the candidate wants one, let them decide (AEPD, « La protección de datos en las relaciones laborales », 2021).

## Protected titles
- Practising an academic-title profession without the title is a crime (Código Penal art. 403), aggravated if the person publicly claims the professional quality. Never write or upgrade a header title into « Ingeniero (Industrial / de Caminos…) », « Arquitecto », « Abogado » unless the candidate holds the matching official title; keep the unregulated function name in the header and the degree in education (see [`references/regional.md`](../../regional.md)).
- Engineering: the habilitating master carries the profession's name: no title may use « Master en Ingeniería Industrial » without meeting the ministerial conditions (Orden CIN/311/2009). A Grado alone does not give « Ingeniero Industrial »: write « Graduado/a en Ingeniería… » and claim the habilitating Máster only if completed.
- Colegiación: required only « cuando así lo establezca una ley estatal » (Ley 2/1974, art. 3.2); whether a given engineering job requires it was not settled by a primary source. Never write « colegiado » or a colegiado number unless the candidate states it; list it under certifications if held.

## Degree names and grading
- Official names: « Graduado/Graduada en [título RUCT] », « Máster Universitario en … », « Doctor/Doctora » (RD 822/2021, arts. 13, 16, 19). Use the exact name registered in the RUCT, plus the university. Grado = 240 ECTS (300-360 for some regulated degrees); Máster Universitario = 60, 90 or 120 ECTS.
- Títulos propios must not be passed off as official (RD 822/2021, arts. 36-37): the university's own master is « Máster de Formación Permanente », never « Máster Universitario »; also « Diploma de Especialización », « Diploma de Experto ». Keep the issuer's exact name.
- Grading: 0-10; Suspenso / Aprobado 5,0-6,9 / Notable 7,0-8,9 / Sobresaliente 9,0-10; « Matrícula de Honor » from 9,0, capped at 5 % of a course's students (RD 1125/2003, art. 5). Keep the original scale for foreign readers.

## Languages
- No constitutional duty to know a co-official language, so do not imply one (Constitución, art. 3).
- Catalan certificates map to CEFR: inicial A1, bàsic A2, elemental B1, intermedi B2, suficiència C1, superior C2 (Generalitat de Catalunya, Política Lingüística, « Informació general (certificats de català) »; Decret 37/2024 itself not loaded, LOW confidence). Cite the certificate name and body, with the CEFR level.

## Application package and public sector
- Europass is an optional EU model, not a Spanish requirement; its own page recommends a professional photo (Europass, CV creation page, 2026). Do not default to its template.
- Public sector (convocatorias): selection follows published bases (Estatuto Básico del Empleado Público, art. 55); general access requires Spanish nationality, with exceptions (arts. 56-57). Candidates follow the convocatoria's forms and méritos baremo, not a free CV; flag this instead of tailoring a CV.
- InfoJobs is the major private board; Empléate (SEPE) is the state portal.
