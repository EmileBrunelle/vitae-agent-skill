# Academic: France

Loaded on demand when the document is an academic CV or a CNU qualification dossier. Generic genre rules: [`references/fields/academic.md`](../../fields/academic.md).

- A CNU qualification application goes through the Galaxie/Odyssée platform with a fixed set of required pieces (thesis, defense report, publication list, up to 3 works) (Ministère de l’Enseignement supérieur et de la Recherche, Galaxie, « Qualification aux fonctions de maître de conférences et de professeur des universités », consulted 2026-11-07, S38; 2026 campaign: filing window Nov 3 – Dec 15, 2025).
- Each CNU section publishes its own recommendations, and they differ meaningfully by discipline (CNU section 25, « Qualification », consulted 2026-11-07, S39; doctoral-school guides tell candidates to check the target section's own page). Do not hard-code a CNU rule: read the target section's page and cite it in the deliverable.
- French-specific section names: "Qualification CNU section NN", "Responsabilités collectives", "Diffusion / vulgarisation".
- Teaching load is counted in hours with the CM / TD / TP breakdown, often in HETD (heures équivalent TD) *(not checked: the vocabulary is certain, whether it is mandatory on a free-form CV is not)*.
