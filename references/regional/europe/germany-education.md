# Education: Germany, Austria, Switzerland

Loaded on demand when the education section has an exchange, a dual study programme, a grade, or an MBA. Generic rules: [`references/education-cases.md`](../../education-cases.md); this file holds the DACH wordings and sources.

## Exchange / study abroad
- Never list the same stay in two sections: a study stay abroad sits inside the Studium entry (Münster WiWi CDC, « CDC Career Info – Lebenslauf », 2019).
- DACH may use its own heading, "Auslandsaufenthalte" (Köln WiSo Career, « Kurzinfo Lebenslauf », 2018).

## Work-integrated study (duales Studium)
- Label the paid employment as the contract says (dual student, apprentice). LOW, unsourced (convention).

## Grades
- Print the Abschlussnote against its scale: « Abschlussnote: 3.8 von 4.0 oder 3.8/4.0 » (Münster WiWi CDC, same document); never convert.

## MBA accreditation
- Check the programme in ELIAS, the Akkreditierungsrat's database of programme accreditation results, valid accreditations by default (Stiftung Akkreditierungsrat, « ELIAS-Database », consulted 2026-11-07, S172). FIBAA "awards its own quality seals for programme accreditation" and "is recognized as a quality assurance agency in Germany" (FIBAA, home page, consulted 2026-11-07, S173); still verify in ELIAS.
