## Personal data and law
- Ireland: the Employment Equality Acts outlaw discrimination in access to employment, including discriminatory advertisements, on nine grounds: gender, civil status, family status, sexual orientation, religion, age, disability, race, Traveller community (Workplace Relations Commission, « Employment Equality », consulted 2026-11-07, S62).

## Protected titles
- Ireland engineering: the right to the description « Chartered Engineer » and the abbreviation « C.Eng. » is confined to Chartered Members of the Institution and to persons the Council accepts as authorised by a recognised professional body (Irish Statute Book (Attorney General's Office), « The Institution of Civil Engineers of Ireland (Charter Amendment) Act, 1969 », s. 7, consulted 2026-11-07, S527). The Act gives a right of use and a register; it states no penalty. A UK CEng is not the Irish title; keep the awarding body in the entry.
- Ireland accountancy: « accountant » alone is not yet protected; the Companies (Protection of Title: Accountant) Bill 2025 passed to Second Stage (Chartered Accountants Ireland, « Introduction of the Companies (Protection of Title: Accountant) Bill 2025 in Dáil Éireann », 24 October 2025, consulted 2026-11-07, S67). Re-check status.

## Degree names and grading
- Ireland: honours bachelor's is NFQ Level 8 (normally 180-240 ECTS credits; QQI, « National Framework of Qualifications », consulted 2026-11-07, S523); never convert a GPA or mark to a class unless the transcript states it (LOW, unsourced: no official source on thresholds). Write the full degree title, not the abbreviation (Gradireland, « The graduate's guide to CVs: What your CV should include? », 2023, consulted 2026-11-07, S68).

## Application package
- Irish public service (publicjobs.ie): an online application form is the basis; a CV is needed only if the competition booklet says so, « usually only for very senior roles or specific technical positions »; a submitted application cannot be amended (Public Appointments Service, « Application advice », consulted 2026-11-07, S70). Evidence each criterion in the form, not the CV.
