# Market: Ireland

Part of the UK / Ireland pair: shared points in [`uk-ireland-common.md`](uk-ireland-common.md). Points that could not be confirmed are labelled LOW confidence.

## At a glance
- Length: Ireland: Gradireland tells graduates one A4 page, two as an absolute maximum (2023).

## Personal data and law
- Ireland: the Employment Equality Acts outlaw discrimination in access to employment, including discriminatory advertisements, on nine grounds: gender, civil status, family status, sexual orientation, religion, age, disability, race, Traveller community (Workplace Relations Commission, « Employment Equality », consulted 2026-11).

## Protected titles
- Ireland engineering: « Chartered Engineer » (CEng, MIEI) is awarded by Engineers Ireland (LOW confidence on the statutory basis: search snippets only). A UK CEng is not the Irish title; keep the awarding body in the entry.
- Ireland accountancy: « accountant » alone is not yet protected; the Companies (Protection of Title: Accountant) Bill 2025 passed to Second Stage (Chartered Accountants Ireland, « Introduction of the Companies (Protection of Title: Accountant) Bill 2025 in Dáil Éireann », 24 October 2025). Re-check status.

## Degree names and grading
- Ireland: honours bachelor's is NFQ Level 8; never convert a GPA or mark to a class unless the transcript states it (LOW confidence: institutional thresholds vary). Write the full degree title, not the abbreviation (Gradireland, « The graduate's guide to CVs », 2023).

## Application package
- Irish public service (publicjobs.ie): an online application form is the basis; a CV is needed only if the competition booklet says so, « usually only for very senior roles or specific technical positions »; a submitted application cannot be amended (Public Appointments Service, « Application advice », consulted 2026-11). Evidence each criterion in the form, not the CV.
