# Market: DACH (Germany / Austria / Switzerland), common points

Three markets sharing a language and a tabular « Lebenslauf » but differing on law, titles and certificates: read the country notes, never the average. Weakly sourced points are labelled LOW confidence.

## At a glance
- Paper: a4 in all three.
- Photo: optional everywhere, no legal duty; the Bundesagentur: « nicht zwingend erforderlich, wird aber immer noch von den meisten Arbeitgebern erwartet » (S506). Ask the candidate; follow the posting.
- CV language: the posting's language (German; French or Italian in Romandie and Ticino).
- Personal data: date and place of birth, nationality and marital status are optional in Germany and Austria: ask the candidate, never default-include or forbid. Bundesagentur: « Geburtsdatum und Geburtsort (Angaben kein Muss) » (S507), marital status « nicht erforderlich » (S506); AMS: they « können … angegeben werden, müssen aber nicht » (S508). Religion, party membership: not mentioned unless an advantage for the post (S507); religion, political or union membership, health: never (S508). Switzerland: see [`switzerland.md`](switzerland.md).
- Formality / verb style: tabular, reverse chronological (Bundesagentur; AMS). No official source on verb style: assert none.

## Zeugnisse (employer certificates)
- A Zeugnis is evidence to attach, never to paraphrase on the CV; never write the codes.
