# Market: DACH (Germany / Austria / Switzerland), common points

Three markets sharing a language and a tabular « Lebenslauf » but differing on law, titles and certificates: read the country notes, never the average. Weakly sourced points are labelled LOW confidence.

## At a glance
- Paper: a4 in all three.
- Photo: optional everywhere, no legal duty. Follow the posting; tell the user the custom.
- CV language: the posting's language (German; French or Italian in Romandie and Ticino).
- Personal data: omit by default in Germany and Austria (LOW confidence: custom, no source cited here); Switzerland differs for foreigners (see [`switzerland.md`](switzerland.md)).
- Formality / verb style: tabular, reverse chronological (Bundesagentur; AMS). No official source on verb style: assert none.

## Zeugnisse (employer certificates)
- A Zeugnis is evidence to attach, never to paraphrase on the CV; never write the codes.
