# Market: Germany

Part of the DACH group: shared points in [`dach-common.md`](dach-common.md). Weakly sourced points are labelled LOW confidence.

## At a glance
- Photo: the Bundesagentur describes a photo-free variant for employers who ask for anonymised CVs (Bundesagentur für Arbeit, « Bewerbung schreiben », n.d.).
- Length: max 2 A4 pages in Germany (Bundesagentur).

## Personal data and law
- Germany: the AGG says nothing about photos (AGG § 1 lists the grounds); optionality comes from the Bundesagentur's guidance, not from the statute.

## Protected titles
- Germany: « Ingenieur » is protected by Land law, not federal. NRW: a technical or natural-science programme of at least 3 years at a German Hochschule (or equivalent German Ingenieurschule), or a right granted by the authority; misuse is an Ordnungswidrigkeit (Land NRW, « Ingenieurgesetz – IngG », §§ 1, 8, S49). A foreign degree needs the Bezirksregierung's authorisation, granted when no substantial difference from the German programme exists (§ 2, S49). Other Länder not checked.

## Application package
- Cover letter: Germany, max one A4 page, no conditional (« würde », « könnte »), salary only if asked (Bundesagentur).
- Attachments: Germany: certificates and Arbeitszeugnisse of each former employer, scans only, never originals by post (Bundesagentur).

## Zeugnisse (employer certificates)
- Germany: § 109 GewO: on request, a « qualifiziertes Zeugnis » covers performance and conduct on top of type and duration, with no wording meant to convey a hidden meaning.
