# Market: Germany

Loaded on demand when the target market is Germany. Read together with [`dach-common.md`](dach-common.md) (shared DACH points).

## At a glance
- Typst flags: `--lang de --region de` for `pick_design.py` (written into `#set text(lang:, region:)`).
- Paper: not covered here (not checked); see [`dach-common.md`](dach-common.md)
- Photo: the Bundesagentur describes a photo-free variant for employers who ask for anonymised CVs (Bundesagentur für Arbeit, « Bewerbung schreiben », consulted 2026-11-07, S48).
- CV language: not covered here (not checked); see [`dach-common.md`](dach-common.md)
- Personal data: see « Personal data and law » below; the CV items to include are not covered here (not checked), see [`dach-common.md`](dach-common.md)
- Length: max 2 A4 pages in Germany (Bundesagentur für Arbeit, « Bewerbung schreiben », consulted 2026-11-07, S48).

## Personal data and law
- Germany: the AGG says nothing about photos (Bundesministerium der Justiz, « § 1 AGG Ziel des Gesetzes », consulted 2026-11-07, S47, lists the grounds); optionality comes from the Bundesagentur's guidance, not from the statute.

## Protected titles
- Germany: « Ingenieur » is protected by Land law, not federal. NRW: a technical or natural-science programme of at least 3 years at a German Hochschule (or equivalent German Ingenieurschule), or a right granted by the authority; misuse is an Ordnungswidrigkeit (Land Nordrhein-Westfalen, « Gesetz zum Schutze der Berufsbezeichnung Ingenieur/Ingenieurin (Ingenieurgesetz - IngG) », §§ 1, 8, consulted 2026-11-07, S49). A foreign degree needs the Bezirksregierung's authorisation, granted when no substantial difference from the German programme exists (§ 2, same source, S49). Other Länder not checked.

## Application package
- Cover letter: Germany, max one A4 page, no conditional (« würde », « könnte »), salary only if asked (Bundesagentur für Arbeit, « Bewerbung schreiben », consulted 2026-11-07, S48).
- Attachments: Germany: certificates and Arbeitszeugnisse of each former employer, scans only, never originals by post (Bundesagentur für Arbeit, « Bewerbung schreiben », consulted 2026-11-07, S48).

## Zeugnisse (employer certificates)
- Germany: § 109 GewO: on request, a « qualifiziertes Zeugnis » covers performance and conduct on top of type and duration, with no wording meant to convey a hidden meaning (Bundesministerium der Justiz, « § 109 GewO Zeugnis », consulted 2026-11-07, S46).
