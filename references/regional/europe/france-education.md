# Education: France

Loaded on demand when the education section has a work-integrated programme or a grade. Generic rules: [`references/education-cases.md`](../../education-cases.md).

## Work-integrated study (alternance)
- Label the paid employment as the contract says: « alternant » (contrat d'apprentissage or de professionnalisation). LOW, unsourced (convention).

## Grades
- Print the grade against its scale: "15/20"; never convert. LOW, unsourced (convention).
