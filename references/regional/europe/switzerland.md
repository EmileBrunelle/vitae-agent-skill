# Market: Switzerland

Loaded on demand when the target market is Switzerland. Read together with [`dach-common.md`](dach-common.md) (shared DACH points).

## At a glance
- Paper: not covered here (not checked); see [`dach-common.md`](dach-common.md)
- Photo: arbeit.swiss lists « personal details, including photograph » among CV contents (SECO / arbeit.swiss, « Tips on applying for jobs », consulted 2026-11-07, S509); the 2018 SECO dossier says add one only if the ad asks (SECO, « Wie bewerbe ich mich richtig? Bewerbungsdossier (Art. Nr. 716.209.d, 08.2018) », consulted 2026-11-07, S55). Ask the candidate.
- CV language: not covered here (not checked); see [`dach-common.md`](dach-common.md)
- Personal data: see « Personal data and law » below.
- Length: arbeit.swiss: « A CV should be no longer than three pages » (SECO / arbeit.swiss, « Tips on applying for jobs », consulted 2026-11-07, S509). Default to 2; go to 3 only if the content needs it (skill choice).

## Personal data and law
- Switzerland: the federal brochure lists « Staatsangehörigkeit » and « Aufenthaltsbewilligung » (bei Ausländern) among personal details, and its sample also shows birth date, Heimatort and Zivilstand (SECO, « Wie bewerbe ich mich richtig? Bewerbungsdossier (Art. Nr. 716.209.d, 08.2018) », a dated sample, consulted 2026-11-07, S55): Swiss custom: date of birth, nationality and marital status are optional, ask the candidate. Never invent a permit or work-authorisation status. Non-EU/EFTA hires require the employer to prove no suitable candidate in Switzerland or the EU/EFTA (SEM, « Nicht-EU/EFTA-Angehörige », 2026, consulted 2026-11-07, S57); EU/EFTA: permit L under 12 months, B from 12 months, G for cross-border commuters (SEM, « FAQ – Free Movement of Persons », consulted 2026-11-07, S58).

## Protected titles
- Switzerland: the architect profession is not regulated federally; seven cantons regulate who may sign building plans (SBFI/SERI, « Ausübung des Architektenberufs in der Schweiz », May 2023, consulted 2026-11-07, S512). No source found on « Ingenieur » title protection (cantonal law not checked): do not carry over the German or Austrian rules.

## Application package
- Attachments: Switzerland: Begleitbrief, CV, copies of all Arbeitszeugnisse and diplomas (SECO, « Wie bewerbe ich mich richtig? Bewerbungsdossier (Art. Nr. 716.209.d, 08.2018) », consulted 2026-11-07, S55); references only if the ad asks.

## Zeugnisse (employer certificates)
- Switzerland: Art. 330a OR: « Vollzeugnis » (performance and conduct) or « Arbeitsbestätigung » (type and duration only); truthful, no coded language (SECO, « FAQ zu Arbeitszeugnis », consulted 2026-11-07, S56).
- Switzerland: « ss », never « ß » (LOW, unsourced); « Heimatort » is a Swiss-only field.
