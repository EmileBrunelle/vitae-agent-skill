# Market: Switzerland

Part of the DACH group: shared points in [`dach-common.md`](dach-common.md). Weakly sourced points are labelled LOW confidence.

## At a glance
- Photo: Switzerland: add one only if the ad asks (SECO, « Wie bewerbe ich mich richtig? Bewerbungsdossier », 2018).
- Length: Switzerland: no official rule found (LOW confidence: 2 pages).

## Personal data and law
- Switzerland: the federal brochure lists « Staatsangehörigkeit » and « Aufenthaltsbewilligung » (bei Ausländern) among personal details, and its sample also shows birth date, Heimatort and Zivilstand (SECO, 2018, a dated sample): Swiss custom, the candidate decides. Never invent a permit or work-authorisation status. Non-EU/EFTA hires require the employer to prove no suitable candidate in Switzerland or the EU/EFTA (SEM, « Nicht-EU/EFTA-Angehörige », 2026); EU/EFTA: permit L under 12 months, B from 12 months, G for cross-border commuters (SEM, « FAQ – Free Movement of Persons », n.d.).

## Protected titles
- Switzerland: no authoritative source on title protection found (LOW confidence): do not carry over the German or Austrian rules.

## Application package
- Attachments: Switzerland: Begleitbrief, CV, copies of all Arbeitszeugnisse and diplomas (SECO, 2018); references only if the ad asks.

## Zeugnisse (employer certificates)
- Switzerland: Art. 330a OR: « Vollzeugnis » (performance and conduct) or « Arbeitsbestätigung » (type and duration only); truthful, no coded language (SECO, « FAQ zu Arbeitszeugnis », n.d.).
- Switzerland: « ss », never « ß » (LOW confidence); « Heimatort » is a Swiss-only field.
