# Education: United Kingdom

Loaded on demand when the education section has an exchange abroad, a degree in progress, or credits. Generic rules: [`references/education-cases.md`](../../education-cases.md); this file holds the UK wordings and sources.

## Exchange / study abroad
- A work placement or year abroad with employment goes in Experience (Kent, « Returning from a Year Abroad: Employability Workshop », consulted 2026-11: "you may wish to list this under 'Employment'"). Kent and Warwick (Warwick Careers, « CV example template ») differ on structure: Warwick puts the exchange on a separate line after the degree, Kent allows Education or Experience.

## Grades, degree in progress
- "predicted 2:1" or "grade to date", only if the transcript or a tutor supports it. Warwick template: "if still studying state predicted grade OR grade achieved to date"; University of Leeds, « Guidelines for writing CVs and covering letters »: "Include grades, actual and/or expected" (consulted 2026-11).

## Credits
- UK credits are CATS: print the count with "CATS", never convert to ECTS silently (labelling rule: convention).
