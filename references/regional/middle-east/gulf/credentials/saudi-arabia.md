# Gulf credentials: Saudi Arabia

Loaded on demand when the target employer is in Saudi Arabia and the candidate's degree or professional licence will be checked. Read together with [`gulf/credentials.md`](../credentials.md).

- **Engineers:** Saudi Council of Engineers accreditation is a stated element of the 2026 engineering Saudization decision (HRSD (Saudi Arabia), « Implementation Begins for the Decision to Raise the Saudization Rate in Engineering Professions as of 30 June », 2026, consulted 2026-11-07, S252); its registration grades and experience thresholds were not read from a primary source, so none is stated. Do not write an accreditation the candidate lacks.
- **Health practitioners (SCFHS), classification then registration** (Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS), « Professional classification requirements », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S258; SCFHS, « Professional Registration - Service Guide », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S259): the bachelor's must be authenticated when the application rests on a bachelor's alone, the higher degrees when it rests on postgraduate degrees; experience not below one year for non-Saudis (except graduates of Saudi universities); the professional registration certificate from the home country's official authority with a verification letter, or a Ministry of Health letter where no such body exists; an employment letter issued within three months proving clinical practice. Registration asks for a residence permit for non-Saudis. For the candidate: keep the home registration number and issuing body, and exact training dates.
- A permit or licence from another agency « shall not substitute » for the work permit (HRSD (Saudi Arabia), « Labor Law, Royal Decree No. M/51, September 27, 2005 », art. 34, 2023-02, consulted 2026-11-07, S241).
- Ministry of Education equivalency for foreign university degrees: no source found.
