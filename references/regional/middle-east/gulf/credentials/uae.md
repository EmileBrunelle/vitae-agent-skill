# Gulf credentials: UAE

Loaded on demand when the target employer is in the UAE and the candidate's degree or professional licence will be checked. Read together with [`gulf/credentials.md`](../credentials.md).

- **Attestation chain for a degree issued abroad:** authenticated in the country of origin by that country's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, then by the UAE embassy or consulate; « certain jobs » need it to complete residency and employment procedures (UAE Government portal (u.ae), « Preparing to work », 2026-09-24, consulted 2026-11-07, S242). The UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs attests the authenticity of signatures and seals, not the content of the degree (UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs, « Attestation of Official Documents and Certificates », undated, consulted 2026-11-07, S256).
- **Why it matters for the permit:** « skilled » classification needs a certificate above secondary level, attested by the competent authorities, and AED 4,000 or more a month (UAE Government portal (u.ae), « Professional levels of jobs in the UAE », 2026-08-12, consulted 2026-11-07, S248).
- **Ministry of Education recognition:** « some professions » need this Ministry's attestation of the university degree (UAE Government portal (u.ae), « Preparing to work », 2026-09-24, consulted 2026-11-07, S242). A 2023 Ministry announcement (current rules not checked) says the « University Certificates Recognition » system replaced « University Qualification Equivalency »: for universities ranked 1-200 verifying validity is enough, lower-ranked ones face more conditions, and the relaxed rules do **not** cover certificates in specialised professions such as engineering, medicine and law (UAE Ministry of Education, « MoE unveils the "University Certificates Recognition" system to replace the "University Qualification Equivalency" system for degrees issued by foreign higher education institutions », 2023-06-22, consulted 2026-11-07, S257).
- **Health:** physicians, lab technicians and others must pass local examinations set by the Ministry of Health and Prevention, the Dubai Health Authority or the Department of Health Abu Dhabi before being licensed to work (UAE Government portal (u.ae), « Preparing to work », 2026-09-24, consulted 2026-11-07, S242). CV: « licence application in progress » only if true; never « DHA-licensed » before issue. Exam names and timelines: not read, the candidate checks the authority. Resuscitation and specialty certifications (HSFC, AHA, CNA, CCRN, NREMT, PTCB): [`../../../../certifications/health.md`](../../../../certifications/health.md).
- Engineering licensing in the UAE: no source found. Ask the user.
