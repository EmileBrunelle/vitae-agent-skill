# Gulf credentials: UAE

- **Attestation chain for a degree issued abroad:** authenticated in the country of origin by that country's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, then by the UAE embassy or consulate; « certain jobs » need it to complete residency and employment procedures (S242). The UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs attests the authenticity of signatures and seals, not the content of the degree (S256).
- **Why it matters for the permit:** « skilled » classification needs a certificate above secondary level, attested by the competent authorities, and AED 4,000 or more a month (S248).
- **Ministry of Education recognition:** « some professions » need this Ministry's attestation of the university degree (S242). A 2023 Ministry announcement (2023-06-22; current rules unchecked) says the « University Certificates Recognition » system replaced « University Qualification Equivalency »: for universities ranked 1-200 verifying validity is enough, lower-ranked ones face more conditions, and the relaxed rules do **not** cover certificates in specialised professions such as engineering, medicine and law (S257).
- **Health:** physicians, lab technicians and others must pass local examinations set by the Ministry of Health and Prevention, the Dubai Health Authority or the Department of Health Abu Dhabi before being licensed to work (S242).
