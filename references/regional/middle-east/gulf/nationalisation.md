# Gulf: roles closed to or quota-limited for foreigners (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar)

Loaded when a target role may be on a list reserved for nationals (a quota alone does not change the CV). Read [`gulf/market.md`](market.md) first. Status **as of 2026-11-07**, from HRSD news items (Saudi) and the UAE portal; the full title lists sit in HRSD's procedural guides, which were not read. **A quota is not a ban**: only 100% lists and statutory restrictions close a role to foreigners. Rates, lists and dates change by ministerial decision: tell the user to check the current procedural guide before advising on a specific title.
