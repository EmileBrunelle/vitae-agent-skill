# Gulf: roles closed to or quota-limited for foreigners (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar)

Loaded when a target role may be on a list reserved for nationals (a quota alone does not change the CV). Read [`gulf/market.md`](market.md) first. Status **as of 2026-11-07**, from HRSD news items (Saudi) and the UAE portal; the full title lists sit in HRSD's procedural guides, which were not read. **A quota is not a ban**: an establishment must keep a percentage of Saudis in a listed occupation and expatriates fill the rest; only 100% lists and statutory restrictions close a role to foreigners. Rates, lists and dates change by ministerial decision: tell the user to check the current procedural guide before advising on a specific title.

## Saudi Arabia
- Legal frame: a non-Saudi works only on a work permit, which requires qualifications « which the country needs and which are not possessed by citizens » (or ordinary-worker status); the Minister issues a decision listing professions « prohibited for non-Saudis » (Labor Law 2005, arts. 33, 36; S241, English translation possibly not the latest amended text).
- **100%, role closed to expatriates:** 69 administrative-support professions added, effective 5 April 2026, by Saudi Standard Classification of Occupations title, in establishments employing one worker or more in them; fields named: secretarial, clerical, translation, data entry, administrative support (S249). Tourism: Receptionist, Hotel Receptionist, Telephone Operator, Information Clerk, effective 22 April 2026 (S250). A secretarial, clerical, translation, data-entry or reception target is therefore a flag; whether the exact title is listed needs the guide.
- **Quotas (still hireable, but the employer must hold the percentage, so expect Saudi competition):**
  - Engineering: 25% from 21 July 2024 in establishments with 5+ workers in the covered titles (S251); 30% for 46 titles from 30 June 2026 (5+ workers; titles named include Architectural, Industrial, Electronics, Marine, Oil and Gas, Aerospace Engineer), and the decision also requires Saudi Council of Engineers accreditation (S252). Whether the 25% still applies to titles outside the 46 was not read.
  - Project management (Manager, Engineer, Specialist): 70% from 14 February 2027, establishments with 3+ workers in them (S253).
  - Tourism: 12 titles at 70% and 12 at 50% from 22 April 2026 (S250).
  - Four health professions: radiology 65%, medical laboratories 70%, clinical nutrition and physical therapy 80%; minimum monthly wage SAR 7,000 (specialists) and SAR 5,000 (technicians). Phase 1 from 17 April 2025 in Riyadh, Makkah, Madinah, Jeddah, Dammam, Khobar and in large and mega establishments elsewhere; phase 2 (all other establishments) scheduled for 17 October 2025, its start not checked (S254).
- Nitaqat bands were not read: do not describe them.

## UAE
- The only role the UAE portal names as practised by UAE nationals only is the lawyer licensed to appear before the court (S242). Citizen-participation rules are exempt from the discrimination ban (art. 4(2); S240). An Emiratisation programme exists (S255) but **no quota percentage or threshold was read from a primary page: none is stated here.**

## Qatar
- No quota, list or reserved role was read: do not state any Qatarization percentage or reserved role.

## What to do in the deliverable
- Title in a Saudi 100% list: tell the user the role is likely closed to non-Saudis and propose the nearest open title (never rename the candidate's real role to dodge a list).
- Title in a quota list: no change to the CV; mention the licence/accreditation expectation in the application notes.
- UAE and Qatar: no reserved-role warning beyond the lawyer case can be sourced; say so rather than guess.
