# Gulf: degree attestation, equivalency and licences for expatriates (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar)

Loaded when the candidate's degree or professional licence will be checked (always for engineering, health, law, and for any UAE « skilled » work permit). Read [`gulf/market.md`](market.md) first. Same rule as [`india/to-europe/common.md`](../../india/to-europe/common.md): the CV states the credential as issued and its verification status; fees, queues and procedures live in the application, not the CV. Never write « equivalent to a UAE/Saudi/Qatari degree » unless an evaluation says so, in its own words. Read 2026-11-07.

## UAE
- **Attestation chain for a degree issued abroad:** authenticated in the country of origin by that country's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, then by the UAE embassy or consulate; « certain jobs » need it to complete residency and employment procedures (S242). The UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs attests the authenticity of signatures and seals, not the content of the degree (S256).
- **Why it matters for the permit:** « skilled » classification needs a certificate above secondary level, attested by the competent authorities, and AED 4,000 or more a month (S248).
- **Ministry of Education recognition:** « some professions » need this Ministry's attestation of the university degree (S242). A 2023 Ministry announcement (2023-06-22; current rules unchecked) says the « University Certificates Recognition » system replaced « University Qualification Equivalency »: for universities ranked 1-200 verifying validity is enough, lower-ranked ones face more conditions, and the relaxed rules do **not** cover certificates in specialised professions such as engineering, medicine and law (S257).
- **Health:** physicians, lab technicians and others must pass local examinations set by the Ministry of Health and Prevention, the Dubai Health Authority or the Department of Health Abu Dhabi before being licensed to work (S242). CV: « licence application in progress » only if true; never « DHA-licensed » before issue. Exam names and timelines: not read, the candidate checks the authority. Resuscitation and specialty certifications (HSFC, AHA, CNA, CCRN, NREMT, PTCB): [`../../../certifications/health.md`](../../../certifications/health.md).
- Engineering licensing in the UAE: no source found. Ask the user.

## Saudi Arabia
- **Engineers:** Saudi Council of Engineers accreditation is a stated element of the 2026 engineering Saudization decision (S252); its registration grades and experience thresholds were not read from a primary source, so none is stated. Do not write an accreditation the candidate lacks.
- **Health practitioners (SCFHS), classification then registration** (S258, S259): the bachelor's must be authenticated when the application rests on a bachelor's alone, the higher degrees when it rests on postgraduate degrees; experience not below one year for non-Saudis (except graduates of Saudi universities); the professional registration certificate from the home country's official authority with a verification letter, or a Ministry of Health letter where no such body exists; an employment letter issued within three months proving clinical practice. Registration asks for a residence permit for non-Saudis. For the candidate: keep the home registration number and issuing body, and exact training dates.
- A permit or licence from another agency « shall not substitute » for the work permit (Labor Law art. 34; S241).
- Ministry of Education equivalency for foreign university degrees: not sourced.

## Qatar
- Degree equivalency and attestation, health licensing and engineering licensing: not sourced; ask the user.
- Qatar's amended Labour Law bars a foreign worker who leaves the country without honouring the probation or notice rules from a new work permit for one year (Law 18/2020; S245): relevant to a candidate who left Qatar abruptly; the CV states nothing about it.
