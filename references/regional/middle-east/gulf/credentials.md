# Gulf: degree attestation, equivalency and licences for expatriates (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar)

Loaded when the candidate's degree or professional licence will be checked (always for engineering, health, law, and for any UAE « skilled » work permit). Read [`gulf/market.md`](market.md) first. Same rule as [`india/to-europe/common.md`](../../india/to-europe/common.md): the CV states the credential as issued and its verification status; fees, queues and procedures live in the application, not the CV. Never write « equivalent to a UAE/Saudi/Qatari degree » unless an evaluation says so, in its own words. Read 2026-11-07.
