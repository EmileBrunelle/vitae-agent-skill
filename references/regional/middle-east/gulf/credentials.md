# Gulf credentials: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar

Loaded on demand when the candidate's degree or professional licence will be checked (always for engineering, health, law, and for any UAE « skilled » work permit). Read together with [`gulf/market.md`](market.md). Same rule as [`india/outbound/europe-common.md`](../../asia/india/outbound/europe-common.md): the CV states the credential as issued and its verification status; fees, queues and procedures live in the application, not the CV.

## Scope

Never write « equivalent to a UAE/Saudi/Qatari degree » unless an evaluation says so, in its own words. Consulted 2026-11-07.
