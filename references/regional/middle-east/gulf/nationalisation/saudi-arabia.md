# Gulf nationalisation: Saudi Arabia

Loaded on demand when the target employer is in Saudi Arabia and a role may be reserved for Saudis or quota-limited. Read together with [`gulf/nationalisation.md`](../nationalisation.md).

## Scope

A quota is not a ban: an establishment must keep a percentage of Saudis in a listed occupation and expatriates fill the rest.

## Rules by category
- Legal frame: a non-Saudi works only on a work permit, which requires qualifications « which the country needs and which are not possessed by citizens » (or ordinary-worker status); the Minister issues a decision listing professions « prohibited for non-Saudis » (HRSD (Saudi Arabia), « Labor Law, Royal Decree No. M/51, September 27, 2005 », arts. 33, 36, 2023-02, consulted 2026-11-07, S241; English translation possibly not the latest amended text).
- **100%, role closed to expatriates:** 69 administrative-support professions added, effective 5 April 2026, by Saudi Standard Classification of Occupations title, in establishments employing one worker or more in them; fields named: secretarial, clerical, translation, data entry, administrative support (HRSD (Saudi Arabia), « The Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development announces an update to the decision on the Saudization of Administrative Support Professions », 2026-05-03, consulted 2026-11-07, S249). Tourism: Receptionist, Hotel Receptionist, Telephone Operator, Information Clerk, effective 22 April 2026 (HRSD (Saudi Arabia), « … commencement of the first phase of implementing the Saudization decision for tourism professions in the private sector », 2026-05-03, consulted 2026-11-07, S250). A secretarial, clerical, translation, data-entry or reception target is therefore a flag; whether the exact title is listed needs the guide.
- **Quotas (still hireable, but the employer must hold the percentage, so expect Saudi competition):**
  - Engineering: 25% from 21 July 2024 in establishments with 5+ workers in the covered titles (HRSD (Saudi Arabia), « Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development, in partnership with the Ministry of Municipal and Rural Affairs and Housing, Announces Implementation of the Decision to Saudize Engineering Professions », 2024-08-26, consulted 2026-11-07, S251); 30% for 46 titles from 30 June 2026 (5+ workers; titles named include Architectural, Industrial, Electronics, Marine, Oil and Gas, Aerospace Engineer), and the decision also requires Saudi Council of Engineers accreditation (HRSD (Saudi Arabia), « Implementation Begins for the Decision to Raise the Saudization Rate in Engineering Professions as of 30 June », 2026, consulted 2026-11-07, S252). Whether the 25% still applies to titles outside the 46 was not read.
  - Project management (Manager, Engineer, Specialist): 70% from 14 February 2027, establishments with 3+ workers in them (HRSD (Saudi Arabia), « … Decision to Raise the Saudization Rate for Project Management Professions to 70% », 2026-09-09, consulted 2026-11-07, S253).
  - Tourism: 12 titles at 70% and 12 at 50% from 22 April 2026 (HRSD (Saudi Arabia), « … commencement of the first phase of implementing the Saudization decision for tourism professions in the private sector », 2026-05-03, consulted 2026-11-07, S250).
  - Four health professions: radiology 65%, medical laboratories 70%, clinical nutrition and physical therapy 80%; minimum monthly wage SAR 7,000 (specialists) and SAR 5,000 (technicians). Phase 1 from 17 April 2025 in Riyadh, Makkah, Madinah, Jeddah, Dammam, Khobar and in large and mega establishments elsewhere; phase 2 (all other establishments) scheduled for 17 October 2025, its start not checked (HRSD (Saudi Arabia), « HRSD and MOH Announce the Implementation of Increased Saudization rates for Four Health Professions in the Private Sector », 2025-04-17, consulted 2026-11-07, S254).
- Nitaqat bands were not read: do not describe them.

## What to do in the deliverable
- Title in a Saudi 100% list: tell the user the role is likely closed to non-Saudis and propose the nearest open title (never rename the candidate's real role to dodge a list).
- Title in a quota list: no change to the CV; mention the licence/accreditation expectation in the application notes.
