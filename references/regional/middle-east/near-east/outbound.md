# Near East credentials (Egypt, Lebanon, Jordan), other markets

Loaded on demand when the candidate holds Egyptian, Lebanese or Jordanian credentials (alone or mixed with other countries') and targets Québec/Canada or Europe, in addition to the target market's file. The Maghreb outbound files already carry the destination-side rules, which are not country-specific and are not repeated: [`../../africa/maghreb-outbound/common.md`](../../africa/maghreb-outbound/common.md) (print the title as on the diploma, never convert a level, never « = Bac+5 », campus abroad), [`../../africa/maghreb-outbound/to-quebec-canada.md`](../../africa/maghreb-outbound/to-quebec-canada.md) (MIFI, IRCC ECA, OIQ: read « Maghreb » as « Egypt, Lebanon or Jordan » only for the procedures; whether an OIQ agreement covers these countries was not checked) and [`../../africa/maghreb-outbound/to-europe.md`](../../africa/maghreb-outbound/to-europe.md) (France: no legal equivalence, attestation de comparabilité). General cases (transfers, stopped studies, grades with their scale): [`references/education-cases.md`](../../../education-cases.md). The Gulf as destination is covered elsewhere: `middle-east/gulf/`. Every title below is Nuffic's catalogue of what the countries issue (Nuffic, the Dutch ENIC-NARIC, pages undated, accessed 2026-11-07). Lebanon and Jordan each rest on one Nuffic landing page and Egypt on three Nuffic pages, with no ministry cross-check; Nuffic's comparisons to Dutch levels are for Dutch readers and never go on a CV.

## What the CV must make visible
- **Duration, because the same title covers several lengths.** Lebanon: « Bachelor / Licence » (بكالوريوس / الإجازة) listed with durations of 3, 4 and 5 years; « Licence Technique » (الاجازة الفنية) and « Master » are separate entries, and Nuffic gives no duration for the Licence Technique (S260). Jordan: Bachelor's degree (البكالوريوس) of 4, 5 or (medicine) 6 years (S261). Egypt: university bachelor usually 4 years, some 5 (often technical programmes), medicine 6; a bachelor of a *Higher Institute* is a different diploma, usually 4 years, sometimes 5 or 6; the university bachelor is « sometimes called: Licence / ليسانس » (S262). So print the title exactly as the parchment carries it (« Licence » or « Bachelor »), the institution, the country and the years; never the bare title when the parchment or transcript shows a duration.
- **Egypt, three look-alikes** (S262): « Higher Diploma of Technology » (دبلوم عالي في التكنولوجيا, 3 years, Higher Institute, work or access to bachelor's programmes); « Diploma of Higher Studies » (دبلوم الدراسات العليا, 1 or 2 years, awarded after a « master's programme » at a Higher Institute, admission with any bachelor's degree, function: work); university « Master of Arts / of Science », at least 2 years, coursework and thesis or thesis only. The first two are neither a bachelor's nor a master's degree: print them as issued.
- **Egypt, documents.** The secondary diploma and its transcript are one document, and the grand total is what universities use for admission; higher-education institutions use different grading systems, often per faculty, with the scale « often displayed on the reverse side of the transcript » (S263). Print a grade only with the institution's own scale, never converted (`references/education-cases.md`).

## Egypt: military service status (single source, second-hand)
- UK Home Office country policy note « Egypt: Military service », version 3.0, June 2025 (S264, section 14.1), relays two statements. DFAT (Australia, 2019): a man over 18 who seeks employment without having completed service « generally has to produce an exemption certificate from the Ministry of Defence to his employer ». A Human Rights Watch researcher (email, February 2025): employment can be affected, especially in government jobs, because the completion or exemption certificate is required for any job application, and « Private sector companies are also required by law to request this document and maintain a copy of it as part of the employment process for any young man ». Neither is the statute: LOW confidence on the legal basis.
- It supports only « Egyptian employers collect a certificate at hiring ». It does not support a line on the CV, nor that a Canadian, French or other destination employer wants it. Default for outbound: omit; tell the candidate it is a home-market document, and ask whether the posting (or an Egyptian employer) requests it.
