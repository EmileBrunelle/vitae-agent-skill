# Jordan as a destination for a foreign applicant

Loaded on demand when a candidate who is not a Jordanian national targets a job in Jordan. Only what the Ministry of Labour's own texts say (read 2026-11-07); local-market CV conventions for nationals are not covered (no source loaded). Credentials the candidate brings from Egypt, Lebanon or Jordan: [`outbound.md`](outbound.md). The route is **employer-led**: the CV is at most a supporting document in the employer's permit file, so it must match the occupation named in that file.

- **Employer applies, not the worker.** Under the Ministry's « Instructions for 2012 … and Their Amendments » (undated, amended to an unstated date: current practice may differ; S265, art. 4) the employer files the signed recruitment form with the contract, the business licence and the worker's passport; on the worker's entry a non-conviction certificate from the country of origin is added.
- **Closed professions and quotas.** Permits follow labour-market needs, « taking into account the list of closed professions », and the Ministry may cap the share of non-Jordanians in any economic sector « in a manner that serves the policy of Jordanization of workers » (S265, art. 10). The list itself was not retrieved from an official file and no profession is named here: ask the employer or check the Ministry's current guide.
- **Skilled route.** « Principles for Issuing Work Permits for Non-Jordanian Workers with Specialized Skills for the Year 2024 » (S266; the copy read is undated and revokes the 2021 principles, art. 12): the Minister issues, and may amend, a list of specialized-skills professions (art. 4); permits run one year, six months or three months; the sector quotas do not apply (art. 8); the holder may not move to a profession outside the list (art. 9).
- **CV in the file.** For a first-time specialized-skills permit the employer attaches « A copy of the worker's CV or a letter from the employer confirming that the worker possesses the required expertise to perform the work », and, « if necessary », an official recommendation letter from the relevant authority; the worker must pass a medical examination recorded in the national system before issue (S266, art. 6). Inference: the CV's job title should match the listed profession.
- Not retrieved: the specialized-skills profession list, professional-body requirements (for example the Jordan Engineers Association), diploma attestation. The permit fees are in art. 7 of the Principles (S266).
