# Türkiye as a destination for a foreign applicant

Loaded on demand when a candidate who is not a Turkish national targets a job in Türkiye. Only what the Ministry of Labour and Social Security's own pages say (Directorate General of International Labour Force, pages undated, read 2026-11-07); Turkish CV conventions, diploma recognition (YÖK) for a foreign degree and military service are not covered (no source loaded; military service concerns Turkish nationals only). Credentials the candidate brings from Egypt, Lebanon or Jordan: [`outbound.md`](outbound.md). The route is **employer-led**: the CV supports the employer's permit file, so it must match the occupation named in that file.

- **Permit before work.** Under Law No. 6735 on International Labour Force the types are: definite (first permit up to one year, tied to a workplace, job and employer; a different employer counts as a first application), permanent (after a long-term residence permit or eight years of legal work permit, not an absolute right), independent (on the foreigner's own account) and the Turquoise Card (S267).
- **Salary floors, as published** (S268, « Work Permit Evaluation Criteria », section A.3, as of 2026-11-07; the page carries clauses marked effective March and August 2026, so re-check before relying on it): remuneration at least a multiple of the current gross minimum wage at the application date: five times for senior executives and pilots, **four times for engineers and architects**, three for other managers, twice for jobs « requiring expertise and mastery », once for domestic work and other jobs. The multiplier follows the category the job falls under: inference, the job title on the CV and in the contract should not be shifted between categories (engineer to manager or the reverse) for presentation.
- **Exemptions and pre-permits** (S268). For balance-sheet businesses the criterion is at least five Turkish citizens employed per foreigner, with exemptions. For expert roles in the IT industry (for example software development) the employment and financial criteria are not applied, and outside IT the same exemption covers up to two foreigners per workplace; the page lists these exemptions for the employment and financial criteria only, not the salary floor. A permit granted on a sector-specific exemption is evaluated negatively if the person changes employer, industry or job within six months (except force majeure; section B.12). Foreign staff in educational institutions need a pre-permit from the Ministry of National Education or the Council of Higher Education, and in health institutions from the Ministry of Health, for professions requiring professional qualification. The page mentions « professions reserved for Turkish citizens » without listing them: not retrieved.
- **Turquoise Card** (S269). Assessed on education, professional experience, contribution to science and technology, economic and employment impact and the advisory board's recommendations; first a three-year transition period, then indefinite; spouse and dependent children receive a document serving as residence permit.
