# Market: Brazil and Latin America

Brazil (currículo, pt-BR) plus short notes for Mexico, Argentina, Chile and Colombia (es). *Non-interchangeable* markets: read the country section, never an average. The four Spanish-speaking notes rest on one statute each; everything else there is LOW confidence.

## At a glance
- Paper: a4 in Brazil (Argentina LOW); letter for Mexico, Chile, Colombia (all LOW confidence).
- Photo: Brazil omit (Gupy says no photo); Chile not required, do not add by default; Mexico, Argentina, Colombia unverified (LOW confidence).
- CV language: pt-BR (EN if the posting is in EN); es-MX, es-AR, es-CL, es-CO.
- Personal data: Brazil, name, city/state, phone/WhatsApp, e-mail, LinkedIn; omit CPF/RG, estado civil, children. Spanish-speaking markets: omit protected data (statutes below).
- Formality / verb style: no sourced rule; neutral-formal.
- Length: 1-2 pages everywhere (LOW confidence).

## Brazil
- Vagas.com lists « estado civil, quantidade de filhos, endereço completo ou número de documentos, como RG ou CPF » as excess that exposes the candidate to fraud (Vagas.com, « O que não colocar no currículo? Saiba como evitar erros », n.d.). CPF and RG never go on the CV.
- Photo: Gupy says « Não insira imagens nem fotos » (Gupy, « Veja dicas e boas práticas para o seu currículo na Gupy », 2026-10-29). Vagas.com: under the LGPD a photo can reveal racial or ethnic origin; requesting one « ainda não traz implicações legais, mas é uma prática antiética »; a bill (PL 187/21) to ban the request was pending (Vagas.com, « Devo colocar uma foto no currículo? Saiba quais as regras sobre esse recurso », n.d.). Default: no photo; add only if the posting asks, and say so to the user.
- Law: LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018) governs candidate data, enforced by the ANPD (ANPD, « Titular de Dados », n.d.).
- Gupy reads the **fields entered in the Gupy profile, not the uploaded file** (« as empresas só vão ler as informações inseridas no cadastro da Gupy, não no arquivo do currículo »): tell the user the profile fields must match the CV (Gupy, « boas práticas », 2026-10-29).
- Counter-intuitive for a PDF pipeline: Gupy says « Formatos em PDF são lidos como imagem e não como texto » and recommends DOC/DOCX; single-column layouts read better (same article). Offer a DOCX/plain-text export alongside the PDF when the application runs through Gupy; the PDF claim is Gupy's own and unmeasured here.
- Protected titles: engenheiro, arquiteto and engenheiro-agrônomo are reserved to professionals registered with the CREA, with the training qualifier (« engenheiro civil »); Lei 5.194/1966 (Senado Federal, « Lei nº 5.194, de 24/12/1966 », consulted 2026-11; LOW confidence on article numbers). Contador requires CRC registration (Decreto-Lei 9.295/1946, art. 12, as reproduced by CFC, « Para quem se destina o CRC? », n.d.).
- Never write « engenheiro », « arquiteto » or « contador » as a title unless the candidate confirms CREA/CAU/CRC registration; use the function title with the degree in parentheses. Whether CREA applies to IT titles (« engenheiro de software ») is unsettled: flag, do not rule. Other councils (OAB, CRM, CRP, COREN): not verified; check the council.

## Spanish-speaking Latin America
- Mexico: Ley Federal del Trabajo art. 133 I forbids refusing workers on grounds including « edad », « religión » and « estado civil » (Cámara de Diputados, « Ley Federal del Trabajo », última reforma DOF 14-05-2026). Omit age, civil status and religion by default; do not claim « Ingeniero » or « Licenciado » beyond the degree held (cédula profesional: LOW confidence).
- Argentina: Ley 25.326: « Ninguna persona puede ser obligada a proporcionar datos sensibles » (racial/ethnic origin, political opinions, religion, union membership, health, sexual life); do not put such data on the CV (Argentina.gob.ar, « Ley 25.326 », 2000).
- Chile: Código del Trabajo art. 2: discrimination is any « distinción, exclusión o preferencia » based on grounds including « edad, estado civil … apariencia personal … nacionalidad »; job offers listing them as requirements are themselves discriminatory acts (Dirección del Trabajo, « Código del Trabajo », edición 2026-11, art. 2 and notes). Art. 2 exempts distinctions based on « calificaciones exigidas para un empleo determinado ». Age, marital status and photo stay off the CV by default; if a posting demands them, tell the user.
- Colombia: Ley 842 de 2003: practising engineering requires the matrícula (COPNIA), and « el presentarse o anunciarse como ingeniero » counts as practice (art. 2); unregistered practice is sanctionable (arts. 13, 15) (Colombia Compra Eficiente, « Ley 842 de 2003 », 2003-10-09). A degree-holder without matrícula writes the degree (« Ingeniería Civil, Universidad X »), not the title « Ingeniero », unless the candidate confirms it.
