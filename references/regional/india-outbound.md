# Indian credentials, other markets

Loaded on demand, in addition to the target market's file, when the candidate holds Indian credentials or Indian CV habits and targets the USA, Canada, UK, Ireland, Australia or New Zealand. The target market's file still applies; this add-on covers only what is specific to Indian credentials and habits. Grade and title conventions at home: [`india.md`](india.md).

## Drop Indian-format blocks
- Drop the Declaration and Personal Details blocks and Class X/XII marks for experienced candidates; CTC and notice period stay off the CV: see [`india.md`](india.md) and the target market file.

## Degree wording
- Write the degree exactly as awarded in English form (B.Sc., B.Com., BCA, B.E./B.Tech.). State only a verifiable length ("3-year program"); never write "equivalent to a US/4-year degree".
- Affiliated college: awarding university first, college in parentheses: "B.Sc. Physics, University of Delhi (Hindu College)", so the CV names the body that awards the degree (LOW confidence: inference).
- Grades: as printed, with scale and class/division (« CGPA 8.4/10, First Division »); never convert. Keep "First Division"/"Division I": it matters for WES's 3-year rule below.

## Three-year bachelor's
- When a posting or form requires a 4-year degree and the candidate holds an evaluation report, quote its wording verbatim; never promise an outcome. WES may treat a 3-year Indian bachelor's as a US bachelor's only if « The degrees have been earned in Division I » and the institution is « accredited by India's National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) » with a grade of "A" or better (WES, « Understanding the Three-Year Indian Bachelor's Degree in the U.S. » (2024-01-19), consulted 2026-11).
- UK ENIC, since Sept 2025: "3 years/120+ credits" = UK Bachelor degree; "4+ years/160+ credits" = UK Bachelor (Honours); both FHEQ level 6 (UK ENIC, « Revised UK ENIC comparisons for Indian Bachelor degrees » (2025-09-30), consulted 2026-11).
- USA, H-1B with a 3-year degree: routes in [`europe-to-north-america.md`](europe-to-north-america.md#credential-evaluation). Give exact experience dates (month+year); equivalence is for an evaluator or immigration counsel, never written by the CV.
- Canada: ECA rules in [`europe-to-north-america.md`](europe-to-north-america.md#credential-evaluation); for Indian degrees, the evaluation report states the Canadian equivalence, quoted as reported.
- Australia: a skills assessment only if held, in the body's own wording ("ACS Migration Skills Assessment: suitable, ANZSCO <code>, <date>"). « Suitable outcomes are valid for 2 years » (ACS, « InfoHub - Assessment Process » (© 2025), consulted 2026-11): drop the line once expired.

## Professional titles
- Chartered Accountant: ICAI membership rule in [`india.md`](india.md). CPA Australia / CA ANZ only once membership is confirmed: the ICAI-CPA Australia agreement is « a mutual pathway to dual professional recognition », not a membership by itself (CPA Australia, « CPA Australia and ICAI renew Mutual Recognition Agreement » (undated), consulted 2026-11).
- Engineers: "B.E./B.Tech. (NBA-accredited)" only if the candidate confirms the program is accredited. Make no Washington Accord claim unless the candidate shows that their program and graduation year are covered (LOW confidence: scope not verified; no source loaded). Protected-title rules of the target market apply ([`north-america.md`](north-america.md), [`australia-nz.md`](australia-nz.md)).
