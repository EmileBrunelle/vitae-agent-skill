# Certifications: language certificates

Loaded with [`rules.md`](rules.md) when the candidate lists a language test or diploma. Each fact is what the publisher's own page says (consulted 2026-11-07) and holds for that test only. JLPT, TOPIK, TOCFL: [`../regional/asia/east-asia/japan.md`](../regional/asia/east-asia/japan.md), [`korea.md`](../regional/asia/east-asia/korea.md), [`taiwan.md`](../regional/asia/east-asia/taiwan.md); Catalan to CEFR: [`../regional/europe/spain.md`](../regional/europe/spain.md); CEFR as plain text: [`../ats.md`](../ats.md). Not read: DELF/DALF and TOEFL verification.

## Print the exact test, the scores as issued, the date
- Name the variant: "IELTS Academic" or "IELTS General Training" (same Speaking and Listening, different Reading and Writing) (S365); "TCF tout public", "TCF Canada" or "TCF Québec" (different result layouts) (S360); "TEF Canada" (S363); "TOEFL iBT"; "TOEIC Listening and Reading" (Speaking and Writing: separate tests, 0 to 200 each, "No total score is reported") (S372).
- A level with no test ("French, B2") is the candidate's own claim, not a certificate; never attach a test the candidate did not give (SKILL.md rule 1).

## Diplomas that do not expire
- DELF tout public (A1 to B2) and DALF (C1, C2): official diplomas of the French ministry of national education, "valable à vie" (S356, S357). Each level is an independent diploma: "DELF B2", not "DELF/DALF". The diploma is issued "en cas d'admissibilité"; otherwise the centre may supply a "relevé de notes" (S358), which is not a diploma.
- Cambridge English: "Your Cambridge English certificate does not expire. Recognising organisations ... can choose how long to accept results for" (S374). Results are on the Cambridge English Scale, with a grade and a CEFR level per exam. Scores of 142 to 159 (C1 Advanced) and 162 to 179 (C2 Proficiency) get no certificate, only a Statement of Results (S374). Print exam, score and grade, not the exam name alone. Recipients verify with the reference number plus date of birth (S374, S375).

## Results with a stated validity window
- TCF tout public: the attestation "aura une durée de validité de 2 ans à partir de la date de délivrance des résultats", validity date printed on it (S359). It carries an attestation number, an expiry date and a QR code that "permet d'authentifier les résultats" (S360). Per-skill scores are translated to CEFR levels from "niveau A1 non atteint" to C2; a global level exists "si et uniquement si toutes les épreuves ... sont passées lors d'une seule et même session" (S360). Print per-skill levels; a global level only if the attestation gives one.
- TEF: "une certification valable deux ans à partir de la date de passation" (S361); TEF Canada: "valide 2 ans (à compter de la date d'émission de l'attestation)" (S363). The two pages count from different dates: print the attestation's date, compute no end date. CCI table, score out of 699: bands of 100, from 0 to 99 below A1 to 600 to 699 C2 (S362).
- IELTS: "We recommend that IELTS test results are considered valid for two years after a test has been taken"; organisations "can choose to accept results for a longer period" (S366). IELTS also recommends that a report over two years old "should only be accepted if it is accompanied by proof that the test taker has actively maintained, or tried to improve, their English" (S367). Print overall band, four section bands and date (S366). Recipients verify by Test Report Form number in the IELTS Results Service (S367); print it only if the candidate supplies it.
- TOEFL iBT: "Your scores are valid for 2 years after your test date" (S370). Since 21 January 2026 reports use 1 to 6 in half points (four section scores, overall = average rounded to the nearest half band); earlier tests use 0 to 120, sections 0 to 30 (S368). Print the scale used; do not convert. ETS aligns 6 to C2, 5 and 5.5 to C1, 4 and 4.5 to B2, 3 and 3.5 to B1 (S369). MyBest scores, a separate section of the score report that converts older 0 to 120 scores to 1 to 6, are accepted at each institution's discretion (S368): label such a figure.
- TOEIC Listening and Reading: Listening and Reading 5 to 495 each, total 10 to 990 (S371). Public session: "You can use these scores globally"; institutional session: they are "not globally portable for a test taker" (S373), and a score report from one is "only valid within the issuing institution" (S371): leave an employer-administered score off a CV sent elsewhere. "a score report will not be re-issued if two years have passed since taking the test" (S371).

## CEFR: the publisher's own mapping only
- Each publisher maps its own scores. ETS on TOEIC: the minimum scores "should be considered guidelines, as ETS does not recommend the use of rigid cut scores" (Listening 275 B1, 400 B2, 490 C1; Reading 275, 385, 455) (S372). Print the score, and a level only as that publisher assigns it; never convert with another publisher's table.

## Canada
- Pointer: IRCC's approved tests for Express Entry include TEF Canada, TCF Canada and IELTS ("You must take the IELTS General Training option"), with results "less than 2 years old" at profile and application (S364). CLB and NCLC equivalence is IRCC's table: print test and scores, not a converted level.
