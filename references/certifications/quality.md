# Certifications: quality and operations bodies

Loaded with [`rules.md`](rules.md) when the candidate lists a Six Sigma belt, an ASQ credential (CQE, CQA), an ISO management-system auditor credential, or an APICS/ASCM credential. Each fact is what the body's page says (consulted 2026-11-07; ASQ, ISO, CQI/IRCA and ASCM through Wayback captures) and holds for that credential only. Not read: ASQ CSSGB page, CQI/IRCA renewal, IASSC validity, ASCM verification, ISO pages on individuals.

## Belts: name the awarding body
- No loaded source says one body is the authority for belts: ASQ and IASSC each publish their own Black, Green and Yellow Belt exams (S459, S460). A bare "Six Sigma Black Belt" names no body: print the body.
- Belt from an employer or training provider: no body loaded prescribes wording; suggested (not sourced): "Green Belt, `<employer or provider>`, 2022", never the ASQ or IASSC mark.
- ASQ: "Brown Belt is not traditionally used in Six Sigma and is not recognized by most organizations or accrediting agencies"; some organizations use it for a Green Belt who passed the Black Belt exam (S461). Print it only as the issuer's own title, with the issuer.

## ASQ (CSSBB, CSSGB, CSSYB, CQE, CQA)
- Registry names: "Certified Six Sigma Black Belt (CSSBB)", "Certified Six Sigma Green Belt (CSSGB)", "Certified Six Sigma Yellow Belt (CSSYB)", "Certified Quality Engineer (CQE)", "Certified Quality Auditor (CQA)", "Certified Master Black Belt (MBB)" (S462); the catalogue says "(CMBB)" (S459).
- Certification is not a course: ASQ does "not require you to take the ASQ training"; it contrasts certification (an exam, with experience or education prerequisites) with "paper certificates awarded for participating in specific training" (S463, undated). CSSBB: a full-time, paid role, "3 Years of on-the-job experience", and one completed project with signed affidavit, or two (S459).
- Validity, per ASQ's recertification page (capture of 2026-07-04): "recertify ... every three years" for Six Sigma Black Belt, Quality Engineer, Quality Auditor and others, by 18 recertification units or by exam; "Six Sigma Green Belt, and Six Sigma Yellow Belt are lifetime certifications" (S464).
- Lapse: the person "must immediately discontinue use of the associated acronym and is prohibited from stating or implying certification is current" (S464). Dated entry only, per rules.md.
- Verification: the ASQ Certification Registry; "Inclusion in the registry is voluntary" (S462).

## IASSC
- Marks: "IASSC Certified Black Belt™", Green Belt, Yellow Belt. Correct: "John Doe, IASSC Certified Black Belt™"; not allowed: "John Doe is an ICBB (IASSC Certified Black Belt)" (S465). The "IASSC Accredited ..." marks are a separate family, "intended to be used with an accredited entity's name" (S465).
- Verification: a request form; its database covers "all professionals who have sat for IASSC Certification Exams", not those certified through non-affiliated providers (S466). Validity: not found.

## ISO management-system auditors
- "ISO does not perform certification or issue certificates ... a company or organization cannot be certified by ISO" (S467). The page speaks of organizations. "ISO certified" names no certifier: print the personnel-certification body and its grade.
- CQI/IRCA grades (pages captured March-May 2025): Associate Auditor, Internal Auditor, Auditor, Lead Auditor, Principal Auditor. Associate: "undertaken an IRCA approved auditing course", names "on the IRCA register to demonstrate that they have been trained to an IRCA approved standard", no auditing experience required (S468). Auditor: an "IRCA Certified Auditor/Lead Auditor course" plus "a minimum of four management system audits" within five years of applying (S468). Lead Auditor: also three audits as team leader (S468). Principal Auditor: "extensive and demonstrable experience of auditing" but "no longer carry out audits or lead audit teams routinely" (S469).
- The scheme page says the audit experience "will determine your auditor certification grade" and that the QMS scheme "is for auditors working with ISO 9001:2015" (S470). Suggested (not sourced): print grade and scheme, "IRCA Lead Auditor, Quality Management Systems scheme". Whether Associate counts as certified is not stated: write "IRCA Associate Auditor". Renewal period: not found.
- Exemplar Global (formerly RABQSA): the QMS (ISO 9001) auditor steps include an auditor training certificate and a Work Style Assessment (S471). Graduates of its certified courses "may be eligible for Exemplar Global's personnel certification" (S472). Management-system schemes (QMS, EMS, OHS): certification is "continuous" while fees, code of conduct and required assessments are kept up, yet "will show an annual expiration date"; if not maintained it is "made inactive" and the details leave the Search Register (S472).

## ASCM (APICS): CPIM, CSCP
- Names: "Certified in Planning and Inventory Management (CPIM)", "Certified Supply Chain Professional (CSCP)" (S473).
- Maintenance: "75 professional development maintenance points every five years"; unmet, the certification is suspended, then expires after 90 days; "you no longer hold the designation and cannot claim recognition for the certification on your resume, LinkedIn profile or anywhere else" (S474).
