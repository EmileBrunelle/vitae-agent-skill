# Certifications: safety professional designations (BCSP, NEBOSH, BCRSP)

Loaded on demand when the CV lists a CSP or ASP (BCSP, US), a NEBOSH certificate or diploma (UK) or a CRSP (BCRSP, Canada). Read together with [`rules.md`](rules.md) (exam passed is not certified; validity and renewal).

## Scope
Paper cards (OSHA Outreach, first aid, forklift): [`../regional/north-america/workplace-safety.md`](../regional/north-america/workplace-safety.md). Each fact is what the body's own page says (consulted 2026-11-08) and holds for that body only. Not read: IOSH's own site, NEBOSH guidance on CV wording, any BCSP display rules (none found).

## BCSP: CSP and ASP (US)
- BCSP's "Associate Safety Professional (ASP)" is not the Québec "ASP Construction" site-safety card: that one is in [`../regional/north-america/quebec/trades-safety.md`](../regional/north-america/quebec/trades-safety.md).
- BCSP calls them certifications and the holders "certificants": "Candidates who meet requirements and pass the examination are awarded their certification and become BCSP certificants" (BCSP, « Certified Safety Professional (CSP) », consulted 2026-11-08, S574). No page read calls either one a licence; never write "licensed".
- CSP prerequisites: "At minimum, a bachelor’s degree" and "Four (4) years of safety, health, and environment (SH&E) experience, at least 50% of which is/was preventative, professional level work with breadth and depth of safety duties" (BCSP, « Certified Safety Professional (CSP) », consulted 2026-11-08, S574). ASP: "At minimum, a bachelor’s degree in any field or an associate in safety, health, or the environment" and "Passing the ASP examination" (BCSP, « Associate Safety Professional (ASP) », consulted 2026-11-08, S575). Exam alone is not the CSP: print the credential only if awarded.
- Renewal: "Recertification cycles normally run five (5) years, starting July 1 and ending June 30 of the fifth year" (BCSP, « Recertification », consulted 2026-11-08, S576). Holders "share their activities to earn recertification points required every five (5) years"; "an annual fee is assessed to credential holders in order to retain use of BCSP credentials" (BCSP, « Certified Safety Professional (CSP) », consulted 2026-11-08, S574). Ask the candidate for the current cycle dates.
- Lapsed: the annual-fee sentence above is the only rule on retaining use; BCSP's policy "prohibits unauthorized use of designations, certifications, marks" (BCSP, « Unauthorized Use of Credentials and Other BCSP Protected Materials Policy », 11/2022, consulted 2026-11-08, S577). Print a lapsed CSP as earned with dates, not as current (`rules.md`).
- BCSP's own wording when it confirms a holder: "(Name of person) is currently certified as a (Certification Title) and in good standing with the Board of Certified Safety Professionals" (BCSP, « Unauthorized Use of Credentials and Other BCSP Protected Materials Policy », 11/2022, consulted 2026-11-08, S577). That is BCSP's reply to a verifier, not CV wording; (LOW, unsourced): do not print "good standing" unless the candidate has BCSP's confirmation.
- Print (LOW, unsourced): "Certified Safety Professional (CSP), BCSP, since 2019" / "Associate Safety Professional (ASP), BCSP". Avoid (LOW, unsourced): "licensed", "CSP (pending)", "CSP-eligible"; ASP is not an associate degree.

## NEBOSH (UK)
- NEBOSH's pages say "qualification". "Ofqual regulates our two NEBOSH Occupational Health and Safety Diplomas taken internationally and in the UK", and "Certificates for regulated qualifications carry the Ofqual logo" (NEBOSH, « Our Recognition », consulted 2026-11-08, S580). The NGC page says it "has been accredited and credit rated by the Qualifications Scotland Accreditation" (NEBOSH, « National General Certificate in Occupational Health and Safety », consulted 2026-11-08, S578). (LOW, unsourced: inference from S580, S578): do not write "Ofqual regulated" for an NGC or IGC.
- National General Certificate (NGC): "has been accredited and credit rated by the Qualifications Scotland Accreditation"; level: "SCQF Level 6 is comparable to RQF/CQFW Level 3 in England, Wales and Northern Ireland" (NEBOSH, « National General Certificate in Occupational Health and Safety », consulted 2026-11-08, S578). The International General Certificate (IGC) page has the same level sentence (NEBOSH, « International General Certificate in Occupational Health and Safety », consulted 2026-11-08, S579). State a level only as NEBOSH words it.
- Where it was taken: the regulator "does not currently regulate outside of the UK and therefore does not permit use of its logo on parchments" (NEBOSH, « Recognition of NEBOSH qualifications », 2022-08-16, consulted 2026-11-08, S583); "Assessments must be taken in the UK to be eligible for IOSH membership, unless the learner was registered with NEBOSH before 31 August 2022" (NEBOSH, « National General Certificate in Occupational Health and Safety », consulted 2026-11-08, S578; same sentence in NEBOSH, « International General Certificate in Occupational Health and Safety », consulted 2026-11-08, S579). IOSH grades are IOSH's, not a NEBOSH result; not checked.
- Diplomas: holders are "entitled to use the designatory letters IDipNEBOSH" (International Diploma, "set at SCQF Level 10") (NEBOSH, « International Diploma in Occupational Health and Safety », consulted 2026-11-08, S581) or "DipNEBOSH" (National Diploma) (NEBOSH, « National Diploma in Occupational Health and Safety », consulted 2026-11-08, S582). No designatory letters are stated for the General Certificates: print none.
- "The National Diploma in Occupational Health and Safety has been withdrawn" (NEBOSH, « National Diploma in Occupational Health and Safety », consulted 2026-11-08, S582): on an older CV print it with its year.
- Validity: no page read states an expiry. Print issuer, exact title and year awarded; never "valid until".
- Print (LOW, unsourced): "NEBOSH National General Certificate in Occupational Health and Safety, 2024". Avoid (LOW, unsourced): "NEBOSH certified", "licensed", "NEBOSH Level 3".

## BCRSP: CRSP (Canada)
- BCRSP says "certifications", "certificate", "credential" (BCRSP, « Use of Certification Marks », consulted 2026-11-08, S584; BCRSP, « Certificate Usage Policy », consulted 2026-11-08, S585); no page read calls it a licence: never write "licensed". « Professionnel en sécurité agréé du Canada (PSAC) » is BCRSP's own French form (S584).
- "BCRSP certifications are awarded to individuals, not to companies, and must only be used with individual names" (BCRSP, « Use of Certification Marks », consulted 2026-11-08, S584): never a firm described as CRSP-certified.
- Form: "The credential may be displayed either with or without periods between the letters" (BCRSP, « Use of Certification Marks », consulted 2026-11-08, S584).
- Validity: use beyond the authorised period without renewal "constitutes unauthorized use of the credential" (BCRSP, « Use of Certification Marks », consulted 2026-11-08, S584). Proof is a certificate plus an ID card "issued annually upon receipt of the renewal fee"; "for verification purposes both a certificate and ID card are necessary" (BCRSP, « Certificate Usage Policy », consulted 2026-11-08, S585). The ID card shows the "expiry date of certification" (BCRSP, « Certificate Usage Policy », consulted 2026-11-08, S585): ask for it.
- Print (LOW, unsourced): "Canadian Registered Safety Professional (CRSP), BCRSP, current (ID card 2026)"; FR « Professionnel en sécurité agréé du Canada (PSAC), BCRSP ».
