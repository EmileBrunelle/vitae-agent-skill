# Certifications: teaching English (CELTA, DELTA, TESL Canada, "TEFL")

Loaded with [`rules.md`](rules.md) when the candidate lists an English-language-teaching qualification. These do not depend on where you work; a state or provincial K-12 licence does: [`../regional/north-america/canada/teaching.md`](../regional/north-america/canada/teaching.md), [`../regional/north-america/us/teaching.md`](../regional/north-america/us/teaching.md), [`../regional/north-america/quebec/teaching.md`](../regional/north-america/quebec/teaching.md). Tests of the candidate's own English (IELTS, Cambridge exams): [`languages.md`](languages.md). Rule 1 of SKILL.md applies.

## CELTA (Cambridge English)
- Exact name: "CELTA (Certificate in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages)", "regulated by Ofqual (the regulator of qualifications, examinations and assessments in England) at level 5 on the Regulated Qualifications Framework" (S432). Courses are "delivered by authorised centres on behalf of Cambridge English" (S433).
- Cambridge says every CELTA course has at least 120 contact hours, 6 hours' assessed teaching practice and 6 hours' directed observation (S433); the CV needs none of it. Face-to-face, online and combined courses give "the same qualification" (S433): the mode is not a different credential.
- Grades: Pass, Pass B, Pass A (S432). Print the grade only if the certificate shows it.
- Print the level only as the Ofqual level above (an England framework); no source gives a degree equivalence: write none.
- Validity: no expiry or renewal period appears in the pages read; write the year earned (rules.md, non-expiring credentials); add no validity.
- Course "in progress": training, not CELTA (rules.md, "Exam passed is not certified").
- Verification: Cambridge's pages on lost certificates and accepting exams describe a free Results Verification Service for an "examination result" (verification number from the Statement of Result and Certificate, plus date of birth) and a "certifying statement" for a lost certificate (S434). Neither names CELTA or DELTA: do not say a CELTA can be checked there.

## DELTA (Cambridge English)
- Exact name: "DELTA (Diploma in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages)", three modules: Module 1 "Understanding Language, Methodology and Resources for Teaching", Module 2 "Developing Professional Practice", Module 3 "Extending Practice and ELT Specialism" or "English Language Teaching Management" (S435).
- "You will receive a separate certificate for each module you complete and you will also receive the full DELTA qualification when you complete all three modules" (S435). So a candidate with one or two modules writes "DELTA Module 1 (Pass with Distinction)", never "DELTA".
- Grades: Pass with Distinction, Pass with Merit, Pass (S435). Never print "DELTA (expected)" (rules.md, future awards); "There is no time limit to complete all three modules" (S435).

## TESL Canada Professional Certificate
- Exact title: TESL Canada Professional Certificate: Standard One (or Two, or Three); print the standard (S436). Standards One and Two: a bachelor's degree plus a TESL Canada recognised training programme (at least 100 / 250 hours of theory and methodology, supervised practicum of at least 20 / 50 hours); Three: a Master's/Doctoral degree in TESL or equivalent from a Canadian Universities Canada member institution, with adult-education background and a 50-hour supervised practicum, or Standard One or Two plus a Master's in a related field (S436). The Manual says the standards « were developed for instructors trained in Canada ».
- Scope: "intended for ESL/EFL instructors teaching in adult learning environments"; "Provincial Teachers' Colleges and provincial regulatory bodies govern the certification of K-12 teachers" (S423). Not a school-teaching licence: it "does not replace provincial professional certificates where they exist" (S436).
- Validity: "To keep your TESL Canada Certificate valid you must be a member in good standing of TESL Canada" (S437). Membership ("signing up and paying a fee to belong") and certification ("recognizes professional adult ESL teaching qualifications") differ (S423). A lapsed membership therefore means a certificate not current (a reading of that sentence).
- A CELTA from an institution not recognised by TESL Canada may apply, with a fee, for the certificate (S423, S436): a CELTA is not a TESL Canada certificate by itself; list them separately.
- Verification: no public lookup was found in the pages read.

## "TEFL" and "TESOL"
- No awarding body in the sources read. Cambridge uses the phrase descriptively ("a qualification for teaching English as a foreign language"; "other TEFL/TESOL qualifications") (S433). No source loaded defines "TEFL certificate" as a single credential.
- Rule: never print "TEFL certified" as a designation. Print the provider, the exact course title and, from the certificate, the hours and any observed practicum (suggested wording: "120-hour TEFL course, [provider], with observed teaching practice"). Practicum hours matter to TESL Canada (S423).
- Course taken without assessment: training, not a certification (bootcamp rule in [`../education-cases.md`](../education-cases.md#intensive-non-degree-training-bootcamps)).
