# Certifications: health professions (credentials that do not depend on where you work)

Loaded with [`rules.md`](rules.md) when the candidate lists a resuscitation, specialty-nursing, EMS or pharmacy-technician certification (HSFC, AHA, CNA, AACN CCRN, NREMT, PTCB). ## Decision rule
Three different things; write only the one the candidate's record shows: exam passed (a step), licence held (the order's permit or the state's licence), certification (a separate credential from a certifying body, not a substitute for the licence). Where a title is reserved (for example OIIQ and OPQ in Québec), it goes with registration at the order, never with an exam or a certification. If the status is unclear, ask for the register's label; print the permit or registration number only if the candidate supplies it (no source loaded requires or bars it; the OIIQ, CMQ and CNO registers accept it as a search criterion).

## Certifications
- Heart & Stroke: BLS « is valid for one year »; ACLS « valid for two years » (HSFC, « Learn CPR », S344). Print provider, level, expiry; an expired card is not current (rules.md). The AHA recognises the HSFC provider card for admission to an AHA provider course (renewal or update) in the same discipline (AHA below).
- AHA BLS, ACLS and PALS cards are « valid for two years » (AHA course pages, Wayback captures 2025-12-18 to 2026-01-01, S350). The card is valid « through the end of the month » of issue, the expiry shows as month and year, and an eCard is verified at the AHA's « My Cards » page with its code (AHA, « Course Card Information », capture 2026-01-02, S351). The HSFC (Canada) card can be used « for admission to an AHA provider course (renewal or update) or Instructor course in the same discipline » (S351); that page does not say the HSFC card is accepted as proof for US employment.
- CNA specialty: « Once certified, you can proudly use the credentials associated with your specialty after your name »; the forms carry « (C) », e.g. « CNCC(C) », listed as registered trademarks of the CNA (CNA, « Certification Exam-based Nursing Practice Specialties », S345). Five-year term; renewal needs the holder to « continue to be a practising nurse in good standing » (CNA, « Renewing Your Certification », S346). « misrepresentation of your CNA certification status » is a ground for denial, suspension or revocation (CNA, « Denial, Suspension or Revocation of Your Credential », S347). Print the letters only once certified, with the licence on its own line.
- AACN CCRN (Adult): initial eligibility needs « A current, unencumbered U.S. RN or APRN license »; renewal runs on a « three-year certification renewal period » (AACN, CCRN pages, S352); free online verification, usable « as primary source verification » (AACN, « Verify Certification », S353). Print the RN licence and CCRN as separate lines.
- NREMT: the page defines certification generally as a « Voluntary process » by a private organization, calls the National Registry a private, non-profit certifying organization and the State EMS Offices or like agencies « the state licensing agencies »; National Registry certification « by itself, does not give an individual the right to practice », although some states use it as a basis for their own authorisation (NREMT, « Certification vs. Licensure », S354). Print « NREMT-certified » with the level from the certificate, and the state licence separately; « many states call their licensure processes “certification,” », so use the state's own label. State licensing: [`us/health.md`](../regional/north-america/us/health.md).
- PTCB CPhT: renew « every 2 years »; status verified through PTCB Certification Verification; the Credly badge may go on « electronic files of a resume »; « the legal scope of practice for CPhTs is defined by individual State Boards of Pharmacy » (PTCB, « Become a Certified Pharmacy Technician (CPhT) », S355). A certification, not a pharmacist title.
