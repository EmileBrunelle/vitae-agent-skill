# Certifications: accounting and finance

Loaded with [`rules.md`](rules.md) when the candidate lists a CPA, CFA, ACCA or other accounting or finance designation. Not read: IMA CMA, CPA Canada and provincial bodies outside Québec, US state boards beyond the NASBA statement below.

## CPA
- Québec: a reserved title, not a voluntary mark (regime: [`../regional/north-america/us-canada.md`](../regional/north-america/us-canada.md#protected-titles), [`../regional/north-america/quebec/market.md`](../regional/north-america/quebec/market.md)). « Il est obligatoire d’être membre de l’Ordre pour avoir le droit de porter le titre de comptable professionnel agréé (CPA) » (Ordre des CPA du Québec, « Les différents titres des CPA », consulted 2026-11-07, S309).
- Forms the Order prescribes: « Prénom Nom, CPA », « Prénom Nom, CPA auditeur » for public-accounting licence holders, « FCPA » for Fellows; since 2022-05-16 members use "le titre unique de CPA, sans référence à leur titre d’origine" (S310). A Québec member writes "CPA" alone, with no earlier title beside it.
- CPA registered in another province: « Pour utiliser le titre de CPA au Québec, vous devez y être inscrit à titre de membre »; CPA Canada membership does not authorise the title. The Order deems a person to use the title in Québec when working there (on site or remotely), or showing a Québec postal address on a business card, e-mail signature, website or social profile; « Pierre Tremblay, CPA (Nouveau-Brunswick) » is not allowed. Occasional visits to present or attend meetings do not by themselves require registration (S311). For a Québec target with a non-Québec CPA: ask about registration; don't print "CPA" until confirmed.
- US: "passed all four sections of the CPA Exam – does that mean you’re a CPA? Not so fast! To practice as a CPA, you must first become licensed by the jurisdiction in which you wish to practice" (NASBA, « Done with the Exam, Now What? », 2023-05-26, S295). Print "Passed the Uniform CPA Examination"; print the licence only with state and status as the candidate holds it. Licence is per jurisdiction, with experience required everywhere and 150 education hours (S295). For a lapsed licence, ask the candidate for the board's status label.

## CFA Institute
- All below: CFA Institute, « How to Share Your Achievements », consulted 2026-11-07, S294.
- Charterholder: use the CFA Marks "only if you are a CFA Institute Charterholder Member in good standing ... To remain in good standing, a Charterholder Member must annually complete the Professional Conduct Statement and pay membership dues." CV form: "Jane Doe, CFA" (no ® after the name); in the entry "CFA® charterholder, CFA Institute". "Don’t use CFA as a job title or in a plural/possessive form."
- Candidate: "CFA Program candidate" only "if you are registered for an upcoming CFA Program exam (or awaiting results)"; otherwise "Passed Level I of the CFA Program". A candidate does not put CFA after the name; "Jane Doe, CFA (expected 2026)" is not permitted. All three levels passed, no charter: "Passed all three levels of the CFA Program; charter award is contingent on meeting applicable work experience and membership requirements".
- Charterholder LinkedIn guidance asks for "the appropriate expiry date"; the page does not define it for a CV, so print one only if the candidate's record shows it.

## ACCA
- Member: "the professional designation Chartered Certified Accountant or Certified Accountant and/or the designatory letters ACCA"; Fellow: FCCA after five continuous years of membership, "providing the member has not breached the continuing professional development requirements" (ACCA, Membership Regulations 2014, amended 1 January 2019, S312; no current version could be loaded, so check it before relying on anything beyond these two designations).
- Affiliate: "You may refer to yourself as having ACCA affiliate status but may not use the designatory letters after your name or refer to yourself as an ACCA member in any context" (ACCA, « Students’ frequently asked questions », consulted 2026-11-07, S313). A student or affiliate writes "ACCA affiliate" or "ACCA student, papers passed", never "ACCA".
