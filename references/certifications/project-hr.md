# Certifications: project management and human resources

Loaded with [`rules.md`](rules.md) when the candidate lists a PMP (or other PMI credential) or a Québec HR designation (CRHA, CRIA, CHRP). Not read: PMI's verification registry, CIPD, SHRM, HRCI, other provinces' HR bodies.

## PMI (PMP)
- Granted at the pass notification: "Once you have been notified directly by PMI that you have officially passed an exam, you are granted that certification" (PMI, « Certification Handbook », revised June 2026, S297). Unlike ISC2 or ISACA, no separate endorsement follows: the application, with its education and experience review, is approved before the exam. A candidate with an approved application and no pass holds nothing.
- Use: "You may refer to yourself as a holder of that certification as long as you have an active certification status. You are authorized to use the certification designation in block letters after your name on business cards, personal letterhead, résumés, websites, and in your email signature" (S297). Form: "Joseph Smith, PMP"; the ® and an attribution statement are not required in that position (PMI, « Trademark Usage Guidelines », S298).
- Not "PMPs": PMI recommends "PMP® certification holders" (S298). Logo and digital badge: "Only PMP credential holders in good standing" (S298).
- Lapse: PMI certifications are renewed "every 3 years" through the CCR program (S297); "Any individual whose PMI certification has lapsed should immediately discontinue use of the credential" (S298). A lapsed PMP: dated entry only, per rules.md.
- Other PMI credentials (CAPM and so on): name the full credential, not "PMP"; the handbook applies the same rule to "that certification".

## Ordre des CRHA (Québec)
- Reserved title: « le Code des professions réserve le titre de “conseillère en ressources humaines agréé” et de “conseiller en relations industrielles agréé” aux professionnels inscrits au tableau de l’Ordre des CRHA ». A person not on the roll may not use « les initiales “CRHA”, “CRIA” ou “CHRP” » or any title or abbreviation that could suggest registration (Ordre des CRHA, « Signalez l’usurpation du titre », consulted 2026-11-07, S314). Regime: [`../regional/north-america/quebec/market.md`](../regional/north-america/quebec/market.md#protected-titles), generic regulated-occupation rule [`../regional/north-america/common.md`](../regional/north-america/common.md#protected-titles).
- Writing rule: "CRHA", "CRIA" and "CHRP" are for roll members only. The Order's page does not address candidates registered elsewhere or lapsed members: check the roll with the candidate and report the gap; until confirmed, write the body and the status, e.g. "Human resources certificate, `<body>`".
