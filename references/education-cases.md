# Education cases

Loaded on demand at step 4 when the candidate's education includes a degree in progress, studies stopped without the diploma, a transfer or program change, credit recognition, an exchange or study abroad, a double or joint degree, work-integrated study (apprenticeship, alternance, duales Studium, co-op), a degree completed but not yet conferred, a return to studies with work alongside, an MBA or professional master's, credits or grades from another system, or non-degree intensive training (bootcamp). Never read otherwise. When the target market is Québec, Québec wordings and credentials (DEC-BAC, AEC) are in [`regional/north-america/quebec/education.md`](regional/north-america/quebec/education.md#education-cases-québec), section Education cases.

Universal rule: the CV never implies a diploma the candidate does not hold. Write what was earned, where, and when; let durations and credit counts carry the weight. A diploma name is the institution's exact name (from the transcript or the program page), never a paraphrase or an upgrade.

## Degree in progress
- Give the expected date in place of the end date, month + year. English: "expected grad date (e.g. June 20xx)" (Penn Career Services, « Write a Resume/CV », consulted 2026-11).
- Put it first in the section.
- ATS handling of "expected" dates: no vendor documentation found, so don't claim a parsing behaviour.

## Studies stopped without the diploma
- Label it as studies, not as the degree, and add the credits earned in parentheses if relevant. Dates: a range, or the year studies stopped.
- It may be omitted if it has no link with the target job (ask the candidate), but never stretch another entry's dates to cover the gap.
- Don't add a reason for stopping unless the candidate wants one.

## Transfer between institutions or programs
- The degree-granting institution is the education entry. A previous institution is listed as studies (never as a diploma) with its own date range when relevant to the target job. Penn: transfer schools "formatted consistently with school name, location, and dates" (« Write a Resume/CV », consulted 2026-11).
- Credit recognition goes in parentheses on the previous institution's line, e.g. "Software Engineering (B.Eng. program, not completed) — University A · 2018–2020 · 60 ECTS recognized toward the B.Sc. at University B".
- Program change (same institution or not): one entry per diploma obtained, naming the program completed; an abandoned program follows the studies-stopped rule (a sub-line when relevant). If both programs were completed, list both diplomas normally.
- Never merge two institutions into one entry or one date range.

## Intensive non-degree training (bootcamps)
- Write an official credential exactly as issued. A private bootcamp without an official credential is training, never a diploma: program name, provider, dates, real duration (weeks, hours).
- Section (Education vs Additional training): no authoritative source settles it, so ask the candidate and state the choice. Projects built during the program go under Projects.

## Exchange / study abroad
- Education: a line or sub-line under the home degree (host institution, place, term, focus). A work placement or internship abroad goes in Experience (Kent, « Returning from a Year Abroad: Employability Workshop », consulted 2026-11: "you may wish to list this under 'Employment'"). Never list the same stay in two sections (Münster WiWi CDC, « CDC Career Info – Lebenslauf », 2019, which also puts a study stay abroad inside the Studium entry).
- Structure varies. UK university examples: Warwick Careers, « CV example template », a separate line after the degree; Kent (same title as above), under Education, or under Experience. DACH may use its own heading, "Auslandsaufenthalte" (Köln WiSo Career, « Kurzinfo Lebenslauf », 2018). University of Sydney Careers, « How to write a resume », consulted 2026-11, lists international exchanges under education.

## Double, dual or joint degree
- One entry per credential actually issued: two diplomas = two lines, may be grouped under the program name (Boston University, « Sample Resume – Addendum for Dual Degree Candidates », 2015).
- A joint degree = one entry naming every institution (LOW confidence, convention, unsourced).

## Work-integrated study (apprenticeship degree, alternance, duales Studium, co-op)
- The academic award goes in Education under the institution's exact title; the paid employment goes in Experience as a job (employer, role, dates), labelled as the contract says (apprentice / alternant / dual student / co-op). Cross-reference, don't merge, and don't count the period as two separate achievements. LOW confidence (convention). Europass: "make a separate entry for each course completed", "highlight work placements during training" (Cedefop, « Instructions for using the Europass curriculum vitae », consulted 2026-11).

## Completed but not yet conferred
- Date it with the completion date, labelled as the transcript or letter supports: "expected `<month year>`" or "completed `<month year>`, awarded `<month year>`". LOW confidence (no source).

## Credits
- Always with unit and system: "60 ECTS", "30 crédits (Québec)", "US credit hours", "CATS". Never convert silently; for credits only, a conversion may go in parentheses, marked approx. Fact: "1 full academic year = 60 credits" in ECTS (Europass, « Europass Mobility Instructions », 2021-07). The labelling rule is convention.

## Grades
- Write every grade or GPA against its scale ("3,8/4,0", "15/20"); never convert, not even in parentheses (Münster: « Abschlussnote: 3.8 von 4.0 oder 3.8/4.0 »). Québec scales: [`regional/north-america/quebec/education.md`](regional/north-america/quebec/education.md#gpa-moyenne-cumulative).
- Degree in progress, UK style: "predicted 2:1" or "grade to date", only if the transcript or a tutor supports it (Warwick template: "if still studying state predicted grade OR grade achieved to date"; University of Leeds, « Guidelines for writing CVs and covering letters »: "Include grades, actual and/or expected", consulted 2026-11).

## Return to studies with work alongside
- Career interruption, full-time studies, part-time job: three separate entries with real dates (the earlier full-time job, the program in Education, the part-time job in Experience). Overlapping dates are normal; order each section by end date; the study period is not a gap to explain.
- Label the part-time job as part-time with hours per week (LOW confidence, convention). US federal résumés require hours per week per job (OPM, « Agency Guidance on the Two-Page Limit on Resume Length », 2025).

## MBA and other professional master's
- Degree line as issued ("Executive MBA", "Part-time" if printed), school, date (expected if current). Within Education, current degree first (Smith, « The Smith MBA Resume », 2017).
- Education before Experience while the MBA is current: LOW, convention. Alumni with long experience usually list experience first (Kellogg, « Resumes and cover letters »).
- Length: one page for a school résumé book (Wharton, « 2018 Resume Tips and Resume Book Process »; Smith: under eight years of experience); elsewhere the market file's rule (Booth: one page preferred, two understood, 2025).
- GMAT/GRE, GPA: include when the target asks (consulting, finance recruiting) or the score is strong for that market, with its scale (Smith, US 4.0: GPA above 3.5, GMAT above 700; Wharton: GMAT >720, GPA >3.7).
- Executive/part-time MBA beside a job: two concurrent entries, real dates, label kept (LOW, convention; same logic as "Return to studies").
- Accreditation line (AACSB, EQUIS, AMBA): only if the accreditor's own list shows it, and name what it covers, because the bodies are not equivalent. AACSB: "By default, AACSB Accreditation is granted to the entire institution", with possible exclusion of programs or a single-unit scope (AACSB, « Accreditation FAQs », consulted 2026-11-07, S167). EQUIS: institutional; programme accreditation is a separate EFMD product (EFMD Global, « EQUIS Accreditation », consulted 2026-11-07, S168). AMBA: "we evaluate and accredit programmes directly" (AMBA, « AMBA Accreditation », consulted 2026-11-07, S169). So "AACSB-accredited school" and "AMBA-accredited MBA" are different claims; write the one that is true, and check the MBA is not excluded. No loaded text covers ACBSP or IACBE.
- France: RNCP level as in [`regional/europe/france.md`](regional/europe/france.md); the France compétences record is the check (« Certification professionnelle », consulted 2026-11-07, S170). Write « visé » or « grade de master » only if the CEFDG archive shows the formation: "Seules sont mentionnées les formations évaluées qui ont conduit à un avis favorable" (CEFDG, « Accueil », consulted 2026-11-07, S171). The CEFDG was abolished by décret n°2026-551 of 26 June 2026; its archive covers opinions up to 2025-2026, later ones belong to Hcéres (no Hcéres page loaded, verify there). No loaded official text says whether a given MBA holds the grade.
- Germany: check the programme in ELIAS, the Akkreditierungsrat's database of programme accreditation results, valid accreditations by default (Stiftung Akkreditierungsrat, « ELIAS-Database », consulted 2026-11-07, S172). FIBAA "awards its own quality seals for programme accreditation" and "is recognized as a quality assurance agency in Germany" (FIBAA, home page, consulted 2026-11-07, S173); still verify in ELIAS.
- Unaccredited or unverifiable MBA: degree line as issued, no accreditation line, no "internationally recognized" wording; add an accreditation only after finding it on the accreditor's list (LOW, convention).
