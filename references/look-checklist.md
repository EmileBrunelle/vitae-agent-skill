# Look checklist

Judge the page image(s) only, no source. One yes/no check per item. Answer with exactly one line per item, in order, nothing else:

`PASS|FAIL <item id> — <zone on the page as evidence>`

Zone = where you looked (e.g. "header", "Experience, 2nd entry", "bottom third", "page 2 top"). A missing item counts as FAIL. When unsure, FAIL.

Items (PASS = the statement is true):

- L1 No text is cut off, overlapped or hidden behind a coloured band or another element.
- L2 Every section heading has clear space above it, bigger than the space between lines inside the section above.
- L3 Each sub-line (role, company, dates, descriptor) sits closer to its own heading than to the next block.
- L4 No skills row, contact line, descriptor line, Languages line or specialities line wraps onto a second line.
- L5 No date sits apart from the others: all dates line up in the same place or style.
- L6 No heading is alone at the bottom of a page, no single line is stranded at the top or bottom of a page, and each page after the first opens on a section heading.
- L7 No ugly break: no word split by a bad line end, no line ending on a one-letter word or a lone short word, no mixed straight and curly apostrophes.
- L8 Body text looks comfortably readable at printed size (not tiny, not cramped).
- L9 The one-glance hierarchy is clear: name first, then section headings, then body.
- L10 Spacing is even: same gaps between entries, no block much denser or emptier than the rest.
- L11 The accent colour is crisp on white and would still be readable in grayscale.
- L12 No coloured edge, tint or box cuts through an icon or a chip (check zoomed crops when given).
- L13 No page looks half empty or overfull; the bottom margin looks the same on every page.
- L14 The design looks specific: a reader could not name a company or a popular word-processor template from it.
