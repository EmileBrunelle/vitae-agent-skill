# Academic mode

**Academic mode.** If the document is an academic CV (a university faculty application, a promotion or tenure/qualification dossier, an academic nomination), load `references/field-academic.md` and follow **its** workflow instead of step 4 and rule 5 of SKILL.md. Academic mode **replaces rule 5 and step 4, it does not relax them** — it has its own measured invariants, checked by `scripts/verify_academic.py`. The replacement invariant in one line: **the atom is the entry, not the section** — "no entry straddles a page break" stands in for "no section is ever split." There is no fill target at all in academic mode, not even a last-page floor — dropped, not loosened. There is no family draw and no palette generation either: one neutral gabarit, from `templates/academic/`. Everything else in SKILL.md (rules 0-4, steps 0-3 and 5-8) still applies.

At step 6, academic CVs are checked with `scripts/verify_academic.py` (run it, never open it) instead of the family-diff and fill checks of `6-verify.md`.

The gate reads no word of any language. An entry that starts with a status word instead of a year ("In press", "Sous presse") is recognised only if the data file declares the word in `[meta] entry_status`, as a case-insensitive whole word (or words; list every inflection you print: Soumis, Soumise...); with none declared only a year starts an entry, and a section with `undated_prefix` above 0 FAILs. The `[counts]` check looks for each declared section's heading as the template prints it (English). A CV that prints other headings declares them in an optional `[headings]` table, with the same keys and nesting as `[counts]` (`education = "Formation"`, `[headings.publications]` `journal = "Articles de revues"`), matched as a literal, case-insensitive whole line; `affiliations` may also be declared for the extra "Affiliations and languages" heading; any other unknown key FAILs naming it. A counted section whose heading is not found is a plain FAIL naming that heading and saying to declare it under `[headings]`; no language is guessed or reported.
