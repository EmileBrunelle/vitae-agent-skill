# Step 6 — Verify mechanically

6. **Verify mechanically** (loop until all pass; re-run after EVERY edit). Run the MECHANICAL subset in one command — `scripts/verify.sh cv.typ EXPECTED_PAGES [FILL_MIN FILL_MAX] --facts facts.md` (POSIX wrapper, two lines, execs into verify.py) on Linux/macOS/WSL/Git Bash, or `python scripts/verify.py cv.typ EXPECTED_PAGES [FILL_MIN FILL_MAX] --facts facts.md` directly on bare Windows — it exits non-zero if any check fails; `verify.sh --all DIR` checks every CV in DIR (page count from a `// vitae: pages=N` header, else from `_2p`/`-2page` in the name) and gates every .typ, printing `SKIP` only for files whose name contains guide, lettre, letter, cover, lisez-moi or readme (case-insensitive, whole word) and that carry no `// vitae:` header (a header always opts in). Never skip it after an edit. **Paste the gate's full output verbatim into the session before delivering** — this is the anti-circumvention check: a summary ("gate passed") is not proof, and a result that was never run cannot be pasted. Either way, the items further down this list (contrast recompute, date order, the fact-sheet diff; the visual look is gated by the `Visual pass:` report line below) are never covered by the script and stay manual:
   - The gate also WARNs (never fails) on year-only date ranges (if the facts file has no month for it, KEEP it year-only and report the gap, NEVER invent or estimate months), and on a degree token beside a non-completion marker ("B.Eng. program").
   - `--facts facts.md` compares the CV text to the facts file and WARNs `not in facts file: "<token>"` for every year, month+year, number or capitalized name (employer, school) the facts do not contain. Every such WARN must be removed from the CV or confirmed with the candidate (then added to the facts file) — never silenced by rewording. When the facts file and the CV are in different languages (detected from stopwords, `INFO  facts file is French, CV is English ...`), only dates, numbers and acronyms are checked: names and titles are not cross-checked, and the step 7 hiring-manager fact diff covers them.
   - When `pick_design.py` drew the CV, `cv.typ` line 1 carries `// vitae: pages=N fill=A-B` and verify.py uses that target when you give no FILL args. Passing a FILL_MIN below A is not a FAIL but prints a loud `WARN  fill target lowered below drawn target` and repeats it on the `PASS (fill target lowered: A-B → X-Y)` line: allowed ONLY if the facts are exhausted — tell the user the page is under-filled and which facts would fill it; never pad or invent to reach the target (rule 1).
   - `WARN  entry "X": the bullet "..." restates the descriptor "..."` (experience and projects) means a bullet repeats its own entry's descriptor line (the note argument of `#job`, or the description in a projects entry's second argument; the employer name is never compared, and a descriptor must have at least 2 content words) — padding by repetition (rule 1: each fact once per entry). Give the bullet a new fact (scope, outcome, figure) or cut it; if no fact is left, leave the page under-filled and say so. It is a WARN, never a FAIL.
   - The gate also FAILs text a filled region hides or cuts (`check_text_clip`: a header band shorter than its own text, white text left on white paper past the band's end). Prefer the `masthead()` function of lib.typ (call it, don't open the file) for new or edited header bands (it grows with its content); a fixed-height band must still pass `check_text_clip` at the tuned leading.
   - If the fill needs re-measuring or tuning (after recopying `lib.typ`, or `measure_fill.py` off target), read `references/steps/6-tune.md` now.
   - `typst compile cv.typ` then `pdfinfo cv.pdf | grep Pages` — exact count. (`icons.typ` must sit beside `lib.typ` first — step 4; the empty fallback compiles, it just draws no marks.)
   - `python3 scripts/verify.py --text cv.pdf` — reading order sane, no orphaned dates, skill lines intact, and dates within each section in reverse-chronological order. Full checklist and known extraction traps: `references/ats.md`. Portal-specific quirks: Eightfold in `references/ats.md`, Workday in `references/workday.md` (parse-only copy: `typst compile --input workday=1 …`).
   - **No section split across a page break** (rule 5's first half) — checked by the gate since 2026-09-13, not by eye. Reduced to its contrapositive, which is what a PDF can actually answer: every page after the first must OPEN on a section heading, so that every break lands exactly on a boundary. The heading list comes from the `#section("...")` call sites in the source, and lib.typ's `runhead` band is skipped by its `n / total` token. `breakable: false` on the section block states the INTENT; this states the outcome. A source with no `#section(...)` call reports NOT CHECKED rather than passing.
   - Recompute the contrast of the accent as actually rendered (not the hex you intended) against its background, with design.md's formula — must still clear 4.5:1.
   - Diff every bullet against the fact sheet again — step 5's check, but the drafting agent self-checking once is not enough; re-verify it here. (The hiring manager's fact diff in step 7's review is the third checkpoint — deliberate, not drift.)
   - Confirm the document actually carries the design family that `pick_design.py` drew (or the family the user imposed explicitly): the family's heading device, its skills-block shape, its accent role, its contact-icon style, its font pair, its **gabarit** (margins, body size and the fill target derived from them), its **section order** and its specialities line if it has one — not just the accent. Re-run the script with the same arguments and diff its output against the `.typ`. None of the three clone markers may survive: UPPERCASE headings over a full-width accent rule, a `Category: items` skills block, accent on role title + rules + links.
   - **Visual pass — mandatory, never silent.** Render with `python3 scripts/verify.py --render <pdf> --out <dir>` (one `<stem>-p<N>.png` per page; `--ppi` defaults to 150), then answer every item of `references/look-checklist.md` (one `PASS|FAIL <id> — <zone>` line each; a missing item is a FAIL; fix every FAIL, re-render, re-run). Pixel statistics (PIL, `measure_fill.py`) are not a substitute, and deleting the PNG unviewed fails this step. Add `--crops` to the same command for the risky bands (solid fills, icons, a painted surface meeting a vector mark): it writes `<stem>-p<N>-<band>.png` at 300 ppi — look at those too (a tint edge slicing through an icon is invisible at page scale and no gate check can see it; design.md § 14, Header). Routes, in order, take the first that works:
     1. **self** — you have image vision (open each PNG so it appears in your context): run the checklist yourself.
     2. **subagent** — a vision-capable subagent gets the PNG paths + `references/look-checklist.md` and nothing else (no source, no verdict from you); it returns the lines.
     3. **another vision model (remote or local)** — any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenRouter, ollama, LM Studio…). Base URL and model come from the user, or from `python3 scripts/look.py --base-url <url>/v1 --list-models`. Render with `--crops` first, then pass ALL page PNGs and the crops of ONE PDF in ONE invocation: `python3 scripts/look.py --base-url <url>/v1 --model <id> <dir>/<stem>-*.png` (render each PDF into its own dedicated `--out` dir; PNGs from several stems are refused with exit 2). For small local models (about 30B or less) add `--per-item` (a fresh call per `pages`/`crops` checklist item and one per page for each `page` item, so calls = page items x pages + the other items; more reliable for small models; `crops:fill`/`crops:icon` items see only that crop type (plain `crops` = all crops) and fall back to the pages when none exists). Calls can take minutes: use a long Bash timeout or run in the background. Exit 0 = all PASS and the last line is the clean `Visual pass: other-model <model>`; exit 1 = some FAIL and the last line is `Visual pass: FAIL other-model <model> (<n> items: L…)`, which is never reportable (fix and re-run); 2 = endpoint or config problem (take the next route). A PASS from a single small-model run is weak evidence; a FAIL is a real signal (measured on Gemma 4 12B: one miss in two runs on a clipped-text page).
     4. **user** — ask the user to look: give them the PNG paths and the checklist, and wait for the answers.
     The final report carries exactly one line `Visual pass: self | subagent <model> | other-model <model> | user`. Absent, or `skipped`, or "no vision" = step 6 FAIL: no vision is a reason to take the next route, never to skip. Beyond the checklist, check by name that nothing else on the page looks wrong (the checklist is the floor, not the ceiling).
   - **Line integrity** (design.md § Line integrity): on the render, no **structured** line wraps onto a second line — every skills/stack row, the education entry's descriptor line, the Languages line, the header/contact lines, the specialities line. Fix by splitting one row into two with fewer items each (preferred — it costs the page nothing), by shortening the label, or by the row's measure. Prose and bullets are step 5's business, not this check's. This one is a **visual** check on the PNG (checklist L5), not a gate check: `verify.py` cannot see a wrap and will pass a file that has one.
   - **Non-recognition, brand clause** (design.md § The final bar): look at the page and ask *could a reader name a company from this?* A recipe that reproduces one brand's signature combination of border, shadow, shape and palette fails the bar exactly as a stock template does.
   - **One date convention per document**: every date on the page sits where the family puts it — the education entry uses the same entry helper as the jobs, and its date is a *wide* run ("2015 – 2019", not a bare "2019", which drops out of the date column in extraction: `references/ats.md`). A date placed differently from the others is a bug, not a variant.
