# Step 2 — Pick region and language

2. **Pick region and language.** Infer a default from the source material — city/country in the header, phone country code, language and spelling variant, posting URLs — state it and confirm; never silently decide. The *target job market* always overrides where the candidate lives (a Montréal candidate applying to London jobs gets UK conventions). Load [`references/regional.md`](../regional.md) (router: file list with load conditions + universal rules), then ONLY the file(s) the router lists for the target market under `references/regional/<region>/` (e.g. [`north-america/us-canada.md`](../regional/north-america/us-canada.md), [`europe/france.md`](../regional/europe/france.md)) — don't load the other markets. Add-ons come on top of the target market's file, each only on its router condition: target NB, NS, PEI, Ontario or Manitoba AND (French CV OR francophone employer), never a Québec target → [`north-america/francophone-canada.md`](../regional/north-america/francophone-canada.md) (Québec is not the French-Canadian case); Québec target → [`quebec/market.md`](../regional/north-america/quebec/market.md) (and [`quebec/education.md`](../regional/north-america/quebec/education.md) when the education section needs it); Québec credentials targeting any other market → [`quebec/outbound-common.md`](../regional/north-america/quebec/outbound-common.md) plus the target's `outbound-north-america.md` or `outbound-france.md`; European credentials or CV habits targeting USA/Canada/Québec → [`europe/to-north-america.md`](../regional/europe/to-north-america.md); Indian credentials targeting USA, Canada, UK, Australia or NZ → [`india/to-anglosphere.md`](../regional/india/to-anglosphere.md); Indian credentials targeting Germany, Ireland, the Netherlands or France → [`india/to-europe/common.md`](../regional/india/to-europe/common.md) plus that country's file; Pakistani, Bangladeshi, Nepali, Philippine or Chinese credentials targeting a market outside their home country → [`asia/outbound.md`](../regional/asia/outbound.md). Apply the language and spelling of the *job market*, not the user's chat language. If the market has no file, do not infer from training data — verify with a current source or ask, and label the assumption in the deliverable.
