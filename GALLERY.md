# Design gallery

Full-page renders of the fourteen reference families — see [README.md](README.md)
for the skill itself. Each render is the actual page 1 output of
`templates/families/<name>/resume.typ`.

## swiss-grid — neutral

International Typographic Style: flush-left grid, accent rule above the
title, no colour on text.

![swiss-grid](assets/families/swiss-grid.png)

## editorial-serif — classic

Contemporary editorial: serif display over a humanist sans body, short
accent bar above the title.

![editorial-serif](assets/families/editorial-serif.png)

## color-band — modern

Reversed-out masthead: the heading is knocked out white on a solid accent
band.

![color-band](assets/families/color-band.png)

## humanist-quiet — neutral

Restraint, no rules at all: one humanist sans, no heading device,
whitespace alone carries the boundary.

![humanist-quiet](assets/families/humanist-quiet.png)

## keyline-corporate — modern

Contemporary corporate report: an accent keyline runs down the left of each
title, with a soft rule across each section boundary.

![keyline-corporate](assets/families/keyline-corporate.png)

## bold-display — modern

Geometric display, heavy contrast: UPPERCASE display title over a heavy
rule.

![bold-display](assets/families/bold-display.png)

## margin-index — classic

Classic marginalia: titles live in a wide left-margin column, serif with
old-style figures.

![margin-index](assets/families/margin-index.png)

## quiet-luxury — neutral

Warm restrained minimalism: warm humanist sans, a short accent tick beside
the title.

![quiet-luxury](assets/families/quiet-luxury.png)

## mono-technical — modern

Technical-documentation aesthetic: filled hue-A/hue-B marker glyphs,
monospace throughout.

![mono-technical](assets/families/mono-technical.png)

## engraved-card — classic

Engraved stationery: a rotated accent lozenge, quiet serif, airy margins.

![engraved-card](assets/families/engraved-card.png)

## clause-index — neutral

Numbered-clause standard document: outdented clause numbers, institutional
grotesque with tabular figures.

![clause-index](assets/families/clause-index.png)

## hard-edge — creative

Neo-brutalist: the title is knocked out of a hard black slab; heavy
grotesque + document mono.

![hard-edge](assets/families/hard-edge.png)

## avant-poster — creative

Constructivist poster: a heavy accent bar bleeds off the page edge,
condensed grotesque display.

![avant-poster](assets/families/avant-poster.png)

## gutter-rail — creative

Themed developer environment: a gutter rail runs down the margin, UI
grotesque + mono chrome.

![gutter-rail](assets/families/gutter-rail.png)

The `creative` register (`hard-edge`, `avant-poster`, `gutter-rail`) is never
drawn by default — see § Design families in `references/design.md`.
